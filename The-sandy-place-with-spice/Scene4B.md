## :sunflower: Scene 4B: Water

You look around for water and come across a body next to a small patch of flowers. He must have died waiting to gather the dew from the flowers because his dew sickle is still in his hands. You pick it up and wave it over the flowers to gather the little water they hold. You put it into your literjon for later.
