## :dancer: Scene 3C: The Dance

You remember that sand worms are attracted rhythmic movements. You have been taught the right way to cross the sands on foot. It's slower and takes more effort but you won't die. You walk in an arhythmic manner. It kind of looks like a dance, a dance of life.

It takes a long time but you manage to make it to a rock island in the sandy sea. You are getting very thirsty at this point.
