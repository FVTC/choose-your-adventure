# That sandy place with the spice

## Based off Dune By Frank Herbert

It's the year 10,191. Shaddam Corrino IV is the Emporer of Humanity. Spice has a vice grip on the Empire.

The desert planet Arrakis is the only known planet that produces the rust brown drug. Used for everything from slight precience to space travel. 

The Atreides family will soon be given control of the planet, and with it, complete control of spice production. You are a lone wanderer who has found themself on Arrakis and must survive.
