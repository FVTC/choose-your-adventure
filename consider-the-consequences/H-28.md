# H-28



When Helen accepted Saunders's invitation to go to the city, she went upstairs and took off her black dress without dampening her husband's high spirits by mentioning that it was the anniversary of the death of her first husband. The beautiful ride in the crisp air blew away the depression from which she had been suffering all day. She enjoyed meeting Carol, who was handsomer than ever and getting a divorce from her husband.

The consequence of considering a living husband's happiness more important than a dead husband's memory was that Helen came back from the trip closer to Saunders than she had ever been and convinced that having a good time was part of a wife's duty.
