# Consider the Consequences!



Life is not a continuous line from the cradle to the grave. Rather, it is many short lines, each ending in a choice, and branching right and left to other choices, like a bunch of seaweed or a genealogical table. No sooner is one problem solved than you face another growing out of the first.

We will be following Helen's story in this project, though there are two others in the original version. It is recommended to follow your first thought on each decision.
