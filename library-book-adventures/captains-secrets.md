# Captain's Secrets

You kneel beside the chest. The cracked brass lock flakes under your touch, and the lid creaks open to reveal:

A tattered logbook with bloodstained pages and strange symbols in the margins. Some entries are in your handwriting—or at least, the captain's.

A half-burned map marked with sea routes and a black X somewhere deep in uncharted waters.

An odd compass that doesn’t point north… it spins slowly, then locks onto something behind you.

Then—you hear it.

A whisper. Not a voice from above. From inside the chest.

“You opened it too soon...”

The ship groans louder than before. The lantern overhead sputters.

Suddenly, you’re not alone down here.

Footsteps echo on wet wood. Not yours.

You run for the ladder.

A cold hand grabs your ankle.

Screams above deck. Silence below.

Later, the crew finds only the chest.
Inside: a new map.
A fresh logbook.
And a compass, spinning...

_— The End —_
