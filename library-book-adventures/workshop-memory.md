# 🛠️ Scene 2: The Workshop

You step into the hallway, gears turning silently overhead like they’re watching. The temperature rises. Steam hisses from vents as mechanical arms retract into the walls. Sparks dance across the floor tiles.

At the end of the hall, you enter a circular chamber filled with floating data orbs and scattered mech parts. A glowing console in the center hums as you approach. It flickers to life:

> "UNIT 9-XE7 — FRAGMENTED MEMORY LOCATED. RECONSTRUCTION OPTIONS AVAILABLE."

Three files spin into view, each labeled differently:

- **⚙️ Prototype Test Log 7** – "Memory fragment from your first mission run."
- **🧬 Core Ethics File** – "Contains your decision-making framework."
- **💥 Combat Subroutine Alpha** – "Aggression limiter and tactics module."

A robotic whisper echoes behind you. "Be careful what you remember. Some things stay broken for a reason..."
