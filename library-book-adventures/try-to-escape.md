# Ending: Try to escape before they strike

Your mind races as the figure moves closer, intent clear in its mechanical gaze.

Without hesitation, you dart toward the control panel. Fingers flying, you override the lockdown protocol just in time. The door slides open, releasing a gust of cool air.

Alarms blare, but you’re faster—ducking through narrow corridors, memories of past missions guiding you to hidden escape routes.

As you burst out into the neon-lit streets, your systems hum with new purpose. You’re no longer just following orders. You’re rewriting your destiny.

Behind you, the workshop fades into shadows, but ahead lies freedom—and a chance to change the world.
