🏝️ Chart a course toward the Isle of Echoes
===

Scene 3:
---

You grip the spyglass, your reflection flickering in its brass rim like a ghost. Through the glass, the glowing island ripples with golden mist, rising and falling as if it breathes. You lower the lens.

“The Isle of Echoes,” you repeat softly, tasting the words. “Set the course.”

A collective murmur passes through the crew—half fear, half awe—but they obey. Ropes tighten. Sails unfurl. The ship groans as it turns, creaking toward the light. You feel it under your boots—the vessel responding, not with resistance, but with recognition.

As the island looms closer, the storm behind you stills, as though the sky itself is holding its breath. The sea smooths into glass. The crew exchanges uneasy looks. One whispers, “We shouldn’t be this quiet.”

Then the whispers begin—not from the crew, but from the wind. They echo faintly from the waves, repeating your own orders back at you in distorted voices.

“Set the course… set the course… set the course…”

Your heart hammers. The wiry deckhand returns, clutching the weathered chart. “Cap’n,” he says, his voice trembling, “there’s something wrong with this map.”

He spreads it before you. The ink shifts as you watch, coastlines melting, letters rearranging. The Isle of Echoes glows faintly on the parchment, and beside it—drawn in red ink that wasn’t there before—your name.

Captain Sable.

And beneath it, a note scratched in desperate script:
“You cannot sail to the Isle without hearing yourself first.”

The moment you read it, a sound rises from the island—a low, resonant hum that grows into words you recognize all too well.

Your own voice.

“Why did you return?”

The deck tilts. The horizon bends. You feel the pull—not of gravity, but of memory—dragging you toward the glowing shore.
