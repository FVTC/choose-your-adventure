# 🌊 Ending: The Storm That Remembers

You give the book to the Oracle and unlock their shackles. They chant a ritual in a forgotten tongue. The ship trembles, sails unfurl on their own, and a whirlpool of memories engulfs the hold.

You awaken back in the library, the book now blank. You remember everything. But the hallway? Gone.

The ship lives on, somewhere.
