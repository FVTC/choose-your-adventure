# ⚓ Ending: The Name You Forgot

You keep the logbook. The Oracle tilts their head—not angry, just tired.

“It always ends this way,” they murmur.

As you ascend, the ship seems quieter. Obedient. You speak commands, and the sails turn without wind. The stars rearrange themselves to your will.

But with every order you give, something leaves you. A detail. A memory.

Your name.
Your voice.
Your purpose.

The logbook writes and writes. You read it, desperate to remember who you were—but the pages only contain commands, dates, tides.

You find an old entry written in your own hand, but you don’t remember writing it.

“The captain is not who we remember.”
“We buried the old name.”

One day, you see someone below deck—crouched by a chest. They're holding the logbook. They look up, startled. You open your mouth to speak, but nothing comes out.

You are the one in the cage now. But you can't remember why.
