# Ending: Echoes Beyond the Shoals

You plot a course for the place marked Memory Shoals. As the ship drifts into stranger tides, the sea begins to glow with the reflections of people you’ve never met—and some you have. Shadows of memory flicker in the sails, whisper through the rigging.

Time slips.

At the Shoals’ heart lies an ancient lighthouse submerged halfway in the sea. You step inside. Its spiral staircase leads to a single room: bare, save for a stone pedestal.

The logbook trembles in your hands.

An inscription above the pedestal reads:

"To remember is to remain. To release is to return."

You understand then: placing the logbook here severs your tie to this place—but not without cost.

You place it down.

The ship dissolves. The lighthouse dims.

You wake back in the library, just as you were. Everything is familiar. Normal.

But as you reach for another book… you realize you don’t remember what brought you here. You remember the story—but not your part in it.

Was it real? Were you?

Behind you, the hallway is gone.

On your wrist, a faint spiral mark—like an old scar—fades as you watch.
