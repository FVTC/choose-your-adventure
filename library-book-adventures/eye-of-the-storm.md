# 🌪️ Eye of the Storm

You tighten your grip on the helm.

“Hold course! Stay clear of that island!”

The crew snaps into motion, though a few glance nervously at the glowing landmass fading behind the stormclouds.

The wind howls. Rain lashes your face. The ship heaves beneath your boots like it’s trying to throw you off. A mast cracks. You shout orders — ones you didn’t know you knew — and somehow, the crew responds without question.

Then the storm pauses.

Not calms — pauses. The wind dies mid-howl. Rain freezes mid-air. The ocean itself stills, like glass. You can hear your own breath.

At your feet, a barrel rolls open with a soft thunk. Inside, glowing faintly red, is the same book you opened in the library.

But this time, the pages are open — and writing is appearing on them.

“Captain must decide: Return and forget… or remember and descend.”

A rumble begins beneath the ship. The sea shifts from glass to mirror, showing not your reflection — but someone else’s.
