Tinkered
========

Scene 1
--------

The first thing you hear is… nothing.

Then:

Whirrrrrrr.
Click.
Blink.
“BOOT SEQUENCE COMPLETE.”

You open your eyes—cameras, actually. The world around you is a pale-blue cube with glowing walls, each one humming with strange symbols. You can feel wires where veins should be. A name flashes in your vision:

UNIT 9-XE7
STATUS: ONLINE
MEMORY: FRAGMENTED
MISSION: UNKNOWN

A panel slides open in the wall ahead. Two paths reveal themselves:

🔧 A hallway of glowing gears and steam — the Workshop.

🛰 A tunnel of blinking lights and static whispers — the Network Core.

Do you:

[🛠️Enter the Workshop and try to repair your memory?](./workshop-memory.md)

[🤖Stay still and pretend you’re offline?](./robot-wars-offline.md)
