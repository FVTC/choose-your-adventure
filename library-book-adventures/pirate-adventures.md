Ahoy!
======
Scene 1: Portal
----------------

You were drawn to the pirate book. It's glowing red exterior sparking more curiosity any cat's ever known.

And when you touched the ink-stained corner of that first chapter, the world tilted.

Now the library is gone. The floor is wood. The air tastes like brine and storm. Below your feet, something creaks—no, groans—as if the ship itself were waking up.

A bell rings from above. Heavy boots thunder across the deck. You look down: you're wearing someone else’s boots. A long coat whips behind you in the sea wind.

A voice shouts from behind:

“Oy! Captain’s awake! Tell ‘em the storm’s coming back!”

Captain!? You turn.

They're looking at you.

Do you:

[⚓ Step up and pretend to be the captain?](./pretend-captain.md)

[🏃 Hide below deck and figure out what’s going on?](./captain-hides.md)

[📖 Try to find the book again and wake up?](./pirate-book-search.md)
