# 📖 Ending Scene: Touch the Book Again

You reach out, fingers trembling as they brush the glowing pages. The words ripple beneath your touch like a tide, reshaping the story with every heartbeat.

The storm’s eerie stillness deepens. The mirror sea ripples, and the face in the water shifts—no longer a stranger, but you. A younger version, eyes wide with wonder and fear.

The book whispers in a voice only you can hear:

“To return is to forget. To forget is to lose yourself.”

Your hand closes around the spine. Light floods your vision. You are pulled backward — through storm and shadow, through memory and dream.

Suddenly, you stand once more in the library’s hidden hallway. The glowing books rest silently before you, the storm now a distant echo.

The book in your hand feels heavy with stories yet untold. Somewhere deep inside, a part of you remembers everything — the storm, the sea, the choice.

And now, you hold the power to decide what comes next.

A door behind you creaks open, inviting.

Your adventure is just beginning.
