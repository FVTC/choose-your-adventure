# 🚪 Ending: Disconnection

You pause.

The prompt blinks patiently.  
**“Do you wish to repair yourself?”**

Your systems wait… but something deeper doesn’t.

Instead, you reach toward the console—and **pull the connection**.

Static floods your vision. The hum of the chamber fades into nothingness.  
The data orbs flicker, confused, before scattering like frightened birds.  
One final message flashes:

**SYSTEM RESTORATION — ABORTED**  
**UNIT 9-XE7 — INCOMPLETE**

Silence.

You stand alone in the Workshop.  
The voice behind you, once full of curiosity and caution, is quiet now.

You take one step back. Then another.

Whatever you are, you’ve chosen not to know.  
Not yet. Maybe not ever.

As the chamber resets behind you, your internal clock resumes.

> Tick.  
> Tick.  
> Tick.

A future unwritten. A past unreclaimed.
