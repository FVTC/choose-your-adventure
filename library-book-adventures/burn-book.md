# 🔥 Ending: The Flame Remembers

You hesitate only a moment before throwing the logbook and the chart into the captain’s brazier. The flames erupt—green, then white, then impossibly dark. The ship lurches as the fire spreads unnaturally fast, licking across ropes and sails without consuming them.

You hear the Oracle’s voice, faint and distant:

“Fire doesn't forget. It forges.”

The spiral emblem blazes on the deck, glowing hotter than the sun—then explodes into light.

You wake on the library floor. Dry. Breathless.

The hallway is still there—but now, there’s no red or green book.

Just a single black book on a pedestal where the hallway begins.

It’s unmarked. Untouched.

When you open it, the first page reads:

“This time, you burned the story. But stories burn back.”

Your name is written underneath—in ink that hasn’t dried.
