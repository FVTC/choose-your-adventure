⚓ Echoes in the Hold
==
Scene 3: 
--

The rocking of the ship deepens, like it’s moving through thicker water than the sea should ever allow. You crouch beside the chest, its cracked brass lock no match for a gentle tug. The lid creaks open with a puff of stale air and something sharper—ink, maybe… or smoke.

Inside, wrapped in black sailcloth, is a leather-bound logbook. The cover is marked with the same strange emblem burned into the ship’s wheel above deck: a spiral over crossed oars. A faint shimmer clings to the edge of the pages.

You flip it open.

The entries are scrawled in jagged, urgent script:

“The captain is not who we remember.”
“Winds are warping. The stars lie.”
“The book found them again. Just like last time.”
“We buried the old name. Gods help us if they speak it again.”

Suddenly—

**CLANG.**

A heavy chain rattles deeper in the hold. You freeze. From the shadows beyond the crates, something shifts.

A voice rasps—not quite human.

“That coat doesn’t belong to you… _but_ the ship listens anyway.”

You slam the book shut and back toward the ladder, heart in your throat. But then:

You notice the voice came from a chained figure, slumped in a shadowed cage.

They wear a blindfold. One wrist is shackled. But they smile like they’ve seen you before.
