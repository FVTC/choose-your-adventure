# Ending 2: Trapped in the Library

You approach the librarian cautiously, your voice barely above a whisper.

“Excuse me, what’s with those glowing books back there?”

The librarian’s eyes flash with a strange, unreadable emotion. “Those books contain knowledge meant only for the chosen few,” they say. “Once you know too much, you can never leave.”

Before you can respond, the library doors slam shut behind you. The glow intensifies, and the librarian’s face twists into a knowing smile.

You realize, too late, that curiosity didn’t just kill the cat—it trapped you here, a prisoner of forbidden knowledge.
