Scene 2: 🏃 Hide below deck and figure out what’s going on?
====



You duck instinctively, heart pounding, and slip through the nearest hatch before anyone can ask questions. The wooden ladder is slick with seawater, the air below thick with damp rope and something metallic—_blood!?_ No... rust. You land softly in the dim hold, shadows shifting around barrels and crates that rock with the waves.

Somewhere above, boots stomp past, and muffled voices bark orders. You catch fragments through the boards—“reef the sails!” … “storm’s rollin’ in faster than she should!”


You creep closer. The ship lurches, and a low moan echoes from the darkness. You take a cautious step closer to the cages, the boards creaking under your borrowed boots. 

_I'm not supposed to be here , what do I do!?_

You spot a weather-worn chest bolted to the floor near the ladder. The brass lock is cracked, and something inside glints when the ship tilts. If you really are the captain, maybe what’s inside will tell you who you’ve become—or what you’ve done.
