# ⚓ Scene 2: Taking the Helm

You straighten your spine, adjusting the coat as though you’ve worn it a hundred times. The crew gathers quickly, their eyes expectant, weathered, and just a bit suspicious.

"Aye," you say, your voice surprisingly steady. "Looks like we’ve got a storm on our tail. Batten the hatches, double-check the sails, and someone bring me the chart!"

A wiry deckhand with a tattooed face nods and bolts off. Another passes you a spyglass with a reverent look. For a moment, it almost feels... natural.

Then the ship groans again, louder. The sky darkens unnaturally fast, and you spot something glinting on the horizon—golden, glowing, and shaped like an island that *wasn’t* there before.

A mutter rises from the crew. One voice cuts through it: "Captain, that's the Isle of Echoes. The last crew that sailed near it never came back."

But you feel drawn to it, like the book called you before.
