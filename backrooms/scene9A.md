# 🌃 Scene 9A: The Window. 

You decide to look out the window.

It's cold and dark. The sky is tinged an unnatural colour that feels dangerous. The landscape stretches off; a dark snowy abyss framed by looming dark pines. It almost feels like looking outside alone drains your body of warmth. 

> 💭 It's probably best to stay inside.

At the edge of the pines, you see a shadowy creature move. 
It almost seems to crawl, moving around on four limbs like a dog. Its limbs don't seem to coordinate with one another, and it almost appears to stumble over its own feet. You swear you catch a glimpse of its eyes flashing towards you, but it's too dark to see fully. 

> 💭 What if it's just a lost dog? If it's hurt, maybe I can help somehow.

You're not sure what's out there, and you pause for a moment, trying to decide what to do. You could always try to take a photo with your phone? Maybe being able to see it in clearer detail would help you chose your next steps.
