# 👁 Scene 13B: You turn to look at it.

You decide to turn around to look at it. 

You freeze in fear as your eyes pass over the humanoid shape peering at you through the window. Its eyes are too large, reflecting neon green like a cat in the night. Its mouth is full of teeth too sharp to be natural, lips curled into a cruel smile. 

You watch its twisted hand reach up, scraping at the glass over and over-- each time using more and more force. 

The glass around its twisted claws begins to splinter and crack. Every second you stare in horror is another second it grows closer to breaking through the glass. 

Despite the imminent threat, you can't seem to look away from the awful thing before you. 
You freeze like a deer in the headlights. Stuck and immobile despite the pressing danger. 

Another tap and the creature's arm pushes through the thin pane. It crumbles as the creature pushes its face against the opening and begins to squeeze its way in; moving in a way that indicates its more *fluid* than *bone*, contorting and stretching as it reaches for you.

- 😱 [I can't look away. I can't move.]()
