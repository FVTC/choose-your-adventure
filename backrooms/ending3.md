# 💤 ENDING 3/3: You fall asleep in the Movie Theatre.

You try to keep your eyes open, desperate to see the end of the movie. 

But bundled up in the dark quiet room, safe in your blankets, you can't help but nod off.

The movie chatters on endlessly, seeming to be a never-ending loop. The sound of other people talking is a comfort you didn't know you'd miss until now. 

When your eyes again, your phone is chiming your wake-up alarm softly on the nightstand. You blink sleep from your eyes and reach over to turn your alarm off, pushing the covers back off yourself. 

It was all just a dream?

*How strange. I wonder if I'll dream about that place again?*
