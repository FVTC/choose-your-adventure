# 🛌 Scene 5B: The Bedroom

You decide to enter the bedroom.

The bedroom is dark, illuminated by the dull glow of a CRT. The TV doesn't play anything meaningful; the static of a dead channel buzzes through the air, intermittently interrupted by clips of cartoons you had once watched. 

This room smells distinctly different from the rest of the house. It smells like *you*, or at least, how you once smelled. Waxy crayons, bubblegum, baby soap, fresh laundry, a hint of perfume you stole from your parents. 

Toys are scattered across the floor. You move with a delicate step so you don't disturb them. 

There's a strange ache in your chest as you look at your old room. Crayon drawing scribbled across the drywall, pictures and posters of all the people and things you loved, clothing balled up in strange spaces. A blanket fort half-collapsed, lit by rainbow Christmas string lights. 

You find a small stuffed animal peaking at you from the fort. 

You pick it up with the delicate hands of a surgeon and tuck it safely into your backpack. You may not be able to bring your childhood innocence and wonder, but you can bring a friend that experienced it all with you. 

You sniffle, and your eyes tear up. Surprised at the overwhelming emotion crawling you your throat, you take a few steps back, trying to decide what to do next before the grief in your chest -- a deep longing for something so tangible but so unobtainable -- makes you cry. 

As you back your way out of the room, you catch a glimpse of a strange plastic opening in the wall. 
You *know* that wasn't there in your childhood. 

After staring at it for a moment, you realize that it's a slide. You have no idea where it goes, or if using it is even a good idea.
