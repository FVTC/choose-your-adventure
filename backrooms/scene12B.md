# 📽 Scene 12B: The Movie Theatre.

You decide to explore the movie theatre. 

You walk into the lobby. There's no one at the ticket booth or the concession stand. Despite the emptiness, it feels peaceful here. You have a feeling you can let your guard down and relax a little. 

You wander the halls, peeking into each cinema in an attempt to try to see if there are any movies playing. 

Eventually you find one. The movie on the screen is something hauntingly familiar. You *know* you've seen this movie before, but you can't place when or why, and can't recall the plot. 

You make your way into the theatre, finding a nook in the center row. There are blankets, snacks, and drinks all laid out for you here. You don't know *how* you know they're for you, but they are. 

With a heavy sigh of relief, you settle down to watch the movie and make yourself comfortable. 

- 🍿 [I'll just watch this movie, then I'll keep trying to find my way home...](./ending3.md)
