# 😵 Scene 1A: Confusing Map

Your head hurts. The fluorescent lights hum with a low buzzing that crackles and crunches. The carpet feels almost wet under your shoes. For something that looks so much like an office building, it's sickly humid. The faded wallpaper peels in spots, and you notice some of the ceiling tiles are soaked in long-left stains. 

You try your best; walking through halls and carefully drawing out the paths in your notebook. However, the further you walk and map out, the more things overlap. 

The map seems to spiral into itself; infinite fractal shapes that don't match any logical geometry. Your map begins to look more and more like one of those optical illusions and less like a map.
