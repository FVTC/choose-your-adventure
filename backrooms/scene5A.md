# 🍴 Scene 5A: The Kitchen

You decide to enter the kitchen.

The kitchen is strange. The scent of cooked food hangs heavy in the air, though you can't seem to spot anything actually *being* cooked. 

Maybe it's for the better. You haven't met anyone else yet, and at this point you have a feeling that would be a very bad thing to do. 

You can almost hear the distant chatter of a radio playing some sort of entertainment show; muffled voices echo like they're coming from under the floor. It feels like if you were to listen a *little* harder, pay a *little* more attention, you'd be able to make out the words. No matter how hard you strain, they don't become any clearer. 

Something about this room feels distinctly *wrong*. The floor squeaks softly under you in protest in all the ways you knew it would; but it feels more like a *warning* than it used to. 

Styrofoam fruit sits posed in a decorative bowl, and plastic flowers never wilt in their vase nearby. Both remain untouched by dust or the hands of time; forever preserved in this snapshot of your childhood. 

But... were those items ever *actually* there? Or are you misremembering things? Can you remember what the kitchen *really* looked like?

You shake your head to clear the overwhelming dread that begins to settle in your chest. Maybe a different part of the house will have answers.
