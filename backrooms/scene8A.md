# 😭 Scene 8A: Freaking out.

You panic. Fully, truly break down and panic.

All of this is *too much*, and the realization hits you suddenly. The fear pulls the breath out of you as you shiver, trying to stop yourself from crying. You can't wrap your head around this place. Its shifting walls, moving objects, and lingering sense of dread are all too much to handle. 

You can't help but feel like you've accidentally found something *terrible*, a place no human was ever truly meant to walk. 

Your hands tremble. You can't escape this persistent creeping feeling that *you're being watched.* It makes you feel like an animal in a cage. 

> 💭 I wonder if this is how lab rats feel. Am... *I* a lab rat?
You look down at yourself in fear, but quickly shake away the silly fear when you see your human hands and torso below. Of course you're not a rat. This place is just... getting to you. 

You become more and more agitated by the feeling of being watched. Your hands tremble as you look up, screaming out loud to the empty building that entombs you;

> 💬 "STOP WATCHING ME! LEAVE ME ALONE!!"

Your voice echoes softly, bouncing down the endless corridors. No sound returns to you. 

You expected screaming to make you feel a *little* better, but the silence in response is almost worse. There's *no one* here but you, and if there *is* something else out there, it is *deathly* quiet.
