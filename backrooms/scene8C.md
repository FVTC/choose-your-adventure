# 📚 Scene 8C: Explore the Library. 

You decide to explore the library.

For such a large space, it feels incredibly small. The shelves are packed together tightly. Some are overflowing with books, and some are sparse. 

The books here are all coated in a thick cloud of dust that makes you sneeze when you disturb it. 

You don't recognize the books, nor do you recognize the languages or symbols written on them, but you can't shake the ever-present feeling of distant familiarity. Maybe you *have* seen that book before. The second you look away from the cover, the image of the book slips out of your mind like a drifting leaf. 

> 💭 Was it something I read for a class? An article I read online? Maybe the ink is just smudged and that's why I can't read them. 

You try to shrug off the vague feelings of amnesia that hang heavy here. How ironic to be in a place of knowledge - surrounded by nearly endless education - yet unable to remember or retain any of it. 

As your eyes drift from book to book, you begin to realize you can hear something else moving around -- back towards the offices.
