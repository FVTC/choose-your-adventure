# ➰ Scene 6A: The Slide!

You decide to use the slide.

You make your way to the opening of the slide; a dark plastic tunnel into darkness opened up in front of you. 

> 💭 Well... what's the worst that could happen? 

You take a breath and sit down. The plastic is cold underneath you. You scoot forwards, attempting to get enough momentum to let gravity do the work for you. You almost feel like the slide just... doesn't work. 

> 💬 "How do you make a slide that doesn't work?" 

You mutter to yourself. A few more scoots forward, and it feels like you get *yanked* into the darkness by your ankles. 
You're suddenly plunging through the slide -- blind and directionless. You can feel that the slide twists and turns sharply, jostling you this way and that. Over and over it takes you around a curve, then another, forever moving faster than what feels physically possible. 

You see a light appear ahead of you and assume that the slide must be ending, finally! 

Your heart sinks as you realize it was just a pinhole in the plastic, letting in light from outside. You're dropped back into darkness, and you can feel the fear creep in as the slide continues to take you onward.
