# 🏃‍♂️ Scene 11A: Run.

You sprint in the opposite direction as fast as your legs will carry you. 

You hear it give chase -- it's heavy feet thrumming on the ground. You can hear its jaws opening and snapping shut behind you. You can *feel* the drool splatter against your skin. It snarls and screams, sounding like a disturbing concoction of animal, machine, and *human*. 

You run faster, your breath coming in ragged gasps. Your chest burns and your muscles ache but you don't give up. You *can't* give up. Not when you've come so far.

>💭 Just a little longer, if I can just get a little further away. I just need to lose it.

You hear that it begins to slow down behind you, clearly losing steam. The creature itself breathes hard, trying to catch its breath. You're almost positive you hear it flop down on the floor; laying down like an oversized dog as it decides the chase isn't worth it.

Still you run. You don't stop, you don't look back. You take every twist and turn that appears in front of you. *Anything* to get away from... *whatever* that thing is. 

Once you're sure that there is absolutely nothing behind you -- no panting or snarling. The only sound you hear is your own heartbeat thrumming in your ears and your breath shallow and raspy in your chest. You're almost positive you're nearly in the exact same spot you were when you first arrived.
