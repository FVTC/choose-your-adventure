# 📺 Scene 7C: The Basement

You decide to go downstairs. 

The darkness slowly gives way to a large carpeted basement. The carpet here is nicer; it's dry and almost soft to the touch. A lone couch sits across from an old CRT TV, retro game consoles and media scattered around its stand. 

The whir of a VHS player drones softly; mechanical clicks and movements audible though the TV screen doesn't change away from its soft static droning. You can't help but recognize this as the basement of your childhood home, though it feels too sterile -- as if it's missing all of its usual clutter. 

You recall this room being much messier when you were here last. Drink bottles, toys, blankets, your parent's weights had all been scattered around at one point. Now, there was just the couch and the TV with all of its retro tech. Things lost to time, you're sure. 

Towards the end of the room, you notice a door that was *absolutely* not there in your childhood. Through the cracked opening you see rows and racks of sprawling books. It seems to be a nearly endless library draped in muted colours. Hushed voices seem to seep through the doorway, but none of the words can be made out fully.
