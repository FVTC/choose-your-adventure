# 🏪 Scene 12A: Explore the Store

You decide to explore the 24/7 store. 

The store is open; the light inside is warm and welcoming. 
There is a person working behind the counter. They look *almost* human at first glance, but staring for a moment longer you realize they're distorted just a bit. Twisted into a strange approximation of humanity, like a marionette doll.

You decide it'd be rude to stare, and cast your eyes instead to the shopkeeper's wares respectfully. 

> 💬 *"Feel free to browse. Don't touch what you're not buying."*

The shopkeeper's rough voice sounds disjointed, worn with age and indistinguishable in gender. You nod your head respectfully and return to looking at the shelves of items; careful to keep your hands tucked in your pockets. 

They seem to sell oddities and trinkets, the shelves an eclectic collection of knick-knacks and items you can *almost* recognize but can't quite place. 

The lights in the store flicker, and for a moment you're plunged into darkness. You glance towards the shopkeeper's desk, but can't see through the darkness. 

When the lights flash back on with a mechanical hum, the store is empty. There are no trinkets on the shelves and the shopkeeper has vanished as well. A shiver runs up your spine. 

As you turn to leave, you hear something shuffling further down the hall.
