# 🌫 Scene 6C: The Lazy River

You decide to check out the lazy river. 

You make your way to the river's edge and grab onto a floaty, stopping it from drifting away. You climb in carefully, making sure you don't tip over in the process. Your hands and feet dangle in the water as you allow the river to carry you onward; bumping into the empty inflatable tubes as they drift along with you. 

It's scenic, in a cheesy cliché sort of way. Fake tropical beach plants line the edge of the river, and plastic replicas of real animals stare at you lifelessly. Their beady eyes seem to shift and follow you as you float past, but you can't be sure it's not just your imagination. 

The river takes you under a sparse imitation of a waterfall, and the scenery seems to grow... *unstable*. The plants and animals begin to look wrong; as if someone who'd never seen these animals created them. 

They're all too long, too many limbs, too many eyes, too many teeth. Just... *wrong.* Dread settles over you, more suffocating than the humidity in the room.

You *swear* you see one twist it's head to follow you as you pass.
