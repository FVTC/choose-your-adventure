# 🏬 Crossroads 2: The mall.

You squint your eyes, blinking in confusion. How exactly did you get here? How does that make sense?

One space turns into another, and without any recollection of how you got here, you find yourself standing dead center in a massive shopping mall. 

The lights are dim, but it's not dark. White, black, and grey brutalist architecture greats you; colourless aside from the storefronts that line the walls. Everything looks to be closed down and empty at a glance. There are no patrons, no security, no signs of life. 

Pop music plays over speakers you can't see. You don't know the song by name, but it's generic enough that you know you recognize it from *somewhere*. The words aren't clear but they don't need to be. Some sort of noise to fill the silence is a welcome change at this point. 

*What do you want to do?*
