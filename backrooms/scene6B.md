# 🌊 Scene 6B: The Wave Pool

You decide to check out the wave pool. 

It's the biggest pool you've ever seen. The far wall hides the mechanisms that create the waves. 

The waves are massive and roll out from the deep end of the pool like tsunami waves, crashing and rolling into frothing water perfectly at the shallow end of the pool. The waves never leave, never change shape or size, and never miss a beat. 

The cold mechanical action controlling something that's usually so natural unsettles you. It makes it feel like this... *thing* is alive. As if the pool itself echos with a heartbeat; the waves acting like the thrumming pulse. 

You notice that it's darker back here than the rest of the water park. There's less light. It almost makes it feel stormy. It feels fitting for something replicating one of nature's violent storms. 
 
### *What do you do?*

- 😱 [I don't think I want to go in there... I'll walk around the edge.](./crossroads1.md)

- 🏡 [I jump in! I want to swim under the waves!](./crossroads2.md)
