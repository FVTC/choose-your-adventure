# 🟥 Scene 10A: The exit? 

You decide to look for an exit.

As you wander the empty corridors of the mall, your eyes scan for anything that might indicate an exit. A fire escape, A glowing sign, a window, *anything*. 

You walk for what feels like miles. Most of the stores are dark; their lights off and security gates down. The pop music playing over the speakers drones on. You can hardly tell when one song ends and another begins, they're all so similar. 

Empty benches, decorative trees in planters, empty trash cans, and nonsensical advertisements for sales litter the mall. 
You're about to give up hope, but one more turn down a nearly identical hallway reveals what you've been looking for.

The faint glow of an exit sign, hovering above a heavy metal door.
You pick up speed and without realizing, you're running towards it. Your hand reaches out to push the door open, but you hesitate for a moment. 

> 💭 What if... what if this is a trap??

The convenience of finding this door *exactly* when you felt like giving up... it makes it feel almost *too* good to be true.
