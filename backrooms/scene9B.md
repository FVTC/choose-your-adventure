# 🔊 Scene 9B: The Sound is moving...

You try to follow the sound.

You could have sworn that while you were in the living room, the sound was coming from this hallway. 

Now that you're here, you pause and listen again; giving the quiet house your *full* attention. 

Sure enough, the shifting and shuffling come again, but from a different direction this time. It seems hard to place. You're not sure if the source of the sound is moving around you to toy with you and drive you mad, or if it wants you to follow. 

> 💭 I think angler fish do something similar with their prey... don't they? Dangle a light and the little fish swim right up to them. 

You pause, taking note of your surroundings before making a decision. One end of the hallway leads you back to the living room. The dim TV light flickers softly, almost beckoning you to come back. As if it's drawing you endlessly towards the past. 

The other end of the hallway has a door that sits barely open. You can't see in clearly, but you can hear outdated pop music playing softly. It's not a song you recognize, but the tune is simple and repetitive enough that you're sure you've heard it *somewhere* before.
