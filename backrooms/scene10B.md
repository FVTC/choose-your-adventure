# 🧭 Scene 10B: Mall Exploration.

You decide to explore the mall. 

You wander aimlessly for a while, taking in the sights. Most of the stores are closed, their security gates resting heavily against the cold tile floor. Ads of happy people smiling and showcasing strange products you've never seen before stare back at you from posters and pictures, attempting to coax you into buying things. 

The ceiling is high and arched, skylights litter the ceiling though you can't see anything besides an inky black veil stretched overtop. 

You find a map of the mall on a still-lit kiosk and approach it, trying to make sense of where you are and what stores the mall offers. 

Most of the map seems like nonsensical gibberish. Things overlap impossibly, your mind struggling to make logical sense of illogical shapes. You try to not think about it too hard. 

With a little effort, you're able to decipher where you are. It seems like just down the hall from you there is a movie theatre, and a store that claims to be "open 24/7". 

> 💭 That's? A strange thing for a mall store to be open 24/7... what a weird place.
