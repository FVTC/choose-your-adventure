# 🛋 Crossroads 1: The Living room.

As you explore, you move into a living room.

The living room is a large mostly empty room. A sofa and rocking chair sit on one side, with a TV and book cases on the other. It's almost an identical replica of the one in your childhood home, but some of the details are skewed. 

There are stains in the paint where paintings and picture frames once sat -- now missing with nothing but a ghost imprinted on the walls. The TV screen flickers softly, jumping between static and what seems to be some sort of weather channel. It claims to display a local forecast, but it's not for any place you recognize. 

Actually... the longer you look at it, the more you realize you can't really *read* anything written there. Staring at the buzzing screen for too long makes your head ache and your ears ring. You decide it's best to leave it alone. 

With the room itself so empty, you're almost overwhelmed with choice. 

There's a window on one side of the living room -- You can move over to the window and see what's outside.

There's an open door on the other side, opposite of where you entered. You're pretty sure you can see racks of books through the door, but you'd have to go in to be sure.

You can move down the hallway, towards a distant shuffling sound. It almost looks like there's another room at the end of the hall, though you don't remember one being there in the past. You can hear the sound of distant outdated pop music, wherever it echoes from. 

Or you can move towards the sound of distant outdated pop music, wherever it echoes from.
