# 🔊 Scene 8B: You *hear* something.

You begin to hear something moving around. 

Shifting, just out of sight, but it's there. Something that doesn't quite sound like an animal, but *definitely* doesn't sound human. As it shifts, you can tell that *whatever* it is it coming straight towards you. 

You can hear its labored wheezing breaths as it draws closer; a rumbling growl echoes as if it knows where you are. 

You hear heavy sniffing; like an animal tracking down its next meal. Fear grips every part of you and locks you to the spot. You don't want to know what it is. You don't want to see it. 

And yet, the creature is lumbering towards you. 

You can hear it disturbing the ceiling tiles as it moves. 

> 💭 Is it looking for me in the ceiling? Or is it just too big to fit in the hallway? 

Your brain screams at you to run, to leave, to get as far away as possible, but you just can't get your body to respond. You shiver in fear, unable to make even a sound. 

You finally see it; a massive hand reaches out of the hallway. It slams into the carpet with such force you can feel the vibration rattle through your bones. It's nearly the size of your torso. The arm attached to it is long; abnormally thin for something with so much weight. The fingertips curve into sharp clawed spikes, digging into the floor and leaving deep score marks. 

You realize it's dragging itself through the hallway. Dragging itself closer to you. You start to see a face peek around the corner. 

Your brain and body finally sync up. You do the only logical thing.

- 😱 [Run.](./crossroads3.md)
