# 👁 Scene 7B: It feels like you're being watched...

You can't shake the feeling that something is *watching you...*

It's uncomfortable. A prickling sensation on the back of your neck. It feels like a bad dream; realizing everyone in the room is staring into you. 

Only, there's not another soul in sight. 

You turn around to check, but you see nothing other than flickering fluorescent bulbs. The feeling is behind you again, so you turn around and you're met with --

nothing. Nothing apart from a slow and steady drip of water from a sagging ceiling tile; the water pooling in a plastic bucket beneath. The bucket overflows, water seeping into the carpet. 

A chill climbs up your spine, cold fear gripping your limbs. You can't help but feel like you're the prey here.
