# 🌊 Scene 4D: The Water Park.

You decide to enter the water park. 

The second your foot lands on the slick tiles, your breath catches in your throat. The smell *inside* the room is *so much stronger* than it was outside. The hot scent of hyper-sterile chlorinated water grabs your senses. It nearly burns your eyes, and the humidity is so high you sweat and your shirt clings uncomfortably to you. 

Despite the overwhelming smells and humidity, you push forward. You're careful to ensure you don't run on the pool deck. There are no lifeguards, but you have the unsettling feeling that something *awful* would happen if you broke the rules. You don't want to find out what.

The entire water park sprawls endlessly in tones of blue and white, dotted with bright plastic slides, toys, and inflatables. Fake jungle plants decorate sitting areas; tables and chairs lined up neatly, dressed with pool towels. The slides are supported by sprawling metal scaffolding; twisting and turning in lazy circles and loops. Some vanish into the concrete walls and reappear later, some never seem to reappear at all.

Off in the distance, the lazy river trails on and on, vanishing out of view into what almost seems to be fog. You can hear the sound of rhythmic waves crashing at consistent intervals and determine there must be a wave pool nearby. It sounds cold and mechanical against the harsh humid atmosphere.  

As you walk along the pool edge and stare down into the water, you notice you can see all the way down. Even the deepest sections of the pool look bright and deceptively shallow. You can't help but shiver; imagining what would happen if you swam down to the bottom.
