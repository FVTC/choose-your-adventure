# 🏡 Scene 4C: Your Childhood Home.

You decide to step out into the cool night air, following the road that leads to the lit home. 

As you pass by the other homes, they remain dark and motionless. Streetlamps flicker as you pass, buzzing softly with an uncanny frequency. The trees and grass sway softly in a breeze you can't feel. It's almost *too* quiet. 

Dogs bark, wind chimes sing softly, crickets chirp; but they all sound *so far away.* As if someone were playing the sounds on a recording, always from somewhere you can't see. 

A chill of unease creeps down your spine as you approach the front door, though you can't place why. 

The door hangs open - golden light pouring out into the inky veil of night like warm honey. 

As you place your hand on the door to push it open, you realize that this *is* your childhood home. The door you had opened countless times without a thought is there before you -- worn down in all the same ways it had been the last time you were here. Of *course* you'd remember. 

You push the door open and step inside; the smell of innocence and childhood lingers heavy here. It's distinct and unnamable. Home cooked meals, cologne and perfume, soaps and cleaning supplies. Every scent that makes a home blurred into one. It smells *exactly* as you remember it. 

Despite the comfort of nostalgia, you can't shake the feeling that it's somehow *wrong* to be here. As if you're an unwelcome intruder on the past.

As you look around, you take a moment to decide which part of the house to visit first.
