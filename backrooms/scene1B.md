# 😵 Scene 1B: Moving Landmarks

Your head hurts. The way this place stinks of rotting wood and wet carpet is enough to make your skin crawl. As you navigate, you take note of landmarks; making a mental note of which direction you take after each one. 

Left at the door with the peeling paint... right at the potted plant... right after the room that stood empty apart from a single plastic chair... left after the pillar with the stain on it...

You pause for a moment. There - in front of you - is the door with the peeling paint. You're *sure* this is the same door you saw earlier, but you know every single move you've made. There's no way you should have ended up back here... right?
