# 😔 Scene 4A: The map doesn't work.

The map isn't working. You're sure of it now. The intersecting lines you've drawn, the overlapping impossible shapes, the infinite maze. 

It almost feels like this place is alive. The more you try to understand it, the more it fights back. The more confusing and disorienting it becomes. Rooms and hallways seem to blur and melt together in your memory. A hallway you just drew on your map seems to have shifted and changed shape when you look up from your notebook. 

> 💭 This isn't working. I need to try something else. I'll never find my way out like this. 

You sigh and shiver involuntarily. This place gives you the creeps. There's a set of stairs set into the wall ahead of you. They seem to lead down into a room that vanishes into darkness. 

It almost feels like there's something else here with you, despite the fact that you haven't seen another soul.
