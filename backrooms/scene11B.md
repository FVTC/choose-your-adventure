# 👁 Scene 11B: Try to see what the creature is.

You try to get a glimpse of the creature barreling through the hallways. 

It's a large lumbering thing, almost the same size as you. It almost resembles an animal, but some of its features are too human-shaped to be comfortable to look at.

No matter what part of the creature you look at, it's all *wrong*. It has too many eyes... too many teeth. Its limbs are too long, stretching out towards you with too many hands and grasping fingers that are too sharp. You can't tell if it has skin or fur but it rushes towards you like a freight train. 

It knocks into everything in its path, desperate to catch the invader in its home. It lets out a choking gargled noise that almost sound like a person's scream. You swear you see excitement in its eyes. 

> 💭 Is it... smiling at me?

You have to move, you *know* you have to move. But you're frozen. The fear paralyzes you, making even your blood feel cold. With every moment the creature grows closer. You can practically smell the stench of rot and decay that radiates from its body. 

> 💭 Come on... ***move!***
