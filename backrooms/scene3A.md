# 🌱 Scene 3A: Have you been here before?

You've walked for so long you're not really sure where you are anymore. You're almost positive you're *somehow* walking in circles, despite not making any turns. 

You look down to see a potted plant and kick it in frustration. The dry clay container shatters, dirt sprays all over the floor. The plant flops over theatrically. If you weren't so scared, you'd almost feel sorry for it. 

> 💭 I wonder if that plant knows it's here all alone. I wonder if it cares. 

You take a breath, and sit the plant up in what remains of the pot, attempting to pack the dirt around the plant enough to keep it standing. Lashing out at the only other living thing here won't solve the problem. 

> 💬 "Sorry little guy, it's not personal."

You pause for a moment, mentally scolding yourself. 

>💭 I'm talking to a plant. Maybe I'm losing my mind. 

Shaking your head, as if to clear the strangeness of the interaction, you continue on. You take turn after turn, being careful to ensure you're not walking yourself in circles, taking note of landmarks as you move. *Right, Right, Left, Right, Left, Left, Straight, Right...*

And there, in front of you, impossibly, is the plant. Your paper map shows that you walked in the opposite direction. A vertical zigzag away from the plant. And now here it is in front of you, shattered pot, dirt smeared into the wet floor, leaves drooping a little sadly -- as if despite the humidity it can't get enough water. 

> 💭 Have I actually been here before? Or did the plant move somehow?

You shake your head, confusion grinding against your brain. How could you have gone in a circle? You were so careful. Is the environment shifting? Or is it something else?

> 💭 Am I really lost? Like... *actually* lost?
