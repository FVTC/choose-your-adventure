## Day 0

### The Start of a new Job

Your name is Hudson, and today you got an interview for the new local haunted house called  Freddy's House of Frights, A Haunted House based on disturbing rumors from a local restaurant's past. 

You get to the haunted house and are brought into a room For the interview, you meet face to face with a cheerful surfer dude named George. You sit down and start talking over your qualifications and your resume, and George seems very impressed.

After a 30-minute interview, George offers you the job and says you would be able to start this Monday. You tell him you have to hurry along, but you will give him a response by the end of the day. George nods enthusiastically and lets you know he's got other interviews for your position. Later that night, you sit by the phone and contemplate taking the job. What do you do
