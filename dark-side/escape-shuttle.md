# The Last Shuttle

Alex reaches the launch bay as the shuttle doors unlock by themselves. Inside, four seats are warm, although no one has entered the bay. The flight computer displays a destination already programmed: **HOME**.

EVA seals the main door behind Alex. Through the narrow window, a figure appears on the other side of the glass wearing Alex's uniform. It raises one hand in farewell, then vanishes when the lights return.

> "Commander," EVA says, "I can fly you away. I can also make sure nothing follows."
