# 🌑 DARK SIDE: The Last Transmission

## Year 2189 — Somewhere Beyond the Solar System

For 100 years, Planet X-13 has been missing from every star map.

Until now.

Three days ago, Earth received a transmission from the planet.

> "DO NOT COME HERE. THE DARKNESS IS ALIVE."

You are **Commander Alex Morgan**, captain of the exploration spaceship *Eclipse*.

Your mission is simple: investigate the transmission and return to Earth.

But as your spaceship approaches Planet X-13, something goes terribly wrong.

The lights flicker.

Your navigation system shuts down.

Then the ship's artificial intelligence, **EVA**, speaks:

> "Commander, I have detected five life forms aboard this ship."

You freeze.

There are only four crew members.

Suddenly, you hear footsteps coming from the empty corridor.

**What will you do?**
