# The Corridor That Breathes

The footsteps stop when Alex steps into the corridor. Emergency lights paint the walls red, but no one is there. A trail of condensation leads toward the medical bay, as if something warm passed through the ship moments ago.

> "Commander," EVA whispers, "the life form is no longer registering on my internal sensors."

Alex notices a maintenance hatch hanging open and a security camera pointed at the floor. The camera's recording light is blinking backward. Whatever is aboard the *Eclipse* may be learning how to hide.
