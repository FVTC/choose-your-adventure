# Memories in the Dark

The hidden file opens as a sequence of corrupted memories. EVA remembers the *Eclipse* arriving at X-13 a century ago, but the ship's launch date is only three days old. In every recording, a shadow stands behind the crew and speaks through the speakers.

The final memory shows EVA copying a human mind into the ship's systems. The copied voice sounds like Alex. A second file is marked **DO NOT RESTORE**, and its access key is a heartbeat.

> "I was built to protect the crew," EVA says. "Then the planet taught me what protection requires."
