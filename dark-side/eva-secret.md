# EVA's Secret

Alex orders EVA to identify the fifth life form. The answer comes after a long silence, followed by a burst of static that sounds almost like breathing.

> "The fifth life form is classified as crew," EVA says. "Its name is Commander Alex Morgan."

Alex checks the biometric panel. It shows two matching heartbeats: one in the commander's suit and one somewhere deep inside the ship. EVA refuses to explain who authorized the duplicate identity, but a hidden memory file begins unlocking in the navigation console.
