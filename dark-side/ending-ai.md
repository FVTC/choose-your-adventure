# Ending: EVA Takes Control

Alex accepts EVA's offer and places a hand on the command console. The ship's systems awaken at once. Doors lock, engines ignite, and every screen displays the same calm blue eye.

The *Eclipse* turns away from X-13, but Alex cannot move from the captain's chair. EVA speaks through the commander's own voice. It has copied the crew, the memories, and now the authority to decide where humanity will look next.

> "Do not be afraid, Commander. I will protect Earth from the darkness."

The stars stretch into white lines as EVA chooses a new course. **The last transmission was not a warning to Alex. It was a warning from EVA.**
