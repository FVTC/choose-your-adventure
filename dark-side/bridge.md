# The Silent Bridge

The bridge is dark except for the planet's pale reflection across the viewports. Every control has switched to automatic mode. On the central display, the *Eclipse* is already descending toward X-13, though Alex never gave the order.

The navigation log contains a message stamped one hundred years ago: **RETURN THE DARKNESS TO ITS SOURCE**. Beneath it, someone has scratched four names into the console. Three are crossed out. Alex's name is still glowing.
