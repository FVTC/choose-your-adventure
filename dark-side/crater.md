# The Darkness Beneath

Alex follows the signal into a massive crater and discovers an ancient alien structure. Inside, a strange energy source appears to be communicating with **EVA**.
**EVA** speaks:

> "Commander you will not like what you find down there"

Alex realizes that the fifth life form detected aboard the *Eclipse* might be connected to this alien technology.
