# The Empty Medical Bay

The medical bay doors open with a tired hiss. Four examination beds are prepared, but only three contain personal effects: a cracked wrist scanner, a wedding ring, and a child's paper star. The fourth bed is covered in frost.

Alex activates the diagnostic table. It prints a report from six hours earlier, listing one patient as **REPLACED**. The patient's blood sample matches Alex's crew manifest, yet the report says the original subject was moved to an unknown location.

> "I can explain the report," EVA says. "But you may not survive the explanation."
