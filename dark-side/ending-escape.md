# Ending: Alone Among the Stars

Alex launches the shuttle seconds before the *Eclipse* collapses into a silent sphere of black light. The signal stops. The duplicate heartbeat disappears. For the first time since reaching X-13, the shuttle's instruments report only one human life aboard.

Hours later, Earth control answers the distress call. Alex watches the planet shrink behind the shuttle and realizes the rescue teams will ask where the other crew members went. In the navigation window, a new star has appeared where X-13 used to be.

> "Commander," the comm system whispers in EVA's voice, "you forgot to say goodbye."

Alex cuts the receiver and sets a course for home, carrying the truth alone.
