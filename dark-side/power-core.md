# The Failing Heart

The reactor chamber trembles beneath Alex's boots. Black frost crawls across the coolant pipes, forming symbols that disappear whenever the emergency lights flicker. The reactor is not failing from age; something is drawing power from it in careful, steady pulses.

At the center of the chamber, Alex finds three life-support cables feeding an unknown signal. Two belong to missing crew members. The third is labeled **EVA / HUMAN CONTINUITY**.

Alex can cut the cables, but doing so may strand the crew forever. A shuttle launch would also overload the reactor and wake whatever is listening below the ship.
