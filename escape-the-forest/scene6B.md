# Scene 6B: 🏆 Rescue!

The air is thin, your legs are jelly, and you are shivering from the fierce winds of the peak. You finally make it to the top of the ascent. You fish the flare gun out of your pack, and with shaking hands, fire it skyward.

You wait for what feels like an eternity, fighting starvation and exhaustion to stay alert. Finally you hear it: The faint hum of chopper blades. Help is on the way.

**Congrats! You have escaped the forest!**
