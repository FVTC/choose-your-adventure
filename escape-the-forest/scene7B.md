# Scene 7B: ☀ Daylight

This damp miserable cave feels like it stretches on for eternity. How much longer can you endure the darkness and fatigue?

Just when you think you are well and truly lost, a glimmer of light catches your eye. A hallucination? No! That's daylight!

You scramble forward towards the exit as fast as your tired legs can carry you.
