# 🌊 Scene 10: Cross the River

You decide to cross the river although you are already fatiqued. You find a patch of rocks that lead to the otherside that you will use. As you traverse the slippery stones you lose your footing. Your left foot slides into the running water but you manage to catch yourself, but at a cost.

### The Injury
You feel a burn in your right hand as you regain the balance on the rocks. You look down and see a gash in your right hand and leg. The injury looks bad, but you need to cross. After 45 seconds you finally reach the end and lay down in exhaustion. You are very tired.

Do you want to try to find water to help your fatique, find something to use as a bandages or find a spot to nap?
