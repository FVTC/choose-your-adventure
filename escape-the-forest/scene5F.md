# Scene 5F: 🧩 The Cave Maze

You venture carefully into the winding tunnel system under the mountain. The only light to guide you comes from the kerosene lamp you hold. You try to check the paper from the skeleton but you can't make out the markings in the dim lighting.

Following along the walls of the cave, you come upon an opening to another section of cave. Then another. And another! You have a few options on where to go from here. But there's not much fuel left in your lamp, so you better decide fast.
