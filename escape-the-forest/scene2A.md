# Scene 2A: ⛰ The Mountain Pass

As the trail narrows, the dense treeline begins to give way to jagged limestone outcrops. The silence here is heavy, broken only by the whistling wind and the occasional clatter of loose shale underfoot.

### The Ascent
After twenty minutes of climbing, you reach a natural shelf in the rock. To your left, the cliff drops away, offering a dizzying view of the canopy you just escaped. To your right, the path splits again, presenting you with a choice: A seemingly manmade opening carved into the side of the cliff face, or a treacherous climb to higher ground?
