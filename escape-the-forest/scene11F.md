# 🚶‍♂️ Scene 11: Keep Looking

You decided to keep looking. As you stagger on your risky decision pays off quickly. You find a clean looking spring with a small waterfall. You also see clean berries! You drink the water and eat the berries and you feel so replinished. You look around happily and realize this could lead to something bigger.

### Camp Ground
As you think about it, this would be a good place to set up a camp. You look around and see big trees with strong sticks, big bushes with leaves you could use as coverage and the water source and food is right there. It is quickly getting dark and cold though, so you have to decide what to do now.
