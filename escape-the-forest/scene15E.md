# 🚶‍♂️ Scene 15: Keep Walking

You decided to keep walking. But the weight of missing what in that tent is high. You trek foreward and see a nice stream with berries. You eat the berries and drink from the stream to be replinished. You feel good and decide you need to build a hut before the sun comes down.
  
### Hut Building
You build a makeshift axe with sticks, twine and stones. You cut down some trees and lay the wood down. You grab some mud from the stream and insulate the walls with the mud and leaves. You are a very skilled builder. Now that you have a hut, ate food and drank water, you should get some rest.
