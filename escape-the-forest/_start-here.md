# Escape The Forest

### Can you survive the elements?

You knew you shouldn't have gone off the trail alone. You have been walking for hours and now find yourself hopelessly lost in the depths of the forest.

Your cell phone has no charge, not that there would be any signal out here anyway.

Rations are running low, and there are only so many hours of daylight left.

The only thing left to do is survive and escape.
