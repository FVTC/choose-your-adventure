# 🚶‍♂️ Scene 12: Walk Toward it

You choose to walk toward the sounds. Although you are scared, you are confident you made the right choice. As you approach you hear a weird sound. It was a bark!

### Dog Days

You have found a stray dog! As you approach it, it smiles and walks toward you. You pet it. The dog cuddles your leg and accepts you! You made a new friend that will help you survive. You name the dog Fido. Fido will help you through the rest of your adventure. You hear Fido's stomach rumble. The dog is hungry but you are running out of food.

Another big decision. Do you spare them some of your food, go look for food, or get some rest.
