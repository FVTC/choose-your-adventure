# 🌊 Scene 9: Welcome to the River

As the trail drops, you start to hear the river before you see it. Then it’s there—moving steady, cutting through the land. The air feels cooler by the water, and you pause for a second, deciding whether to follow it or just cross the river.

### The Crossing
You stand there a moment, watching the current push past the rocks, steady but a little unpredictable. It’d be easier to just cross and keep moving, but following it might lead somewhere better. You weigh it for a second—the risk of wasting time, the chance of finding something more—and then make your choice.

Which way do you go?
