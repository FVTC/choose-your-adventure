# 🌳 Scene 1: A Fork in The Path

You wander deeper and deeper into the forest. Light streams through the leaves above and twigs snap underfoot as you trudge ahead.

The only sounds you hear are the rustling of the leaves and scattered chirps from the birds. No, there is another sound!

To your right you can just hear the sound of running water. Could that lead the way out of here?

But as you pass through a grouping of trees, you can just make out a rocky outcropping ahead of you. Maybe you can climb the cliffs to get a better view of your surroundings?

Which way do you go?
