# Scene 4A: 🦘 A Leap of Faith

You summon all the strength you can and lunge across the gap, your fingers snapping shut around the coarse canvas just as your boots skid onto the crumbling shale of the far ledge. The momentum nearly pulls you into the abyss, but you slam against the rock face and hug the heavy pack to your chest, gasping for air. 

Behind you, the snapped pine branch disappears into the mist, leaving you shivering and alone, but safe.For now. You check the bag for any useful supplies.

You dig through the pack, finding empty ration packs and a drained waterskin. But there, at the bottom of the pack, something small and orange catches your eye; A flare gun! Need to find higher ground and signal someone with this. You carefully begin making your way around the bend where the path widens to a plateau. 

No sooner have you rounded the corner than you hear a low and angry growl to your left. You've just unwittingly stumbled onto a brown bear's turf, and she does not seem happy to see you. You need to do act fast or you could be her next meal!
