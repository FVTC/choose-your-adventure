# Scene 3A: 👆 The Treacherous Climb

You decide to chance the climb. Maybe from a higher vantage point you can see a way out or signal for help.

Halfway across the traverse, the trail has been washed away by a recent rockslide, leaving a five-foot gap of empty air. But hanging from a gnarled, stubborn pine branch extending over the void is a heavy canvas hiking pack. It’s snagged by a shoulder strap, swaying precariously in the wind. If there are supplies in there, it’s your best shot at survival.

Across the gap, the path continues higher. Above the gap, you spot a cluster of exposed roots you might be able to hold onto.

Grab the roots to support you or make a leap of faith? Either way you'll need that bag if you want to make it out of here.
