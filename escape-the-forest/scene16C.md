# 🏠 Scene 16: Go Back to Camp

You decide to go back to your camp. The risk of that far of a walk with no food or water was too much. You get back to camp and look down at your dirty clothes and muddy body. You need to wash off. 

### Washing Off
After some consideration, you realize the only way to wash off is by going in the stream. You get ready and walk into the stream.
