# 🔥 Scene 12: Make a Fire

You want to start a fire. you go to the big tree and find some dead sticks around it. You also grab some of the dried leaves from the tree. You walk over to the water and find a nice stone so you can make the fire. You use your hands to rorate the stick quickly on the stone and you make the fire. You are happy, you have a light source and warmth.
  
### A New Companion

All of the sudden you hear leaves crunching. Someone or something is walking toward the fire. You can't see it, it is too far away. You are scared but also curious...

Do you want to walk toward it, hide or make a weapon?
