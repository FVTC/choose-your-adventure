# 🔒 Scene 12: Hide

You decide to hide. You don't want to risk putting yourself in danger. You go to the big tree and hide in the low hanging leaves. After a few minutes, the footsteps turn around and walk away. Relief fills your body, you get out of your hiding spot and step on something. It's a flashlight! You click the button and it turns on. This is big.

### New Item Aqquired
With the flashlight you look around where you are at. You can see at night! This opens a worl of oppurtunity for you. As the night goes on you start to feel exhausted but don't have anywhere to sleep.
