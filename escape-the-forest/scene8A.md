# Scene 8A: 🏆 Escape!

You stumble out of the cave and take several deep breaths of fresh air as relief fills your body. The light stings your eyes after what feels like an eternity in that underground hellscape. You finally made it.

You have no idea how long you were in there or how far you travelled, but you find yourself on the other side of the mountain, still mostly in one piece.

What's even better than that, you can hear the distant sounds of civilization; You never thought you'd be this happy to hear the rumble of highway traffic. 

**Congrats! You have escaped the forest!**
