# 😴 Scene 16: Get Some Rest

You wake up, energized and excited to take on the day. You use the axe you made yesterday to hunt animals for food. Now that you have a consistent food and water source, your morale is very high. Not sure what to do now, you decide you need to walk somewhere to try and find help.
  
### Help Seeking
As you walk, you see smoke arising from the air miles away. You are still close to camp and don't want to lose it, but this is a sign of life. Your food is at your hut and you don't know if there will be clean water on the way.

Do you want to walk toward the smoke or go back to camp?
