# 💧 Scene 10: Follow the River

You weighed the descion and came to the conclusion that crossing the river was too risky. The water was running too fast and the rocks were too slippery. As you trek foreward you begin to feel hunger and thirst. You think about drinking the river water but it looks dirty. You should find a fresh spring. There could be sources of food there too.
  
### Locate a Fresh Spring
As you keep walking to locate fresh water and food, the hunger and thirst worsens. You start to worry, it is getting late. You begin to lose hope but you see a waterfall in the horizon. You stagger over to it. A small pond surrounded by trees with glowing berries. You jump for joy and rush over. The water looks sketchy but you are very thirsty. You now have a desicion to make.

Do you eat the berries and drink the water, just drink the water or keep looking for a cleaner pond with better food.
