# Scene 4D: 🕯 Stop and Explore

You decide that you need to gear up before you face the dark depths of the caves. Exploring the outside of the cave doesn't turn up anything useful.

You venture closer to the cave entrance, and carefully feel around the dark for something that could help. You run your hands along the wall, desperate for supplies. Finally, your fingers graze something dry and brittle, and you carefully take hold of it so you can drag it into the light.

You suppress your dread as you realize the thing you just dragged into the light is a human skeleton!

Once you stop the growing feeling of sick in your stomach, you notice the skeleton has managed to hold onto two items.

In one hand is a kerosene lamp with barely any oil left in it. In the other is a rolled up bit of old paper. You whisper a quick apology to the poor soul in front of you before prying both free.

You unfurl the paper and see a strange diagram on it:

<pre>
     |     |
 ➡  |  ⬆  |
_____|_____|_____
     |     |
  ⬆  |  ⬅ |
_____|_____|_____
     |     |
     |  ⬆  |
     |     |
</pre>

Some sort of map?

You glance towards the menacing cave entrance and suppose you'll find out one way or another. Light your lamp and venture forth.
