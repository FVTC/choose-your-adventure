# ENDING: ETERNAL REST
You head toward the chair near the fireplace.
Perhaps you could push it into the fire?
Maybe you could hide behind it.
Or maybe, you could take a cozy rest on the chair. You did do quite a bit of walking, haven't you?

Perhaps a few hours wouldn't hurt.
..  yet, those hours turn to days.
Days to weeks.
Weeks to ..  well, time ticks for so long that there's no telling how long it's actually been.
Your slumber continues on soundly, and it doesn't look like you'll be waking up anytime soon.
