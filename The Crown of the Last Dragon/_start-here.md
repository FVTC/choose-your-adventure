# The Crown of the Last Dragon

The kingdom of Eldoria is in danger.

The legendary Crown of the Last Dragon has been stolen from the royal vault, and the magical barrier protecting the land is beginning to crumble.

King Aldric offers a reward to anyone brave enough to recover it.

As a young adventurer standing within the capital city, you must decide where your investigation begins.
