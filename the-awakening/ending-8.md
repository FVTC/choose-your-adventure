# :ocean: The Winding Ride :ocean:

</div>

Once you slowly walk into the river and close your eyes, you fall back and let the rushing water take you downstream. The water feels quite refreshing. The rapids begin to intensify after a minute. You grab onto a large piece of driftwood and hold on for dear life. After five more minutes you realize that there is no longer a current. 

As you take in your surroundings, you see that you are now in a calm lake. You begin to gaze around the lakeshore for any sign of civilization. That’s when you see it – that same dreadful cabin.
