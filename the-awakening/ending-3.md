# :skull: Underwater :skull:

</div>

You bend down closer to the water but can't see anything because it is so murky. As you start to stand up, the water suddenly has a ripple right in front of you. You freeze up and out of the water comes a pair of decaying hands. They come out so quickly that you have no time to react, and you are pulled under the water before you have a chance to scream. The water returns to a calm, foggy silence, bringing your journey to a sudden end.
