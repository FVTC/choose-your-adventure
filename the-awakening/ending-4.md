# :question: The Strange Ending :question:

</div>

As you get closer to this noise, you begin to hear grunting and more leaves rustling. You try to hide behind a tree to get a closer look without being seen, but it’s too late. You’ve been caught! “Hello traveler.” A stranger’s voice calls out. You realize that the source of the noise is a group of old hippies. 

It seems that they were performing some kind of meditation exercise that required them to roll around in the leaves. You wait for them to finish their activities before you hitch a ride to the nearest town and call a loved one to come pick you up.
