# :raccoon: A Rabid Ending :raccoon:

</div>

Walking along the river, you begin to finally feel a sense of peace. The sound of the rushing water just happens to be so calming. Just as you feel your nervous system resetting, you hear a growl. You look towards the alarming sound to find a rabid raccoon just a few feet from you. You begin to run, but trip on a tree root. As you look up from the ground, the raccoon launched itself towards your face. You scream, and jolt awake. You thank the universe that it was all a nightmare, roll over, and quickly fall back asleep.
