# :seedling: The Awakening :seedling:

</div>

You wake up groggy. It takes you just an instant to realize that you are no longer in the warm comfort of your bed. You suddenly realize that you are in a strange and dusty log cabin. You get up from the cold floor and walk to a dirty window. There appears to be a large field with overgrown foliage that is then met with a dense tree line that expands as far as the eye can see. No driveway. No mailbox. No one in sight.
