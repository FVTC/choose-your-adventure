# Path 1, Day 1 - Water Focus

Approaching the water, it's a beautiful, almost crystal clear, glistening water. You were expecting murky and muddy - better than nothing, after all.

You feel a force, no, a need to touch the water. Just to feel it. Walk closer. Touch the water. One little touch is all...

Coming up to the edge, you kneel down and... you tense up. The water feels comforting. Too comforting? It's an odd feeling. It felt like something walked next to you, or... through you? You're starting to feel a little uneasy.

You figure it's time to head back to the house. As you're walking back, you feel like the water is almost... part of you. You can sense its location behind you, its temperature, its motion... I'm sure it's nothing.

As you step inside the house, the atmosphere feels different from the outside. There's a presence, but it refuses to acknowledge you. Pressing on, it's awfully dusty in here. Better get to work!
