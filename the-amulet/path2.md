# Path 2, Day 1 - Earth Focus

As you walk around the house, the hills greet you with a beautiful sunset. It's captivating. Walking towards the hills, you feel comforted, surrounded by a warmth, embraced by the air. Wait... not the air. The ground.

You feel the urge... no, the need to lay down. As you press against the dirt, it feels like something is projecting through your body from the ground - like something reaching through you. It's almost as if the grass is wrapping around you...

As you lay here, you remember what you came here for. You're not here to lay in the grass, even though it is nice. You're here to work on your new house (and now you need to figure out what just happened.)

Standing up, you feel your senses return to you fully. The feeling you had when you were laying down stuck with you. You should probably head back towards the house.

As you step inside the house, the atmosphere feels different from the outside. There's a presence, but it refuses to acknowledge you. Pressing on, it's awfully dusty in here. Better get to work!

*From now on, if there is a path with a 🪨 icon, you must take it.*
