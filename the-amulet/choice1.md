# Your first choice - Arrival

You won the auction! ...that no one else participated in. You didn't even need more than $5,000 of the money you saved for this vacation.

As you claim your winnings, the auctioneer hands you the deed and a slip of paper with the houses address, a short 5 hour drive south. He whispers something to you as you grab the deed.

**"You sure know what you're doing? I've heard some creepy stuff about this place. Just be careful, there's a reason you were the only one that bid on it."**

A chill runs down your back as he says these words - maybe you've gotten in too deep...

No. You're committed - you head home, pack your bags, and prepare to move in to this wonderful vacation home you've bought with confidence!

Arriving at the home, you notice it's quite a large building for $5,000 - nearly double the size of some houses you've seen around your hometown. The view from the yard is incredible... Rolling hills behind your house, a small lake to the right, part of the property, too!

Where do you want to go first?
