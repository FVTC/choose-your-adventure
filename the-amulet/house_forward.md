# Air - Center of the House

As you bring yourself to the center of the stairs, the air around you responds to you.

Pushing through the door in front of you, a pillar sits nicely in the center of the room. It has a depression of a handprint in the center. You are pushed towards it by the wind. You feel like the room is asking you to place your hand on the pillar.
