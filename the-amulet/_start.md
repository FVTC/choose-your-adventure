
# The Amulet

### Background

You are a hardworking person who's been saving vacation days. As you come up on one whole month of paid vacation, you realize you don't know where you want to stay.

You do some research into new houses - cheap ones. You want a vacation home. In a nice clearing, maybe a body of water nearby, something similar.

As your research comes to a close, you hear about this old wooden cabin smack in the middle of nowhere. It needs lots of touching up - it hasn't been inhabited in a while.

You're up for a bit more work to have a nice place all to yourself, right?
