# The Crystal of Aeloria

The kingdom of Aeloria is dying.

Its heart — the sacred Crystal of Balance — has been stolen.  
The Queen calls upon two heroes to retrieve it before darkness consumes the realm.

> "The thief has vanished beyond our borders," she says gravely.  
> "You must choose your path wisely — one by land, one by sea."
