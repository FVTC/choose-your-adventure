# You cast *Invisibilty*

These kobolds can't stop you from entering the cave if they can't see. You cast *Invisibility* to turn yourself invisible. Your disappearance startles the kobolds.
> Where'd she go? Spread out and find her. We can't let her reach Rundrirth!

You enter the cave. As you progress seeper into the cave, the floor and walls slowly transition from rock to ice. You must be getting close.

---

The cave reaches a big open area. Inside is a big pile of gold, silver, and other valuable treasures. In front of the treasure, a huge, **Adult White Dragon** slumbers. You attempt to approach quietly to gain an advantage. Unbeknownst to you, however, an *Alarm* spell was set up on the entryway, which triggers when you enter the open area. The *Alarm* wakes Rundrirth, who begins searching the area for you.
> Where are you, intruder? I know you're here. Akvir's magic doesn't lie. And neither does my sense of smell.
