# "I am Gwyndolin, Gwyndolin Dragonfang"

The Inn fell slient with the sound of your last name and a male voice spoke.
>Dragonfang? As in Knight Dragonfang?

Looking around for the person who spoke.
>Yes?

Around the Inn you notice all the patrons whispering to others and looking away when you stare at them. The old lady cackles.
>Of course! This is the daughter of Sir Dragonfang, Knight of the DragonBlight.

Who is this woman? How does she know so much about you and your father? He was just a Knight wasn't he?
>You know of who I am, but I know nothing of who you are. Who are you?

She steps closer and nodes her head low. The other patrons of the Inn start to fade into different converstations.
>I am Myrrine the Wise. I am an Seer of the future. As you fell I saw your fate.

You step back but the old woman approaches you closer and leans in and whispers.
>Gwyndolin Dragonfang. You must accept your destiny and redeem your family bloodline. You must! There is no other choice.
