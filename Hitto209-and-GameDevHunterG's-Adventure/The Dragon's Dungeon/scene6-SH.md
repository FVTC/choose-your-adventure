# It's time to go to the mountains

With the threat of Karl taken care of, you decide that it's time to go to the mountains. The old woman approaches you.
> Thank you, Sabtha. I believe I have yet to properly introduce myself. My name is Myrrine the Wise. I am a seer.

A seer?! As in someone who can see the future? If she can truly see the future, surely what Myrrine knows can be helpful in your quest. Myrrine, seemingly able to sense your thoughts, says this:
> And before you ask, no, I do not have a fortune to tell to you. At this moment in time, your fate is entirely in your hands.

You decide to ask Myrrine about the mountains.
> You may not be able to tell me the future, but could you perhaps tell me how to get to the mountains from here?

Myrrine smiles.
> Of course. The mountains are just a little ways southeast of here. On foot, it would take about 12 hours. I can provide you with a horse if you want, which would cut the time down to 6 hours. Or I can give you a sketch of a teleportation circle up there. Be warned, however, that the teleportation circle is much more perilous.
