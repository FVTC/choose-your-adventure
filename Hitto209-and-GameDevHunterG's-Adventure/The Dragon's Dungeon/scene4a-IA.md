# You decide to fight Karl

You look around for a place to hide. After all, you're severely outnumbered. You have to play it smart if you want to survive. You find a rooftop that Karl's army doesn't seem to be watching very closely. That makes it the perfect hiding place.

You manage to scale the building with relative ease and minimal attention to yourself. You pull out your shortbow and notch an arrow. You'll only have one shot at this. If Karl is still standing after you fire your arrow, the goblins will surely focus their full force on your location.

After taking a second to ready yourself for what's to come, you emerge from your hiding spot, pull back the drawstring on your bow, and fire the arrow. Your arrow flies true and fells its intended target. You quickly hide so the remaining goblins don't see you.

After a few seconds of silence, you hear panicked screaming. Only this time, the screaming is coming from the goblins instead of the townspeople. You look to where the goblins were and see them fleeing.
