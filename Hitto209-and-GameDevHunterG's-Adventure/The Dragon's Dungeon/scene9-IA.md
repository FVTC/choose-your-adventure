# You arrive at the base of the mountain.

You know that Windrunner can't climb the mountain, so you find a tree and tie him to it. You then begin climbing the mountain.

---

As you near the top of the mountain, you see a cave. Standing outside the entrance to the cave, is a group of kobolds blocking the entrance. With the kobolds there, you reason that this is probably the dragon's lair. You need to figure out how to get into that cave.
