# Better safe than sorry

You don't like the idea of taking 6 hours just to reach the base of the mountain, but if the teleportation circle is more perilous, you'd rather take the safer option. After all, you can't slay a dragon if you're dead.
> I think I'll go by horse.

Myrrine guides you to the stables.
> Pick a horse.

You choose a black horse with white spots. The horse is outfitted with a saddle. You are asked if you would like to name your horse.
> Rylnel. That name will always have sentimental value to me.

You get yourself seated in the saddle, and you and Rylnel ride south towards the mountains.

---

About 3 hours into the journey, you come across a broken bridge that goes across a 60 foot wide river. The river flows swiftly. There's no way you'd be able to swim across. You need to figure out a different way across.
