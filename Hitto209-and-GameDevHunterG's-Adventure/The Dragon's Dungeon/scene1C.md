# "I am Sabtha Hunzrar."
The woman looks at you, then asks a question:
> Well, are you alright, Sabtha?

You sit there for a few seconds before answering.
> I'm a little confused about what's going on. Why is everyone staring at me? And why do I feel like I just fell from the sky?

The woman and the rest of the crowd around you look even more concerned than they did before. Finally, the woman answers your questions.
> Sabtha Hunzrar, the reason you feel like you fell from the sky is because that's exactly what we saw happen to you? Do you really not remember?

You are shocked. Of course you don't remember. The last thing you remember is setting up camp in the mountains. But why were you in the mountains?
## Roll a DC (Difficulty Class) 10 history check

> [!NOTE]
> In the most recent edition of Dungeons and Dragons the rule for ability checks, saving throws, and attack rolls is that you succeed if your result is greater than or equal to the DC (AC if it's an attack roll). To avoid confusion, that is the rule we will be using in *The Dragon's Dungeon.*
