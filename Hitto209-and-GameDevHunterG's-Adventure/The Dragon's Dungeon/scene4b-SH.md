# You decide to try to negotiate with Karl

You apporach Karl slowly. The archers aim their bows at you. Karl speaks to you:
> Stay back, elf! Or else you'll be turned into a pin cushion for arrows!

You stop moving.
> No need for the aggression. I just want to talk.

Karl raises his hand; the bows are lowered.
> Talk? About what?

You point to your spellbook.
>About that. That book belongs to me. It's a spellbook. I need it back.

Karl laughs.
> I'll make you a deal, elf. I'll give you this spellbook if you stand back and let me conquer this village. I need the resources to protect my people from the dragon up in the mountains.

Did he say dragon? You can't, in good conscience, accept Karl's terms. However, assuming he's being honest about why he wants the village, you can think of a pretty good counteroffer.
> I can't give you the village. However, I was on my way to the mountains to go slay the dragon you speak of. However, I can't do that without my spellbook. If you return it to me, I will slay this dragon, and you will no longer need to worry about it.
