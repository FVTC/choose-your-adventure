# Your arrow hits Rundrirth

You fire your arrow, striking down Rundrirth with a single blow. As the arrow pierces the dragon scales, Rundrirth lets out this loud roar and attempts to fly away before falling from the air. With the last of his strength, he says:
> Where did that arrow come from? I suppose it doesn't matter now. Wondering where the arrow came from won't change my fate. The time for me to be reunited with Tiamat is at hand.

You wait a minute before lowering your bow, just in case Rundrirth is laying a trap for you. Then you exitthe cave to see the kobolds still attack the illusory Karl you created to get in. You head back down the mountain and return to the village to tell them of your victory.
