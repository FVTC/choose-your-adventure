# Wait a minute...

You realize why this seems odd. You notice small details in the traveler's voice and body language that clearly indicate one truth. ***He's lying.*** Now that you're aware of the lies, you notice movement in the bushes. It's an ambush! You fire an arrow, which hits the ground inches from the fake traveler's head. You knew it would miss. It was a warning shot.
> If you and your fellow bandits don't get out of here, the next arrow won't miss!

The bandit runs away, and you see 39 additional bandits follow behind him from out of the bushes.
