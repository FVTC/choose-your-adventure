# Who builds a wizard tower on top of a mountain?!

Hopefully the owner of this tower isn't home at the moment and you can just leave without having to awkwardly explain how you ended but here.

***creak***

Or, maybe not. You hear the sound of a door being opened. A tiefling enters the room. By his garb, you can tell that he is the wizard that this tower belongs to. He is looking through his spellbook when he looks up and notices you.
> Hey! What are you doing in my tower?

This is exactly what you were afraid of. Now you have to explain what how you got here and why you are here in the first place.
>I'm so sorry. I recieved a sketch of your teleportation circle from this old lady. I had no idea that it was in a wizard's tower! Please excuse me. I really must get going. I have to slay a dragon that's made its lair somewhere in this mountain.

You start walking toward the door when suddenly, you slam into something. A *Wall of Force*! The tiefling starts cackling.
> Sorry, miss dragon slayer. But Rundrirth and I have a mutually beneficial relationship. He keeps my tower in a place of solitude, and I deal with any pesky adventurers who seek to slay him for his actions.

The tiefling wizard casts *Ray of Frost*, which misses. You realize that you'll have to fight your way out of this one.
