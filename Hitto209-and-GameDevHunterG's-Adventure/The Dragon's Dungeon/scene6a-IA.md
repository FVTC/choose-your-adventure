# Your curious about the future

> What's this vision you wish to tell me about?

Dark clouds roll in, blocking out the sun. The crackle of lightning fills the air. And Myrrine's eyes glow with the light of a thousand stars as she speaks.
> Two fates await you on the road you shall travel. One of victory, and one of death. Which fate is yours will be determined by your choices. Remember that not all obstacles can be passed with your bow.

Both the sky and Myrrine's eyes return to normal. You expected a little more ***information*** with your glimpse of the future. But nevertheless, you still have a dragon to slay.
> Myrrine, do you know how I can get to the moutains from here?

Myrrine replies:
> Of course. The mountains are just 12 hours south of here when traveling on foot. I can provide you with a horse, if you'd like. That would cut the travel time in half.
