# Then you see

A dragon coming at you full force through the trees.

With the wind slapping at your face the dragon lands and speaks:
> Who are you?

Confused you reply:
> Who are you and why are you in my fathers grave yard? Are you the cause for all this mess? 

The Dragon gets irritated but answers:
>I am Rundrirth. I ahve some here to claim a body that tried to slay me many years ago. Are you the Keeper of these grounds?

You sigh. 
> No I am Gwyndolin, Gwyndolin Dragonfang. My father was burried here.

Rundrirth gets angry and yells:
DRAGONFANG? 

Rundrirth laughs.
He has a daughter... Well Had.

> [!NOTE]
> Rundrirth has an AC of 18. In order to win this fight, you'll need to hit him with 3 of the 5 attack rolls. Critcal hits (Natural 20) count as two hits

> [!TIP]
> Gwyndolin has a +4 modifer to spell attack rolls. So the final result of each attack roll is the number on the D20 plus 4.
