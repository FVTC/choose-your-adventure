# One kobold down

You decide to attack the kobolds head on. You fire an arrow, bringing down on of the kobolds in a single blow. What you couldn't account for, however, was enchantments placed on the kobolds that activates the spell *Shatter* upon their deaths, which triggers an avalanche.
