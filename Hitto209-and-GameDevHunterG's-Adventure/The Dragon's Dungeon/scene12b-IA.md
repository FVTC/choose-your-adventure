# Of all the shots to miss...

Your arrow flies over Rundrirth and into the treasure pile, shattering a ceramic vase. The noise wakes Rundrirth. Unfortunately, you have nowhere to hide in this cave, and Rundrirth spots you after a quick look around.
> What a noisy dragonborn. I was just enjoying a peaceful nap, and you had to disturb it. Even worse, you broke one of my most valued treasures with your racket. Let me show you what happens to people who destroy part of a dragon's hoard.

Rundrirth opens his mouth and unleashes his breath weapon. When the mini blizzard subsides, you are completely encased in a massive block of ice.
