# You didn't hit Karl hard enough

You thought you had him. But after you hit Karl with your staff, he was still standing. You bet everything on that hit. And now it's all over. Karl turns to the closest goblin to you and gives the order:
> Don't just stand there! This elf just attacked your king! Kill her!

And so, you are brought down by a bunch of goblins. And the last thing you see is the hoard of goblins overrunning the village and burning the whole things to the ground.
