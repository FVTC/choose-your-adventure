# You decide to play to your strengths

In a duel between wizards, it is not always the one with the most powerful evocation spells who wins. It is more often the smarter wizard who comes out on top. You decide that the best way to survive this encounter is to make use of your skill with illusion magic. You start by casting *Mirror Image*. This gives you an opening to get a few damaging cantrips out there while being protected from your opponents evocation magic.

When the last of you *Mirror Image* doubles disappears, you cast *Mislead*, turning yourself invisible while an illusory double takes the attention off of you. You use this opprotunity to sneak up behind your opponent, who remains unaware that the you that he is fighting is merely an illusion until you have to drop concentration on *Mislead* to be able to cast *Shadow Blade*. But by then, you've already struck the final blow.
