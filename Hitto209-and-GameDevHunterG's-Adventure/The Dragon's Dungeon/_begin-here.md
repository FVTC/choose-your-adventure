# 💡 WELCOME! 

## ➕ How to Play


    To play, simply click on the links provided in each scene to navigate through
 the story. When you encounter a choice that requires a dice roll, follow the
 instructions provided to determine the outcome of your action. The story 
 will branch based on your choices and the results of your dice rolls, 
 leading you to different paths and endings.

    The d8 will determine the damage dealt, the d20 will determine the success of 
 the action performed (Strength, Intelligence, Charisma... and so forth). If you have personal dice feel free to use them, if not 
 you can use an online dice roller like [this one](https://gamedevhunterg.github.io/dice.html). 
 To get the dice you would like, click on the Dice. To get rid of the dice, right click and it will disappear.
