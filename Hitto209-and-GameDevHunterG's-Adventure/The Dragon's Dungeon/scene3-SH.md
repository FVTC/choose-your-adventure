# You can't find your spellbook

This is not good. You need your spellbook to be able to fight a dragon. None of the spells you currently have prepared are useful in a fight.
> Do you know where my spellbook is?

The woman looks confused.
> No. There were no books near you when you were found.

That's not a comforting thing to hear when you're missing your spellbook.

## You hear screams coming from the north

You go to check it out. What you find is townspeople running from a hoard of goblins with scimitars and bows drawn. Standing on the shoulders of one of the other goblins is this one goblin wearing a crown and wielding a longsword in one hand, and holding a spellbook in the other. Wait a minute... that's YOUR spellbook!

The goblin with the crown speaks:
> Run, you filthy peasants! This village now belongs to me, Karl, king of the goblins!

You have to do something. You can't let these people lose their homes, and you can't let this self-proclaimed king keep your spellbook. But how should you proceed?
