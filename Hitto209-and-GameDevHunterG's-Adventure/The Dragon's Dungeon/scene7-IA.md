# Myrrine takes you to the stables

You are told to pick a horse. You pick a brown horse white patches of white around its eyes. You are asked what you would like to name your horse.
> His name shall be Windrunner! With a fast-sounding name, he will surely be fast.

You are seated in the saddle and ride off toward the mountains.

---

About 2 hours into your journey, you see a traveler lying on the ground, seemingly injured.
> Help me! I was attacked by an ogre and left for dead! I need someone to come over here and help me get to the village!

Something about this seems odd to you.
