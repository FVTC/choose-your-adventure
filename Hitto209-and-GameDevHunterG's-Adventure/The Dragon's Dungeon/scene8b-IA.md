# Probably just your imagination

Perhaps you're just overthinking things. You're still on edge after Karl's attack on the village and you're currently on your way to fight a dragon. You approach the injured traveler.

But the injured traveler isn't injured ***OR*** a traveler. He's a bandit putting on an act to lure unsuspecting travelers into a trap. And you just sprung that trap. When you approach, the bandit stops pretending to be injured and casts *Hold Person*. 39 additional bandits emerge from the bushes and attack you while your paralyzed and unable to defend yourself.
