# I have an army

You decide to use magic to trick Karl into fleeing. This attack of his is out of character for him, so you would rather resolve this with as little bloodshed as possible. While out of Karl's line of sight, you cast *Silent Image* to create the illusion of a platoon of archers.

You march out into the open with you illusory army and prepare to shoot an arrow at Karl. But first, you give hime a chance to flee.
> Karl! Turn back. I have 10,000 archers under my command and they have you surrounded. This is your first and final warning!

One of the goblins fires an arrow that hits you square in the shoulder. The blow causes you to lose concentration on *Silent Image*. Karl laughs.
> So much for that army of yours! Finish him off!
