# Karl has agreed to your terms

Karl steps down from the shoulders of the goblin he was standing on and walks over to you.
> I accept your terms. Take your spellbook and find that dragon. We're counting on you.

You take your spellbook back from Karl and the goblins leave. The woman approaches you.
> Aren't you worried that they'll come back.

Your counteroffer was made in the hopes that Karl was sincere when he said he wanted the village for the resources to defend his people against the dragon. So you respond as follows:
> No. He said he wanted needed the resources this village provides to protect his people from the dragon. If he was lying about that, he'd have no reason to part with my spellbook and trust that I'll keep my end of the bargain.
