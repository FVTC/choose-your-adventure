# I believe I can fly

That river is very wide. You don't know how far Rylnel can jump. It's best to play it safe. You send Rylnel forward. When you reach the edge of the bridge, you cast *Fly* on Rylnel. You successfully fly over the river.

---

You reach the base of the mountain. You know Rylnel won't be able to climb the mountain with you. You get down from the saddle and tie Rylnel to a nearby tree.
> Don't worry, Rylnel. I'll come back for you. I just need to take care of something on the top of this mountain quickly.

You begin your ascent up the mountain.
