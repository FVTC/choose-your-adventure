# Teleportation is much quicker

You decide that you want to teleport. 6 hours is quite a long time, and that's just to get to the base of the mountain. And when dealing with dragons, the less time you take, the better.
> Myrrine, do you think I could see that teleportation circle?

Myrrine digs through a pouch she's been carrying this whole timen and pulls out a piece of parchment. She hands it to you, saying:
> Good luck. Given my knowledge of where this particular circle is located, you'll need it.

More than a little ominous, but okay. You take the parchment and study it. Once you have looked at the parchment for a minute, you begin the process of casting *Teleportation Circle*.

---

When the spell finishes, you vanish from the village and reappear in a small room in a stone building. You look out the window and see the snow-covered mountaintop. The very top of the mountain is level with the top of the window. You gasp in horror when you realize where you are.
