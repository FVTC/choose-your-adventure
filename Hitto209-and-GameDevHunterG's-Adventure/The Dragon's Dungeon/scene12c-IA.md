# You withstand the blizzard

Is it cold? Yes. Will you be able to survive it? Also yes. While Rundrirth continues trying to freeze you with his breath, while ready an arrow and fire it right into his mouth. For a few seconds, it seems as if it does nothing. But then, Rundrirth stops breathing ice and collapses. With the last of his strength, he speaks to you.
> Impressive shot, dragonborn. It was a one in a million shot.

When Rundrirth stops moving, you move towards the cave entrance you came from, only lowering your bow when Rundrirth is out of your sight.

When you reach the cave entrance, the kobolds are still attackiing the illusory Karl. You leave to it and climb down the mountain to return to the village to tell them of your victory.
