# You passed the history check

You remember why you were in the mountains.

The group you've been traveling with was hired by the local nobility to slay a dragon that's been terrorizing the area. You went to the mountains, believing that the dragon's lair is somewhere around there. You relay this information to the people around you.

The woman replies:
> Ah, I see. Given what you've told us, I suspect that the dragon carried you off in the dead of night and then dropped you here.

As farfetched as that explanation sounds, no other explanation that you can think of even seems possible. Nevertheless, you still have a job to do.

As you are about to ask for directions to the mountains, you feel like something is wrong. Then you realize that's because something IS wrong.
