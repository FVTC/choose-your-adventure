# You're about to leave the tower

Before you can touch the door, you hear the sound of wings flapping in the wind. Large wings. Dragon wings. This must be the "Rundrirth" that the wizard you just fought was talking about. You hear a booming, draconic voice:
> Akvir, is everything alright?

Akvir? You've never heard that name before. That must be the wizard this tower belonged to. But how did Rundrirth know that something happened here? Then it hits you. There was a 6 second period where Akvir didn't attack you at all, which seemed odd at the time. You now realize that he was casting *Sending* during that time. You exit the tower. Rundrirth looks at you.
> You're not Akvir. Who are you?

You reply:
> I am Sabtha Hunzrar. I defeated Akvir in a duel. This tower is now mine, as is your lair within this mountain. You are to stop terrorizing this region at once!

Rundrirth laughs for a solid 12 seconds before speaking:
> No.

Rundrirth uses his breath weapon.
