# You decide to jump the river

It seems like a risky idea, but the alternative is using a spell slot to cast *Fly*. You're going to need as many spell slots as you can when you go to fight the dragon. So you decide to jump the river.

You walk Rylnel back to 60 feet before the bridge. On your command, Rylnel runs toward the bridge and jumps when he reaches the edge. Unfortunately, Rylnel is not able to jump far enough, and both of you fall into the river and are swept away by the current.
