# You decide to try to sneak past the kobolds.

You cast *Minor Illusion* at a spot that's 40 feet away from the cave entrance. The illusion takes the form of Karl. The kobolds notice this.
> Hey, goblin! Turn back now! Rundrirth wants no visitors!

When illusory Karl doesn't move, the kobolds apporoach him with their spears ready to attack. You use this as an opportunity to slip by unnoticed.

---

As you go deeper into the cave, the rock slower turns to ice. You must be on the right track. The cave opens up, and you see a massive pile of gold, silver, and other valuables. In front of this treasure is a sleeping **Adult White Dragon**. That must be the "Rundrirth" that the kobolds mentioned. If can you avoid waking him up, you might be able to complete your quest with minimal danger to yourself.

## Roll a DC 25 Stealth check

> [!TIP]
> You can use the [dice roller](https://gamedevhunterg.github.io/dice.html) on the companion site if you don't have dice.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 25 may look like a high number to beat, but Iarath has a +11 modifier for stealth. Therefore, the final result will be the number rolled on the D20 plus 11.
