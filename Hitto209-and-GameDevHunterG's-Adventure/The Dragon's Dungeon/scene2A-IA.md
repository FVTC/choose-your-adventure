# You passed the history check

You remember what you were doing in the mountains. You and the group you are usually with were hired by the local nobility to slay a dragon that's been terrorizing the region. You believe that the dragon's lair is located somewhere in the mountains, which is why you were up there. Remembering that you were asked a question, you answer:
> No, I don't remember falling from the sky. The last thing I remember is setting up camp for the night up in the mountains while searching for a dragon's lair.

The townspeople stare at you for a second before the woman speaks again.
> Ah. I should have known you were here to save us from the dragon. Let us know if there is anything you need from us to help you on your journey.

Before you can ask for directions to the mountains, your ears are filled with the piercing sounds of terrified screams coming from the north.
