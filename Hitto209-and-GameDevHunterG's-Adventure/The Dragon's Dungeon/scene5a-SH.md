# You defeated Karl!

Your staff makes contact with Karl. The impact is so hard that he goes flying, but not before he drops your spellbook. You catch the spellbook with your right hand right as Karl hits the ground. When the other goblins realize that their king is dead, they point their weapons at you. You have your spellbook now, so you can follow through on what you say next:
> Surrender, or share your king's fate!

The remaining goblins drop their weapons and surrender. They then run away. A few of the farmers grab pitchforks and attempt to follow them, but the woman stops them.
> Let them flee. Goblins are a superstitious bunch. Now that their king has been felled here, they will consider this place cursed. We won't have to worry about them anymore.

Karl has been defeated and the people of this village no longer have to worry about those goblins, but your work is still incomplete. There is a dragon that you still need to slay.
