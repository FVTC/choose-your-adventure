# You decide to attack Karl with your staff

You walk toward Karl with your staff in hand. He decides to try and taunt you:
> It looks as if we have a challenger to my power. Beat it, elf. Or do you not know what's good for you?

You reply:
> Do you?

You proceed to swing your staff with both hands, casting true strike to make the blow hit harder. You hit Karl.

---
## Roll for damage
> [!WARNING]
> Because you are severly outnumbered, if you do not defeat Karl with this attack, you will surely be killed. Karl has 10 hit points.

> [!NOTE]
> You can the D8 on the [dice roller](https://gamedevhunterg.github.io/dice.html) on the companion site if you do not have dice.

> [!TIP]
> Because Sabtha cast True Strike before attacking, the ability modifier that's added to the damage is her intelligence modifier of +4. So the total damage you will deal is the number you rolled plus 4.
