# Not quite enough

You hit Rundrirth with your staff. As the staff makes contact, Rundrirth does a barrel roll. Since you needed both hands to hit with your staff as hard aas you did, you have no way to hold on. You fall off Rundrirth. As you fall, you see Rundrirth dive bomb the village and unleash his breath weapon.
