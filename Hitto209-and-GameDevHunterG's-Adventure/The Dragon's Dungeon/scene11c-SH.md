# You passed the saving throw

The breath weapon is ***REALLY*** cold! But you manage to keep yourself warm enough to survive and launch a counter attack.

You cast *Misty Step* and land on Rundrirth's back. He flies wildly toward the village to try to make you fall off. But you manage to hold on. You cast *Mirror Image* so protect yourself from Rundrirth's attempts to throw you off. As you fly through the air, you cast *Shadow Blade* and use the sword to weaken Rundrirth.

While you are hitting Rundrirth with your sword, he manages to break through your *Mirror Image* and break you concentration. Your sword vanishes. But your foe is weak. You can defeat him with a single blow from your staff. You grab your staff and swing downward, casting *True Strike* mid-swing. The staff connects. It's a critical hit!

# Roll for damage

> [!WARNING]
> If this attack isn't enough to finish off Rundrirth, you will be thrown off. Rundrirth has 12 HP remaining.

> [!NOTE]
> You can use the [dice roller](https://gamedevhunterg.github.io/dice.html) on the companion site if you don't have dice.

> [!TIP]
> Sabtha used *True Strike* before attacking, so her +4 intelligence modifier is added to the damage.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Because this is a critical hit, Sabtha's staff will deal 2d8 damage instead of 1d8. Remember to roll the d8 twice.
