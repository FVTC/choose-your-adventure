>WAIT! I HAVE SEEN THE FUTURE! You will hear me, I know you want to hear about your father! 

You step out of the Inn and she is still trying to catch up to you. You keep walking. 
>Gwyndolin. Please hear me. Your future depends on it.

>Please leave. 

Myrrine the Wise looks at you. 
>Leave? No I cannot. I've been searching for you for sometime now. I saw you in a dream. I saw the struggles and horrors you will face.
