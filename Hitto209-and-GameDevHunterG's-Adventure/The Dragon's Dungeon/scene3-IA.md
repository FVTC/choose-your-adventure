# You go to investigate the screaming

You go to the north of the village to see what the commotion is about. You see an army of goblins marching toward the village. Standing on the shoulders of one of the goblins is one with a crown.
> Yes! Flee in terror, peasants! Flee from the might Goblin King Karl!

*Goblin King Karl?* That name rings a bell. Thinking it over, you realize that's because Karl has been menacing travelers on the road for nearly a decade, but he's never done anything *this* bold before. Something is odd about this attack.

Either way, you can't just stand here doing nothing. These people need help, and you're the only one here who can help them.
