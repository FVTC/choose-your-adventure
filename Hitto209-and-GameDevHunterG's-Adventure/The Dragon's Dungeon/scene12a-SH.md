# You slay the dragon

After your final attack, you reappear next to Rundrirth. As you reappear, Rundrirth falls to the ground. With the last of his strength, he speaks:
> So this is the hand fate has dealt to me, is it? It would seem my time has come to be reunited with **Tiamat** in Avernus.

It's over. Rundrirth has fallen. This region is now safe from his influence. You decide to leave ASAP, since you recall Rundrirth mentioning someone named "Akvir" and you don't want to stick around to meet whoever this is. You return to the village where this all started to inform Myrrine of your success.
