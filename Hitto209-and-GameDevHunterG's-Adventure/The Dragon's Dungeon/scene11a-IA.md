# You get close enough without waking Rundrirth.

This is your chance. You have one shot before Rundrith wakes up. You prepare your bow with an arrow.

## Roll to hit: AC 18

> [!NOTE]
> Because you were hidden when you attacked, your attack roll will be made with advantage. So roll 2d20 and use the higher roll.

> [!TIP]
> You can use the [dice roller](https://gamedevhunterg.github.io/dice.html) on the companion site if you don't have dice.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Iarath has a +9 to hit with his bow, so the final result will be the number rolled on the D20 plus 9.
