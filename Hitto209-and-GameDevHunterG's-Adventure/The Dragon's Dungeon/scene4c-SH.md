# You decide to steal back your spellbook

You need to do something, but without your spellbook, your options are rather limited. Threfore, you decide that you need to take your spellbook back. You doubt that Karl will willingly give it up, so you decide to steal your spellbook back from him.

You determine that openly approaching Karl will probably get you filled with arrows. Fortunately, Invisibility is one of the spells you choose to always have prepared precisely for situations like this. You cast Invisibility on yourself and approach Karl unnoticed. You attempt to take your spell book out of his hands.

## Roll a DC 14 Sleight of Hand check

> [!TIP]
> You can use the [dice roller](https://gamedevhunterg.github.io/dice.html) on the companion site if you don't have dice of your own.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sabtha has a +2 modifier for Sleight of Hand. So the result of the check will be the number rolled on the D20 plus 2.
