# Pushing Forward in the Mine

You wander through the mine, taking turn after turn through the dusty tunnels. Along the way, you spot a coil of old rope beside some abandoned equipment and decide to take it in case it comes in handy.

Before long, you realize you're completely lost. After what feels like hours, you finally spot a faint light ahead. You follow it out of the mine, relieved to feel the fresh air again. The sun is beginning to rise over the mountains, painting the sky orange and pink.

After a long, exhausting night, you've finally made it out!
