# Hide

You duck behind a cluster of trees as heavy footsteps shake the ground around you.

"Oh god... please keep walking," you whisper. A shadow falls over your hiding spot. Slowly, you look up and find a Carnotaurus staring directly at you. You freeze, barely daring to breathe.

"Oh, no..." A branch snaps beneath your foot. The Carnotaurus roars and lunges, tearing through the trees as you desperately try to crawl away. It's too late.
