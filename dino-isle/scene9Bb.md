
# The River Way

There is a small 12 x 12 inch square opening you notice nearby. A rusted door that says 'Samples Here' lies on the ground. Looking into it, you see it's about 20 feet long. 'Must run straight to the inside,' you think. Taking off your shirt and boots, you put them in the backpack. After placing the pack inside the sample tunnel, you find a long branch to push it almost all the way through and shut the door. 'The less I have on to swim, the better!' you tell yourself.

Getting in the water again doesn't bother you because you could use a good rinse, and it feels refreshing. The river is over your head, so you take a deep breath and dive underwater. On each side are concrete walls that narrow, increasing the water's speed.

In the darkened, rapid water, you slam into metal plates and thick fencing. The barriers are meant to keep any dinos foolhardy enough to try a submersive route. You are disoriented, panicking when you realize there is no way through. Trying to surface, your head hits the concrete ceiling. In a desperate attempt, you grasp at the slippery walls to pull yourself toward the entrance. But it's over. Unable to hold your breath any longer, your lungs fill with water, your body slumping lifelessly in a watery mausoleum.
