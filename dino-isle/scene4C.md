
#  Bypass the Truck

Upon seeing the old rusted truck, you decide not to investigate it.  You want to continue on even though darkness is falling.  Taking one last glance at the map you see the end of the swamp is not far ahead.  Relieved, you suddenly start feeling exhaustion shroud over you.  Looking ahead you can just make out what looks like several trees at the edge of the swamp and decide to sit there and rest for a little bit.

Not realizing you are actually sitting next to the leg of a brachiosaurus who is also resting.  There are several of them there that you did not see in the darkness.  As morning approaches, the lot of dinos begin to move.  You wake up as the leg pulls away and you flop back on the ground.  With sleepy brain haze still clearing, you open your eyes in time to see a gargantuous foot coming down on you.  Your body is crushed flatter than a tortilla on Cinco de Mayo.

The brachiosaurus begins shaking her back leg and wiping it on a boulder to get whatever remains of you off.
