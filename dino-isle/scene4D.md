
# Search the Truck

Approaching this old truck that hasn't been ran in years, you go to the back side of it.  Checking the bed of the truck you see a rusty crowbar laying in the corner. 'I'll take that,' you think.  Cautiously rounding the back you see there are a few brachiosaurus off in the distance as the light fades from the day.  The front wheels are buried deep in the mud but the right side door opens.

Holding up in the cab seems like a good place to spend the night. Inside the cab you can feel how tired you really are.  You do a little searching but with very faint light you find nothing.  Trying to stay awake is a lost battle.  You rest your head and doze.

All sorts of sounds you never heard before awaken you.  You stretch and open your eyes to see the largest ever alligator peering back at you from the hood.  It's a stare down.  The mega-gator lunges at you.  Surprisingly the windshield holds but spiders out.

What would you like to do other than fill your pants?
