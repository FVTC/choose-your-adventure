# Searching the ATV

You approach the old ATV and begin searching through its rusted compartments, hoping to find something useful. Most of what you find is either broken or too weathered to be of any use. 

"Please tell me there's something in here besides dinosaur snacks," you mumble, peeking beneath the seat. 

Just as you're about to give up, you notice a folded piece of paper tucked underneath it. Carefully pulling it free, you unfold it to reveal a map of the island. Despite its age, the map appears to be mostly intact, with several locations marked across the island's terrain. Finally, something that might help you figure out where you are and where to go next!
