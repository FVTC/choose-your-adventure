
# Morning is Such a Beach

You feel something pinching your fingers and hear gulls squawking.  The grittiness of sand presses on your cheek and a salty taste in your mouth.  Sitting up, a crab scurries away from your hand.  Dawn is breaking and you have no idea where you are.  The events of how you got here are a blur, but you're thankful you are alive.

Looking around you survey a serene beach on your left and a dense forest obscuring the inland on your right.  Waves lapping on the golden sand, nice breeze and the smell of the surf is paradise.  A paradise until you hear the crack of a growl/scream coming from somewhere off in the way of the forest.

>💭 You debate what to do: 'Should I search the beach and see if there is an way around the forest? Or should I stay here and maybe someone will find me?'
