
# The Ladder

Not wanting to risk an uncontrolled fall you get on the ladder.  About halfway down it ends.  'This shouldn't be too hard.' you think.  Hanging you guess that it's about 8 feet from your shoes to the ground.  Landing you hear a CRACK and immense pain from your left leg.  It is broken.

Sitting there for a little bit clutching your lower leg you hear a 'sqwark' from around the corner.  A compsognathus comes into view.  It sqwarks again and suddenly there are dozens more all looking at you.  In a flash they are upon you.  These little land piranhas devour you by the time the rescue plane arrives.  They only find a skeleton and hear the sound of tiny 'burps'.
