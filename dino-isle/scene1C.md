
# Cliff Climb

Not wanting to risk going through the forest or wait for rescue on the beach, you decide to start climbing.  It is rough and you have to skirt along a thin ledge taking you out over the water.  Looking down, there are indeed rather large sharks all over the place.  You can't see the beach anymore but you figure you're about 50 or 60 yards from it.

It is then you notice there is a gap in the ledge.  Only about a 2 foot space to jump and you go for it.  Getting across it you silently congratulate yourself.  This ledge is a little wider and you breathe a sigh of relief.  As you turn to continue on the ledge a large pelican flaps out from a hidden crevice in the rock wall.

You are knocked off balance, frantically reaching for anything to hang on to.  There is nothing.  Knocked unconscious after your head hits the wall on the way down, you don't have to worry about how the shark bites will hurt.

After several sharks 🦈 have dined on you a large one says "It's a good thing that morsel fell into the deep.  I was famished!  Since we all know fish are friends, not food."

☠️  THE END
