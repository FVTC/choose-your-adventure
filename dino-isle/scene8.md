
# More Plains!

Looking out on the lush plains before you seems peaceful.  You can make out a couple different types of dinos in the distance.  They appear to be grazing, and you can't blame them.  The ground is a bit trampled in spots but the rolling grass look like they are waving in the breeze at you.  A very fertile feeding ground.  Thankfully you don't see any kind of predator around.

Looking at the map it doesn't seem to have any planned route across the plains.  You know the compound is on the other side of this grassy buffet.  The dinos seemed to be split into two groups.   To the north it looks like there are a family of triceratops, to the south a group of stegosaurus and between the two is an open area.  The river you know is also to the south.  

Which way are you going?
