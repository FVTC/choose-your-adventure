# Going Around

As you walk, loud crashes and deep, rumbling bellows echo through the darkness. You catch a glimpse of movement in the distance, but the tall grass hides whatever is making the noise.

> 💭 “Yeah, I'm definitely not getting involved in that,” you think to yourself.

You leave the strange sounds behind and continue toward the mountains, hoping the rest of your journey will be a little more peaceful.
