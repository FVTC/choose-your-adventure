# The Strange Forest

You follow the path and take your first few steps into the strange forest, passing beneath the twisted branches that hang low overhead. The trees are enormous, their thick roots twisting through the damp earth, and their leaves block out most of the sunlight. The deeper you go, the darker and quieter it becomes. A cold breeze brushes against your skin, carrying a faint, unfamiliar scent. Something about this place feels unsettling, as if you're being watched from somewhere between the trees. 

The path carries on, but do you?
