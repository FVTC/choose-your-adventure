# Dodging Thin Air!

You watch the dilophosaurus closely, waiting for it to make its next move. When it shifts slightly, you panic and quickly dive to the side to avoid an attack.

Except... the dinosaur never attacked.

You hit the ground hard, scraping yourself on the rocky terrain. The Dilophosaurus stares at you before taking the opporunity to rip you to shreds. It's an instant death for you my friend!
