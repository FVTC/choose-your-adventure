
# Between the Dino Groups

Deciding to go between the dino groups seems like a good option.  It's the quickest way to the compound.  'With two groups of dinos on each side of me, I should be relatively safe,' thinking to yourself.  You keep alert for any large predator dinos that may be lurking in the area.

A ways off to the north you hear the triceretops are fighting with something, but it is nowhere near you.  As you trek your way through the tall grass you hear some rustling and movement ahead of you.  Suddenly you stumble out of the tall grass and right into a large area of trampled down grass.  You get up and see several ankylosaurus around you.  That's why this area looked clear!  These low, armored dinos are no push over with that huge club they swing on their tail.

You turn to leave the way you came but it is too late.  You never even see the one off to your side bring its club tail around and hit you in the back.  With such force your spine is broken and back skull shattered.  Later that evening a dimetrodons finds a free meal with your carcass.
