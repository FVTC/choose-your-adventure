
# ⛔The Aftermath⛔

You search the compound the next couple of weeks for a radio.  Rummaging through all the trucks and equipment here only yielded a few daily-use items but no radio.  You did find a delicious can of spam and a flare gun. The flare gun should have helped to signal a passing boat or plane.  Unfortunately you had to use it to dispatch a Dilophosaurus - those spitting and hissing bastards.

It was obvious that you were going to be here for a while.  You settle into a daily routine.  Other than a few dilophosaurus, there were only those little compsognathus that attack in masses.  You figured out how to trap them so they wouldn't ambush you.  They tasted like chicken.

Eventually you come to accept this as your new home.  At least you cleaned all the dinos out of the compound.  They were getting in through a square hole in the wall down by the river.  You plugged it up with rocks and soil, then moved a large piece of equipment in front of it.

In a few months you see that it isn't so bad here.  You have a safe area, food from the ocean and nearby dino kills, plus some native edible vegetation.  You finally decide that this is your life now - a modern day caveman, well sort of.  You live a long life up to the ripe old age of 37.  Sad that you died from tetanus after getting cut by a rusty metal bar.
