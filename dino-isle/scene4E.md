
# Out the Side Door

You grasp the door handle to jump out and it snaps off.  Reaching for the crowbar to smash the side window, the mega-gator lunges again.  This time there is no holding it back and the windshield shatters.  With jaws agape, it clamps around your whole upper torso.  Pulling your limp, lifeless body back into the swamp.
