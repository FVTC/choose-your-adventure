
# Search the Wall

Walking north next to the wall you can see it is getting late in the afternoon. Keeping an eye out over the plains the only dinos you see are a few Corythosaurus munching on the grass. You get a ways north from the river to a bend in the wall. Going around the bend you see a large garage type door. It's heavy and you can't move it at all.

Going back to the wall bend you notice a metal strut sticking out about 5 feet from the top. 'If I can loop the rope around that I may be able to climb over.' you think. After several tries the rope finally catches on the strut. You pull, it bends a little but holds.

Second guessing your decision you think 'Maybe I should go back and try the river?'.  That thought is cast aside when you see 3 Velociraptors running straight at you. Climbing up the rope for dear life there is nowhere else to go.  They leap at you, hissing and snarling.  Thankfully you are out of their reach.

Climbing onto the metal strut and leaping, you grasp the ledge of the wall.  Once on top you see the wall is about 15 feet wide. There is a staircase leading down to the compound not very far away.
