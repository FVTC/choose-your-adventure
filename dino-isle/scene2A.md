# Continue on the Path

You continue along the narrow path, carefully stepping over the thick roots that stretch across the ground. Suddenly, you notice enormous three-toed footprints pressed deep into the mud. They look fresh. Before you can get a closer look, a deafening roar echoes through the trees. Birds burst from the canopy, and the ground begins to tremble beneath your feet. Something massive is moving nearby.

"Maybe it's friendly," you tell yourself, though you know better than that.

The trembles grow as each second passes. Think fast!
