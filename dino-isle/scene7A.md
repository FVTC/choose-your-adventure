# One with the Herd!

You carefully approach the moving herd of parasaurolophus, keeping a safe distance from the lone dilophosaurus circling nearby. As the herd begins moving down the path, you cautiously fall into step alongside them, trying your best to blend in. The ground rumbles beneath the herd's heavy footsteps as you move together across the hills. 

Eventually, the herd reaches the edge of the plains. You quietly slip away and continue down the path, relieved to have made it through safely.. or so you thought!
