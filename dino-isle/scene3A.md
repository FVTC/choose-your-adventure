# To the Mountains!

You make your way in the direction of the mountains, pushing through the tall grass as the rocky slopes slowly grow closer. The walk is exhausting, and the open landscape leaves you feeling a little exposed. After a while, something catches your eye..

An old ATV sits abandoned in the grass, its tires half-sunken into the dirt and its metal frame covered in dust and rust. It looks like it has been sitting here for quite some time, left behind by whoever once explored this island.

"Well... that's something," you mutter as you approach it. You have no idea whether it still works, but it might be worth taking a closer look.
