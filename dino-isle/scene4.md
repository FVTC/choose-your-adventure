
# The Swamp

Coming upon a lowland area you feel the ground beneath your boots getting softer.  There is a slight haze in the air.  It looks like a wetland area and it smells 'swampy'.  Looking out you can't see how far it goes since the view is obscured by distant fog.  Taking a breather you look at the map while it is still light out. The path on the map zig-zags across the swampy area.  You could try to go around, but don't want to chance being ambushed in the forest.  Besides the map has scribbled 'DO NOT ENTER' on both sections of forest.  The map shows this is where you are supposed to enter so you press on.

Trudging on in knee deep water you see you are supposed to make a turn south.  Before you though there looks to be some worn down plants that lead straight.  Thinking to yourself 'I could save time by following the straight path.'

What do you do?
