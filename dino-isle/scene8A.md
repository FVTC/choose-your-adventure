
# North Around Triceretopa Family

Heading north to get around the triceretops should be easy.  'What kind of animal would want to menace a three pronged tank?' you think to yourself.  Getting closer to the triceretops group (but still keeping distance) you observe much smaller "children" dinos in their midst.  There is one big "daddy" dino in the group that keeps eyeing you.

Suddendly from your rear two tyrannosaurus rexes burst from the treeline, heading straight for the triceretops family.  They must be after the young.  You have nowhere to run. Caught between a rock and a hard place.  Both groups start their battle for survival right where you are hunched down.  It didn't help much to hide.  You were trampled, mauled, gored and even shish kebabed.  At least you were tenderized for the foot high compsognathus' meal later.
