# Run Towards the Nearest Hill

You sprint toward the nearest hill as the dilophosaurus races after you. Its footsteps pound against the ground, and you can hear it closing in.

Just before it catches you, you scramble over the hill and slide down the other side. The dinosaur stops at the top, seemingly unwilling to follow you any farther.

Breathless but relieved, you check your map and continue toward the plains.
