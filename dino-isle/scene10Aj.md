
# The Bridge

Arriving at the bridge you see the plane is much closer.   You MUST get to the dock soon!  On the bridge, one dilophosaurus lunges at you, misses and falls into the water.  Hissing and splashing in its watery grave.

The other dilophosaurus is not as fast and you are able to make it to the other side.  Grabbing a large branch you face off with it when it catches up.  It lunges, you swing, knocking it to the ground - unconscious.  You're exhausted, but must keep going.
