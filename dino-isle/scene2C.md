# Run Down the Path

You don't dare look back. With the sound of heavy footsteps thundering behind you, you sprint down the narrow path, ducking beneath low-hanging branches and stumbling over tangled roots. "Get away from me!" You scream as you run. The creature roared in return, it's roar echoing through the forest, growing louder as it tears through the trees behind you. With one final burst of strength, you dash out of the trees and into the open plains. You stumble forward, gasping for air, and turn to look back at the forest.

The footsteps stop and for a moment, everything is silent. You may have escaped the creature, but as you look out across the unfamiliar plains, you realize the forest was only the beginning of your troubles.
