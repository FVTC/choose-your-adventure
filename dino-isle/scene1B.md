
# Stay and Wait

You sit on the beach all day waiting for a passing boat or plane to spot you.  Nothing materializes.  The sun is setting and you're dehydrated.  'There must be water in those trees' you think and decide to take your chances since you haven't heard any terrible noises recently.

Going into the forest you are making some loud cracking noises as you push through the dense foliage.  Then you feel it.. your being watched? 👀 Ahead of you something passes quickly, but you have no time to react.  You are pounced on by a raptor from behind that clamps down and rips your head off.

Later on while the raptors are dinning over your carcass one of them remarks "This meat is a little dry, but very tender."

☠️  THE END
