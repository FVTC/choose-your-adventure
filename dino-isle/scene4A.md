
# The Worn Down Path

You move forward and think 'I will be across this in no time!'  At first you are moving with ease but the water starts to get deeper.  Up out of the mist you can make out a few parasaurolophus grazing on the underwater foliage.  'I better not spook them,' you think and decide to move off to their side a ways.

The water is chest deep here and you head for a large log to hold onto.  Upon reaching the 'log' it feels quite smooth.  You then realize it's a huge snake!  Too late to try swimming away.  Coiling around you in a tight death grip, you gasp "OH GOD!"  Squeezing tighter with each of your timed last breaths you look up, seeing it stare down at you.  A flicking tongue is the last thing you see before it all goes dark.
