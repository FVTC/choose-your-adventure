# Hide in the Tall Grass

You quickly duck into the tall grass, crouching low as the dilophosaurus approaches. You hold your breath, hoping it will pass without noticing you.

For a moment, everything is quiet. Then, the grass rustles beside you. The dilophosaurus has found your hiding spot!

Nothing else can be heard but your screams and the thumping of the herd that you should've hid with.
