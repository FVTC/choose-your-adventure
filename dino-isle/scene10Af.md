
# The Fire Escape

Jumping for the fire escape ladder you climb up away from those spitting hissers.  'They can't get up here.' you muse and walk to the other side of the large roof.  Looking out you see there is a dock on the peninsula in the distance.  Stretching before you a stretch of land with less vegetation, a bridge, then the long peninsula.

Resting for a few minutes, you hear a faint engine sound.  "I have to get to the dock!" you gasp.  On your left is a ladder that only goes halfway to the ground.  To the right is a steep awning with a truck under it.

Which route will you take?
