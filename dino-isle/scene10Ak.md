
# The Dock

![alt text](images/DinoIsleMapDock.jpg)

You're about halfway between the bridge and the dock when the float plane flies overhead, circling for a place to land.  The engine noise wakes up the dilophosaurus, it shrugs off the hit and resumes the chase. You get to the long dock and see the plane is at the end of it.  A person at the plane is pointing at something, you look behind you and see the dilophosaurus quickly moving this way.  You bolt down the pier but your exhaustion is catching up to you.

About midway on the dock you can hear its hisses behind you.  It is catching up fast!  You turn around, drawing the flare gun, just as it opens its mouth, you fire hitting it squarely in the back of the throat.  It falls limp on the dock still smoking.  You look down, bring the flare gun up, blow across the top of the barrel and say "Do you feel lucky?  Well, do you, punk?".  Dropping the flare gun, you trot the rest of the way to the float plane and board it.
