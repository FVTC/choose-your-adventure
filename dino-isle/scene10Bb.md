# Radio Building

![alt text](images/DinoIsleMapRadio.jpg)

Arriving at the small radio building you cautiously open the door to peer inside.  Entering after making sure it is safe, you see there is a microphone on floor, wires on the desk, but no radio.  Muttering "DAMN! I should have taken that radio!"

In the next room there is a large battery bank.  Looking at the battery status you see a couple still have some charge left.  Unfortunately there is no radio to power since the jeep is smashed after the dino stampede.  You search the small building, but no radio is found.

"There has got to be one here!" you exclaim.
