
# Down the Street

You are hungry but opt to get to the south peninsula to see if there is an area to be picked up.  After a little bit of walking through the ankle deep foliage you hear a little 'sqwark'.  Stopping in your tracks for a second you feel it has to be a nearby bird.

Taking a rest for a moment against a beat up truck, you hear that 'sqwark' again and some shuffling.  Surprising you a little dino jumps up on the truck's hood.  It's a compsognathus.  Hearing multiple 'sqwarks' you turn around seeing a couple dozen of them.

Planning on running away sounds great until your feet get tangled in the undergrowth.  In a flash they are upon you.  These little land piranhas come out of everywhere to devour your entire body by the time the rescue plane lands.  Your skeleton and the sound of tiny 'burps' are all they find.
