
# Reach for the Crowbar

Quickly grabbing the crowbar you lay flat on the seat, just as the mega-gator crashes through the windshield.  It flails for a few seconds then backs out, slipping off the hood, back into the swampy mud.  You sit up quickly with the crowbar in hand and break the rear window of the truck.  Without hesitation you climb into the back bed of the truck.

Looking over your shoulder the mega-gator is back up on the hood, lunges again into the cab.  Its snout makes it through the back window, snapping wildly but falling short of clamping on you.  For a brief moment you see it is lodged in the cab.  Taking a chance you jam the crowbar from the jaw underside.  It pierces through and gets lodged in the upper jaw.  The mega-gator makes a blood curdling growl/hiss as it shakes the entire truck to get free.

Scrambling at this moment, you clamber out the back of the truck and run. You head down the path you remembered from the map.  As you are exiting the swamp area, you see some long necks off in the distance disappearing behind some hills.
