
# The Canteen

![alt text](images/DinoIsleMapCanteen.jpg)

Upon hearing your stomach growl makes you decide to go into the canteen.  You have a little while before the plane gets here and haven't eaten anything for some time.  There is no problem getting in since one door is gone.  Stopping for a moment you hear a strange low gurgling chirping sound.  You look down the hallway but don't see anything.  'As long as it doesn't get any closer I don't care' you think.

You rummage around in the kitchen area since all the vending machines  were smashed.  In one drawer you find a loaded flare gun you pocket for signaling the plane, and JACKPOT - a can of spam in a nearby cupboard.  Opening it quickly, you wolf down the contents, then blurts "AHHHH good stuff!".  It's then you hear that same low gurgle chirping, much closer.

Looking over your shoulder you see a dilophosaurus about 2/3 your height.  Curiously it looks at you, making that strange sound you heard earlier.  It hisses, raising its frills on each side of its head and spits something at you.  Missing you while ducking, you run to the hallway on other side of the kitchen.

Looking left there is an open doorway, on the right there is another dilophosaurus.  You bolt towards the open door.  At the doorway is an alleyway, dead end to your right, left out into the side street, in front of you there is a fire escape ladder that you must jump for.

What is your choice?
