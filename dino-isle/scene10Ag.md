
# Out the Alleyway

Taking a left down the alleyway you run out into an overgrown street.  To your rear you can hear hissing then something sticky hits the back of your head.  The hissing is getting fainter, but the back of your skull is starting to burn.  A wave of dizziness starts to come over you.  Barely making it inside another building you slump to the floor.  Waiting for that low gurgle chirping and hissing to return.

It never comes.  Instead you hear multiple 'sqwarks'.  Opening your eyes is a much worse sight. A couple dozen compsognathus are looking at you.  In a flash they are upon you.  These little land piranhas come out of everywhere, devouring you by the time the rescue plane lands.  They only find a skeleton and hear the sound of tiny 'burps'.

☠️ THE END
