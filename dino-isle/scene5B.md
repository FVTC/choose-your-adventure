# Laying Low Till Sunrise

Exhausted from your escape, you find a sheltered spot between some large rocks and decide to wait until sunrise. You curl up, hoping the darkness will keep you hidden. For a while, everything is quiet. Then, heavy footsteps begin to shake the ground nearby. You slowly open your eyes and see the same carnotaurus from the forest.

“Oh no…”

Before you can run, the carnotaurus grabs you. It's over.
