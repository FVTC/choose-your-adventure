Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Return victorious!]

You sigh in relief as the last of the dragon's life drains out of it. The dragon is dead!

Your blade is sharp, and you quickly cut off its head to take with you as a trophy. You will take it to the capital, where you will be given a hero's welcome! Perhaps you'll even get to meet the princess...

With fantasies of your triumphant return in your mind, you grip the horns of the dragon's head and swing it over your shoulder with a grin. It's cumbersome, but the journey back will be more pleasant than the trip here now that you no longer have to worry about a dragon.

The dragon's blood drips onto your shoulder, too hot and too purple in the daylight to be mistaken for human.

...Actually, is the blood getting hotter?

It is! The heat is strong enough that you already feel it even through your clothes and armor, and it grows hotter by the second. You quickly fling the dragon's head to the ground, but its blood has already covered half your body. You fumble with your armor straps, desperate to get it off. None of it has gotten on your exposed skin, thankfully, but your metal armor is already so hot you can't touch it without instantly burning your fingers.

You're able to pull all the armor off, but—oh dear, it seems the dragon blood has already seeped through the cracks to your clothes. Your gambeson is already smoking. Not long now before it bursts into flames. Ah, there it is!

Screaming is so unbecoming of a knight. But I suppose standards these days aren't what they used to be. The blood has already reached your skin, and I'm sorry, little knight, but there's nothing a poor narrator like me can do to help you now. You're the only one with any choice here, after all.

...Ah, I don't think they're listening anymore. I suppose it's hard to hear a narrator over being burned alive. Well, shall we?
