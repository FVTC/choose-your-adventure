Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[DON'T TURN AROUND.]

Your body remains frozen. There is a feeling like icy fingers at the back of your neck. Your sword falls from numb fingers.

Didn't you know what sort of forest this was before you came charging in here looking for a beast to slay? This place is filled with the forgotten and ferocious creatures your princess thought unsuitable for the type of kingdom she wanted. And the things that live here do not take kindly to knights waving swords around.

They may forget such a transgression once...but you've already been here before, haven't you, little knight? They won't be so kind to you again. Especially after killing one of their own.

Vicious whispers fill your ears, digging deep into your mind. The limbs of the half-dead trees creek and loom above you, cutting off any view of the sky.

Do you really think there's any way out for you anymore, little knight?

Try again?
