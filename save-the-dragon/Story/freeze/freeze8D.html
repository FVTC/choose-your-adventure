Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Escape with the dragon.]

You tense for a moment, waiting to see if the dragon will try to breathe fire or lash out at you. It doesn't move, just watches you unblinking.

You look around once more at the steep walls around you, then back at the dragon.

The dragon is just as trapped as you are, moreso, actually, thanks to the chains wrapped tight around it. Perhaps...

You take a few slow, careful steps towards the dragon. Its eyes track your movements carefully.

"Can you understand me?" You ask.

The dragons ears flick in your direction. Well, its as good of a confirmation as you're likely to get.

You take a deep breath and continue to move slowly towards the dragon. "Look, we're both stuck here, and both probably gonna die here. But if we work together there's a small chance that we can both make it out of this."

The dragon lets out a small puff of hot air. You flinch and stop moving.

"I'm gonna come over there, alright? I'm going to try and get those chains off of you. But you need to promise not to roast me, alright? No fire, no claws, no...tail." You eye the dragons spiked tail warily. Aside from its fire breath, the tail is its most available method of attack. "Just...leave me in one peice, and I can get you out. Okay? We have a deal?"

The dragon's eyes narrow. For a moment you think that this is it, you're dead, all you've accomplished is getting killed slightly faster.

But the dragon closes its eyes and tucks its head away from you, allowing you to come close. You breathe a sigh of relief.

It takes hours of meticulous work to get the chains off of the dragon. The chains are relatively small, and dig deep into the dragons flesh in some places. You wince when you see that they've completely torn through the flesh on one of its wings. It must be painful for the dragon, but it makes no noise and movement asidr from the occassional flinch and tremor.

But eventually, you manage it, and the last of the chains fall away. The dragon slowly rises to its feet, and for the first time you're able to see the true size of it. It's absolutely massive, towering above you even on all fours so that you have to crane your neck to see its eyes.

You...you did it, little knight. You really did it. You freed the dragon.

With the dragon's wing in such rough shape, its incapable of flight. But it doesn't need to fly to get out of this pathetic hole.

The dragon snatches the back of your tunic with its teeth and lifts you up as if scruffing a kitten. With its limbs free, its easily able to scale the stone walls with its sharp claws. In no time at all the both of you are out.

It's only after the dragon drops you to the ground that you realize you left your armor and the remains of your sword at the bottom of the sinkhole.

Oh well. Not much of a loss, I say. You weren't using it for much, were you?

Before you can even breath a word of thanks to the dragon, it has already scuttled off into the forest with surprising speed.

You find your way out of the forest easily.
