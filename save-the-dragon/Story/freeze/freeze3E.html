Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Yes.]

You freeze before the dragon trapped on the forest floor, unable to decide what the best course of action is. Surprisingly—wait...

Have you been here before?

No, no, can't have. What a weird moment to get deja vu. Well, moving on.

After a long moment where both you and the dragon stare at each other, unmoving, the dragon breaks its gaze and continues its struggle. Since you have decided to be neither its savior nor its slayer, it can only rely upon its own power to live or die.

You watch and do nothing as it writhes helplessly against its binds. You stand aside as the chains bite into its flesh, cutting deeper and deeper. Dragon blood stains the dirt beneath it, and still it struggles.

At first it had struggled silently, but now it makes a noise akin to a whimpering dog. Its energy is now mostly spent, its struggle proved pointless. Now it lays still, making wounded noises.

You are faced with a strangely familiar choice. Or...almost familiar.

[Gag it.] [Silence it with your sword.] [Walk away.]
