Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Walk away.]

You turn to walk away. Why should you have to deal with this? You're a knight, you came to fight a dragon, and it's clear there's no fight to be had here.

Between one step and the next, the ground begins to tremble. Somewhere below you there is a CRACK and the ground gives way completely. You fall, knocking against dirt and stone.

As the dust settles, you pick up your aching body to get a look at your surroundings. The ground has collapsed into a large sinkhole with steep stone walls. You stand at one end of the new chasm. At the other end the dragon lies on its back, writhing in its chains in an attempt to get upright again.

You ignore the dragon and try to climb your way up. Your gloved fingers are unable to make any purchase on the smooth stone walls of the sinkhole. You tear off your gloves, and take off your armor too while you're at it. It makes no difference.

The dragon has finally rolled over onto its stomach. It curls up as well as it can against the wall, watching you with wary eyes.
