Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Gag it.]

Your cloak was given to you on the day of your knighting, symbolizing the honor, justice, and chivalry that drew you to the knightly order in the first place. You unclasp it from around your shoulders and approach the dragon, intending to use your cloak to gag it.

The dragon watches you approach it. The closer you get, the more its gaze narrows.

When you are barely a step away, the dragon opens its jaw wide and lets out a roar that shakes the half-dead trees of the forest. Saplings burst from the ground between you and the dragon, surprising you so much you stumble back and fall to the ground. The saplings rapidly become full-grown trees, so tightly packed together that they form an solid barrier between you and the dragon.

You stumble back to your feet and examine the wall of trees for any crack or weakness.

[Cut down the trees with your sword.] [Get out of the forest.]
