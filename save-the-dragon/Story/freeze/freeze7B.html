Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Kill it.]

You draw your sword from it's sheath and walk towards the dragon. It struggles with renewed fervor when it notices your approach.

You see a dim glow rising from its stomach, but the dragon is too late if it wants to roast you alive. You've already reached it and slashed its throat with your sword.

You sigh in relief as the last of the dragon's life drains out of it. The dragon is dead!

Your blade is sharp, and you quickly cut off its head. With fantasies of your triumphant return in your mind, you grip the horns of the dragon's head and shove it in a bag to take to the capital as a trophy.

[Return victorious!]
