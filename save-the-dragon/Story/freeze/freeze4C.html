Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

Walk away.

You tear your eyes away from the dragon and turn to walk away. It's not your problem. Why should you have to be the one to make a choice?

You make it barely three steps before you hear a low growl to your left.

You freeze. You slowly turn your head to look.

In the deep shadows of the half-dead trees, you can make out the gleeming eyes and silhouette of a wolf baring its teeth. The wolf is larger than any you've seen before, easily twice your height or more.

Your breath catches in your throat and your grip the hilt of your sword. The wolf slowly stalks forward into the light.

You barely have time to notice that its flesh is hanging dead off its bones before it lunges and snaps its teeth around your throat.

You really do need to stop getting into these situations, little knight. Well, at least it was quick this time.
