Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Escape alone.]

You tense for a moment, waiting to see if the dragon will try to breathe fire or lash out at you. It doesn't move, just watches you unblinking.

Over the next few hours you try everything you can think of to escape this hole on your own.

You try using your sword to cut footholds in the stone. You only end up breaking the blade in half.

You try tying your clothes into a rope. It doesn't reach nearly far enough to reach the edge.

You try getting a running start and scrambling up as far as you can go, trying to grab hold of one of the plants dangling over the edge. All that happens is you tumble and twist your ankle, severely hindering any future attempts.

It seems all your attempts truly are doomed to be fruitless, little knight.

You slump to the ground, all your energy spent. The dragon watches you, but makes no move. It seems as cautious of approaching you as you are of it.

You roll onto your side, your back to the dragon, and try not to feel hopeless at the situation.

But it is hopeless, little knight.

You will spend what little is left of your life cold and hungry in the depths of the earth, with only the creature you were sent to kill for company. A slow and miserable death.

Unless of course, the dragon decides to have mercy on you and make your end much quicker.

[END: Trapped]
