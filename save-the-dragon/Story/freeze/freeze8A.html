Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Cut down the trees with your sword.]

The trees are each at least twice as thick as your body. You whack at them with your sword, but the pale grey bark proves to be more durable than the steel of your blade.

After an unknowable time of trying to cut them down, your blade shatters.

You haven't made a single mark on the trees. You can't even hear anything from the dragon on the other side. Is it even still there?
