Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Return victorious!]

You return to the capital and are greeted with a hero's welcome! There is a grand celebration and a feast in your honor. You even get to meet the princess, and are allowed to keep the dragon's head. You have it stuffed and mount it on your wall. All is well.

During the day, at least.

At night you find yourself unable to sleep more often than not. What little sleep you get is quickly interrupted by visions of the dragon's eyes silently staring at you as it bleeds out.

The eyes are still watching you when you wake up.

About a month after your return, you start hearing whispers whenever you try to sleep. You can never make out what they're saying, no matter how closely you listen. After a few days, the whispers become constant. You often notice people watching you out of the corner of your eye.

You can't stop the thought that they're planning something. Plotting against you.

You try to stay locked in your house, wary of any people getting close. The mounted dragon head is your only company.

You board up your windows, but eyes still watch you through the cracks. You keep the door locked and barred, but you know they're still just outside, waiting for you to come out so they can strike.

Three months after your return, you can't take it anymore.

One night you sneak out the back dor, taking nothing with you but the dragon head in a bag. You have no conscious goal, no destination in mind. You simply let your feet carry you away.

You find yourself standing at the edge of the forest.

The half-dead trees loom tall above you. A cold mist curls at the border of the trees. You take the dragon head out of the bag and rip off the wooden mount. You're certain you had the head preserved and stuffed, but it bleeds as if you just cut it off.

You enter the forest.

The accusing eyes never leave you.
