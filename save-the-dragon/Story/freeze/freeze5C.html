Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Be sneaky.]

You slowly inch backwards into the shadows of the forest and slink around in a wide arc around the dragon. The trees in this forest are all half-dead, but dense. Combined with the low fog that perpetually hangs over the forest, very little light makes it to the forest floor.

Perfect for things that don't want to be seen. Or little knights that want to hide from a dragon.

You are nearly directly behind the dragon now. You creep forward, and prepare to lauch yourself over the dragon's back onto its neck, reach around, and shove your cloak down its throat before it even notices where you are.

But the moment you jump into the air, the dragon lurches and hits you dead-on with its spiked tail.

What you had failed you notice was that while the dragons eyes may not have been tracking you, its ears never lost track of your location. Dragons, after all, have some of the best hearing of any known creature.

But why would you know that? You're just a knight, after all. No need to study dragons when it's your job to kill them!

But you're too busy failing to breath through your crushed lungs to worry about that right now, aren't you?

Oh, well. Here we go.
