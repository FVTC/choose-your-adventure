Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Be quick.]

Without another moment's thought, you fling off your cape and lunge at the dragon in a single motion. You reach out to shove the cape into the dragon's mouth—

Except the dragon's mouth is closed. You try to pry it open, with no luck.

What? Did you expect the dragon to make this easy for you? As much as you may hate to admit it, the dragon is still a living being, and living beings make choices. In this case, the choice to not open its mouth to let you shove your cloak down its throat. Why would it? It'd just be unsanitary, if you ask me. Who knows where that thing's been?

The dragon gives you an unimpressed look and huffs a hot plume of smoke in your face.

Before you can make another move, the dragon opens its jaw wide and blasts you point-blank with a wave white-hot fire breath.

Try again?

[No.] [Yes.]
