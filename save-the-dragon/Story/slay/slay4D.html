A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

You hold tight onto your blad and push upwards with all your might. A rogue claw rips through your armour and into your left bicep. The wound stings and your strength falters for just a moment, your grip slipping forcing you to brace with your legs in order to not be knocked down. You think once more about your friends and family waiting for you back at the village and let out an anguished gutteral cry, squeezing your eyes shut and giving one final slow push up into the beast's chest.

You can feel the dragon struggling to reach you, hot breath on your skin and growling so close to your ears you worry you won't make it out of this alive. But, just as the thought reaches the front of your mind, you feel a pop and everything goes silent. No more struggling, no more resistance. Just- nothing.

You tenativly release your grip on your blade and take a step back. You've done it. You've slain the Dragon.

You drop to your knees in exhaustion, trying futally to catch your breath in this final moment of reprive. As you sit before the unmoving beast, staring down at your hands, a feeling of sorrow begins to wash over you. Why were you sent here? Why was this Dragon chained up? Why were you attacking it if it was already incapacitated all the way out here in these forest ruins?

Just as these questions start swirling around in your head, you hear an odd sound coming from in front of you. Like the sound of sand falling in an oversized hourglass. You jerk your head up to see the final moments of the Dragon's body turning to dust. No, not dust... Ash.

From the enormous pile of ash on the ground, you watch as a tiny little bird digs it's way out and shakes off the debris. Your eyes widen in surprise. You don't quite understand what's happening here. The bird looks around for a moment before locking eyes with and skittering over to you. It takes a brief second to nuzzle your armoured hand before giving you a little "chirp" and fluttering away.

You don't know if you'll ever be able to make any real sense of what has happened here today, but the one thing you know deep down in your heart?

You saved the dragon...
