Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Heart]

Your training prepared you for this moment. The heart is the way to go. Too many risks with a creature of unknown origin otherwise.

You nimbly dodge claw and fang, making your way closer and closer until you find yourself nearly directly underneith the beast. Without wasting a single second you jab your trusty blade upwards into the Dragon's underbelly. It roars in pain, claws scrambling at you from all directions for just a moment before the creature falls to it's side.

With your hands still on your sword, You can feel the beating of the creature's pulse stop as your weapon remains lodged firmly in it's heart. As you stare at the still form before you, you feel a wave of relief wash over you. You did it.

You won.


Or, at least, That's what you thought.

See, when the creature regenerated and formed it's second monsterous head, you saw that. What you didn't see was it's internals stitching themselves together to form a second, horrifying beating heart...

Before you have a chance to even think about running away, the second head learches forward. You flinch and strech out a forearm in a pitiful attempt at sheilding yourself from the incoming attack. The creature clamps down on your shoulder, engulfing your entire right arm in it's terrible jaws. If only this had been the original head, then maybe you would've been spared from the searing pain of it's acidic siliva eating through your armour and burning away your skin.

It is slow, it is painful, and it is your end.
