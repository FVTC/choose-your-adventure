Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Pull]

Your sword. You need it. You pull with all your might to try and free your weapon from the bowls of the beast but this only ends with you angering it more. It roars and kicks you with it's back leg, gashing open the chestplate of your armour. Weaponless and with your most important piece of armour compromised, you stand back and watch in horror as the Dragon pulls at it's chains, breaking on limb loose to swipe at you once more.

You try to dodge but the Dragon is too quick. It clips your leg and you fall to the ground with a cry of pain. You do your best to crawl away having been incapacitated by the beast's last attack but you just don't have enough time. The dragon reaches out it's freed forelimb and drags you closer. It puts it's foot down on top of your chest, making it nigh impossible to breathe. The Dragon's talons dig into the stone floor beneith you, framing your face between it's claws. The Dragon roars and wastes no time in dispatching you from this plane of existance.
