[Retreat]

Yeaaaahhhh... no.

I'd be out of there too. Being a completely reasonable knight of high standing and honor, you make the first good decision of the night. You turn heel and sprint for the woods. The beast behind you spews a tital wave of acid, splatters eating through your armour and coroding your sword. Even more reason to GTFO.

Your thoughts drift, for just a moment, to the chains that held the beast back and you run even harder as you hear them clatter to the ground. You don't need to look back to know the acid has freed the dragon. The dragon roars and you flinch as you feel an enormous gust of wind hit your back.

The dragon has taken flight.

You take a sharp left and spot a burrow in the side of a small hill just large enough for you to fit into. You know this is your only option so you sprint towards the hole and slide in, kicking up dirt and dust which helps conceal you just in time for the dragon to pass overhead and miss you completely.

You wish you could hold your breath as your heart pounds against your chest but you just can't seem to get enough oxygen in to allow it. You wait, every second that passes feeling like full agonizing hours. For minutes you can hear the Dragon's wings pounding the air of the sky above you. Eventually, however, you hear the sound of the leathery wings beating fade until there's nothing to hear but the crickets and owls of the night.

You cautiously make your way back out of the burrow, overly aware of your surroundings. It seems that you are safe for now. You breathe a sign of relief and collapse to the ground. As you drape your arms over your knees and hang your head in exhaustion, you hear a distant scream of terror.

You may just have to find a new village to live in...
