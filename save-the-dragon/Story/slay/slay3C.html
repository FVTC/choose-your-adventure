Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Chains]

Taking the briefest pauses, you swing your sword with all your might down onto the chains holding back the monsterous beast. In an instant, your sword bounces off the metal with a sharp clanging noise, stinging your ears and hands alike. Your sword goes fling out of your hands and digs itself deep in the dirt ten paces back.

You're not sure why you thought hitting the chains with your sword was a good idea in the first place. Now, however, it is time to reap the concequences...

Not wasting a second more, the dragon opens it's gnarly maw and lunges. The last thing you see being the rarely observed soft pallet of a creature such as the dragon you came to slay. Maybe next time you'll make some better choices.
