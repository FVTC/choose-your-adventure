Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

Without another moment of hesitation, you charge forward, slashing at the beast's throat. Metal hits flesh and the Dragon lets out a pained roar, collapsing to the ground. You watch as your opponent falls, letting the victory wash over you. All your training paid off. That was easy...

too easy...

You bring your attention back to the dragon's slumped form. If you didn't know any better, you would've said it looked like the dragon was... moving.

Before your very eyes, the beast you had just slain begins to writhe and tremble, an unspeakable transformation beginning to take place. In a matter of seconds, the fallen form before you rises to it's feet, the slash you gave it having morphed into a grotesque, horrifying, secondary head.

The new set of jaws gnash their teeth, acidic siliva splattering to the ground below. One glance at the now scorched stone floor tells you everything you need to know about this new monstrosity. Both heads let out a roar, one deep and bone shaking, the other screeching so horribly it makes your ears bleed.

The air around you chills and your heart drops to your stomach. What will you do?
