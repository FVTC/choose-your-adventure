You remember the wise words of a long gone teacher: "The most dangerous opponent is the one you don't know is there."

Without a second thought, you fall back, becoming one with the shadows of the forest. Using the foliage and ruins of stone buildings centuries past their hayday, you work your way around to the back side of the Dragon, unseen. The beast before you sits, tense, serveying the area around it without being able to fully turn around due to the chains locking it to the ground.

This is your moment. What will you do?
