Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Skull]

You stealthly work your way up behind the beast, carefully stepping over and around it's wildly twitching tail and claws. Digging your toes into the earth as you push off into a silent sprint. You can feel your heart beating fast in your chest. You know this is your only chance. If you get this wrong, it's game over.

You take a running leap onto the beast's back and it immediately starts writhing beneith you, trying to shake you loose. You loose your balance for just a moment before collapsing down onto your hands and knees. You don't have much time.

You crawl your way up the Dragon's back and neck, finally reaching it's monsterous skull. With one swift movement you put your trusty blade to use, bringing it down directely on the top of the beast's thrashing dome.

The Dragon screeches in pain, flailing it's head wildly and flinging you to the ground below.You land with a clatter as your armour does little to absorb a crushing blow such as this one. All the air has been knocked out of your lungs and you cough dryly in response. The Dragon continues to howl in pain as you lay on the ground catching your breath.

By the time you can breathe again, you realize your mistake. Your sword, no longer by your side, resided lodged in the Dragon's skull. Unfortunately for you, it doesn't seem to have penetrated deep enough...

The Dragon howls in anguish and anger moving as close to you as it possibly can while chained to the ruins floor. It jerks it's head towards you, it's teeth mere inches away from your face. You squeeze your eyes shut as the creature before you lets out a bone shaking roar, hot breath and siliva hitting your face at horrifying speeds. Before you can muster another thought, your world goes black. Maybe your loyal blade will help the next adventerer...
