Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Eyes]

You stealthly work your way up behind the beast, carefully stepping over and around it's wildly twitching tail and claws. Quietly, you sheith your sword and draw two large daggers out of their hiding place, digging your toes into the earth as you push off into a silent sprint. You can feel your heart beating fast in your chest. You know this is your only chance. If you get this wrong, it's game over.

You take a running leap onto the beast's back and it immediately starts writhing beneith you, trying to shake you loose. You loose your balance for just a moment before collapsing down onto your hands and knees. You don't have much time.

You crawl your way up the Dragon's back and neck, finally reaching it's monsterous skull. With one swift movement you put your daggers to use, making their blades the last thing the creature will ever see.

The Dragon screeches in pain, flailing it's head wildly and flinging you to the ground below.You land with a clatter as your armour does little to absorb a crushing blow such as this one. All the air has been knocked out of your lungs and you cough dryly in response. The Dragon continues to howl in pain as you lay on the ground catching your breath.

By the time you can breathe again, the Dragon has gone silent. You glance up at the now blind beast and realize what it's doing...

It's listening.
