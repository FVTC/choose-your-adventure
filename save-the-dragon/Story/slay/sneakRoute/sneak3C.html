You carefully circle the beast, sword at the ready, watching for any sign that the creature knows where you are. The Dragon sniffs around, head moving silently, listening, waiting. You round the front and slowly make your way towards the creature. You can feel a heat radiating off of it's body that wasn't there before. You know it's preparing it's final, most dangerous act. If you make a mistake now, it may just be your last.

As you creep ever closer your heart pounds deep in your chest. The rythmic thump echos in your ears, quickly becoming the only thing you can think about.

heart.

heart.

heart.

With that final thought, you plunge your blade deep into the dragon's chest. The beast howls in pain and you push once more to finish off the beast. For just a moment it scrambles to try and claw the hypothetical thorn out of it's heart but, alas, it is too little too late. The beast collapses to the ruins floor with a deafaning thud.

You've done it. You've won.

You drop to your knees in exhaustion, catching your breath in this final moment of reprive. As you sit before the unmoving beast, staring down at your hands, a feeling of sorrow begins to wash over you. Why were you sent here? Why was this Dragon chained up? Why were you attacking it if it was already incapacitated all the way out here in these forest ruins?

All questions it seems you may never get the answers to. You give one last glance at the Dragon, your sword still lodged in it's chest. With a sigh, you turn and begin your long journey back home. The forest can keep it.
