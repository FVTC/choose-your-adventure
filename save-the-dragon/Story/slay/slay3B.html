Your training prepared you for this moment. The heart is the way to go. Too many risks with a creature of unknown origin otherwise.

You nimbly dodge claw and fang, making your way closer and closer until you find yourself nearly directly underneith the beast. Without wasting a single second you jab your trusty blade upwards into the Dragon's underbelly. It roars in pain, claws scrambling at you from all directions. Your grip tightens on your sword as you realize it's firmly lodged between the beast's ribs. You have only a moment to decide- What will you do?
