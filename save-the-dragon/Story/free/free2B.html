Your grip tightens on the sword. You never know, you might need it! You continue marching forward, intent on freeing the growling beast. With each step, the growling grows louder and louder, the Dragon straining more and more against it's chains.

The beast swings a giant clawed foot at you as soon as you're near enough and you just barely jump back in time to avoid getting mauled. You huff in annoyance. Doesn't this creature know you're trying to help it?!

You decide quick is better and rush in under the Dragon to try and break open the chains with your sword but, alas, the chains are too strong. With this failed freeing attempt under your belt, you start to wish you had thought this through a bit more.

Unfortunately for you, you did not. The Dragon reaches under itself, one giant claw just barely snagging your armour as it drags you out and slams you to the ground.
