Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Let it]

You take a few slow steps back before turning and sprinting a few yards away and diving behind a half standing ruin wall. You peek out above the moldy stone bricks and watch as the dragon begins to eminate a bright glowing warmth from it's midsection. A few more heinus gulps later, the beast tilts it's head down and spews what can only be described as lava from it's mouth, directly on the chains that currently keep it captave.

The heat hits your face in a wave and you squint to try and keep your eyes from burning. Eventually you have to duck completely behind the ruinous wall to sheild yourself from the onslaught of heat. After 5 solid minutes of nonstop heat and distruction, the Dragon finally stops. You hear chains falling to the ground and you peak back out from behind the wall.

The dragon has set itself free.

As you step out from behind the stone wall, the Dragon rises to it's full intimidating height. It looks at you, eyes glimmering just as much as they were the moment you first saw it, and it flaps it's gargantuan wings. Two big pumps of it's wings send the creature up into the air. It gives you a little "chirp" in thanks as it soars away.

You smile and your heart feels as light as if it had flown away with the Dragon itself.
