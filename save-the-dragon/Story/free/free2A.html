[Drop The Sword]

You look back at the Dragon, gaze locked perfectly on it's glittering eyes. You raise your arm straight out to the side, sword in hand, and drop your wepon to the ground with a clatter. With a swift kick, you shoo your sword even further away, far enough that you couldn't reach it if you wanted to. Then, you wait...

Seconds pass like hours, sweat beading down your neck as you waid for the best before you to make it's decision.

Are you friend? Or foe.

It only takes a few moments more before you see the Dragon's posture visibly relax. You sigh in relief and start to slowly make your way over to the chains that restrain the creature. They're heavy and thick with no clear way to remove them. What will you do?
