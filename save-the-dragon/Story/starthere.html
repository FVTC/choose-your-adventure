Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

You are a knight.
You have a sword.

You find a dragon caught in a trap. It's tangled in a mess of chains. The chains are thin, but strong, digging deeper into its flesh the more it struggles to free itself. You grip the hilt of the sword at your hip.

Before you can draw your blade, the dragon spots you. Its movements stop, and it looks at you with wide eyes. You need to act now; it's surely about to call on the hellfire in its belly and smite you where you stand.

What do you do?
