Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Slay]

You know what you have to do. It's what knights do after all, slaying dragons.

You can feel the dragon's putrid hot breath wash over you as it watches intently. It knows what you're here to do and it's not planning on letting you complete your mission easily. It rises to it's gnarly, taloned feet, as far as it can, the chains preventing the creature from reaching it's full towering height.

For the briefest of moments you wonder if you're prepared for this. The dragon, even tied down holds a nauseatingly dangerous energy, its every movement sending your heart plumeting into the depths of your gut. But, like a leaf on the wind, the thought and fear quickly fades, your expression hardens and you take a step foreward, new thoughts taking the place of the old.

You remember the countless hours of training that put you here, the strength of your armour, hand crafted by the most skilled craftperson in the village, and the people you swore to protect wating for you back home. Thoughts of cheering peers and damsels pining for your attention flash across your mind. You'll be welcomed home a hero. With a quick breath and a widening of your stance, you're ready to battle the beast that lays before you.

How will you procede?
