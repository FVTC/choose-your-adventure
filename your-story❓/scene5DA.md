# 🛖 Scene 5: The Cabin?

The cabins walls stand around you, dark timber stretching from floor to ceiling, polished smooth and free of dust. A warm glow fills the room, cast by a fireplace crackling merrily against the far wall.

The wooden table stands in the center of the room. A ceramic mug rests beside an open book. Two chairs sit neatly beside it, one pulled back as if someone had only just stood up. And at its center, a delicously inviting pie sits, an appropriate spatula beside it.
