# 🌳 Scene 7: The Meadow

The circle grows larger as you fall toward it.

At first, all you can see is a brilliant white light. Then shapes begin to emerge.

Trees; grass.

You are falling toward a wide, green meadow beneath a clear blue sky, surrounded by a circling of trees. Wildflowers sway gently in the breeze, and sunlight warms the landscape below.

You feel yourself slowing as you approach the ground.

You land softly in the grass.

The moment your feet touch the ground, you hear a voice behind you.

"You're finally here."

You turn around.  
No one is there.

Suddenly fearful you look around.

Then you notice something strange.

There are no shadows.

Not from the trees.

Not from the flowers.

Not even from you.

But in the distance, at the edge of the meadow, stands a small wooden chair.
