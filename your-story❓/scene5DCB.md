# 📖 Scene 5: The Book

You lift the book from the table.

It appears to be an ordinary book.

You try to read the words on the pages, but the letters seem to dance across the page, shifting and twisting before your eyes.

Suddenly, a faint glow shimmers from beneath your fingertips. As the light spreads across the book, the letters settle, rearranging themselves into recognizable words.
