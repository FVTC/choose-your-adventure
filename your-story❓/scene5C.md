# 🛖 Scene 5: The Cabin

You step inside. As your foot lands on the wooden floor... it doesn't.

Your foot continues right through the floor!

Unable to stop yourself you fall forward through the doorway and through the ethereal floor of the cabin.

Your foot lands on wood. But as you fall you stumble forward after your foot lands, crashing onto a wooden floor.
