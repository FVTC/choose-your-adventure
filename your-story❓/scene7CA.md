# 😎 Scene 7: Beachside: Hourglass

Standing in the sand is an hourglass, nearly as tall as you are.

Its wooden frame is worn, and the glass within seems to shimmer in the sunlight.

You watch as the sand falls.

Then, for a moment, it stops.

A few grains rise from the bottom, drifting upward into the upper chamber.

You blink.

The sand falls normally once more.

...Perhaps time isn't quite as straightforward here as you thought.
