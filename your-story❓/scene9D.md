# 🤝 Scene 9: The Hand

It's... It's another hand.

Someone grabs your hand. 

You hear something, maybe a voice, but it's too muffled to make out.

Suddenly you feel a pull.

And then, light floods your vision.

And you look at the individual holding your hand...

Then nothing, just before your eyes can register his features...
