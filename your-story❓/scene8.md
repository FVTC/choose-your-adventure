# 🧑‍💼 Scene 8: The Company

The exit leads you to strange office building.

The dim lights give the place an eerie feeling.

Each light gives off a low hum, which almost calm you in a way.

You travel pass cubicles and old computers

The more you go the less dim the lights become.

A loud screeh zooms across the building.

You jump with fear and cover your eyes.

You hear a beep before you hear what sounds like a gravely voice.

"Come up to my office, you look very familiar and I would like to meet you."

The office begins to shift as cubicles transform into steps to an unknown floating door.

"right this way." The voice proclaims.

You proceed to walk up the stairs.

You open the door, A man sits at is chair facing away from you.

"You finally came!" The man says

As he turns his chair to face you, your vision starts to become hazy.

Desperately, your eyes try to stay awake but you soon pass out.

You open your eyes...
