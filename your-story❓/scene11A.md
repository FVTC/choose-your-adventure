# 😴 Scene 11: Just... A Dream?

You open your eyes. Looking up at the ceiling.

Your mind comes back to you. You're safe.

The previous experiences already fading from your mind as you realize, you're home. You're in bed.

You were dreaming.

And now you wake.

Checking your clock, it's still late enough to rest before you need to get up. 

So you lay your head back down, close your eyes...

...and rest.
