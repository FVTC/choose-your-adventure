# 🛖 Scene 5: The Cabin?

You push yourself off the floor, your head spinning as you try to make sense of what just happened.

The cabin is... different.

The walls are no longer bare, weathered wood. Instead, dark timber stretches from floor to ceiling, polished smooth and free of dust. A warm glow fills the room, cast by a fireplace crackling merrily against the far wall.

The wooden table stands in the center of the room, but it is no longer empty. A ceramic mug rests beside an open book. Two chairs sit neatly beside it, one pulled back as if someone had only just stood up. And at its center, a delicously inviting pie sits, an appropriate spatula beside it. 

Even the windows have changed. Where once there was only a view of the trees, now you see a landscape bathed in the orange light of a setting sun. You could swear the sun had been high in the sky only moments ago.

Behind you, there's only wall. The door...  
It's gone.

The cabin is warm, inviting, almost cozy.

And yet, you cannot shake the feeling that you were never supposed to find it.

Seeing nothing else in the near space, you inspect the items on the table
