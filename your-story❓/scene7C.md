# 😎 Scene 7: Beachside

The circle grows larger as you fall toward it.

At first, all you can see is a brilliant white light. Then shapes begin to emerge.

Sand; water.

You are falling toward a vast beach, stretching along a calm, blue ocean beneath a clear sky. Gentle waves roll toward the shore, and a warm breeze brushes against your skin.

You feel yourself slowing as you approach the ground.

You land softly in the sand.

The moment your feet touch the ground, you hear the gentle crashing of waves.

You look out toward the ocean.

Something about it feels... familiar.

But you can't quite recall why.
