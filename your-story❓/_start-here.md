## "...You open your eyes..."

Your eyeslids flutter, slowly, they open. 

You look and see... nothing? 

You stare into the void, pure nothingness around you. Turning your head, effortlessly, still nothing. You acknowledge the sensation of your skin, but you still see nothing as you raise your hands. You feel like you're floating.
