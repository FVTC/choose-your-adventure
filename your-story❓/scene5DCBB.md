# 📖 Scene 5: The Book

You read the words aloud. 

As you go along, you soon realize that while you're able to read and speak the words, you still don't understand them.

By the type you decide you should stop, it's too late.

Your eyes continue reading and your mouth continues speaking.

Unable to stop yourself, your grip on the book tightens and fear enters your tone, though your diction is unaffected.

As you recite the words on the page, you feel a strange rumbling in your chest, beneath your feet. Then a crack shoots through the stone of the fireplace. Then through the walls.  

Then they seemingly fall outwards. And behind them... a void.

The void slowly creeps forward as you continue speaking. 

You feel your hand turn the page as the passage continues.
