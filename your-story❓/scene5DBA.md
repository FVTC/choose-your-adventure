# 🍵 Scene 5: The Cup

You lift the white ceramic cup. It's warm.

Slowly you bring it's lip to your parched mouth.

Then take a long sip of the liquid.

...

It's amazing! Tasting as if some sort of ambrosia you continue. Drinking the entire cup before you can realize it.

Setting the now empty cup down, you feel no different. Although, you can't get that deliscious taste out of your mind.
