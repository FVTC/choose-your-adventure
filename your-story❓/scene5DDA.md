# 🥧 Scene 5: The Pie

You pick up the silver spatula and carefully lift a slice of pie onto a nearby plate.

You lift the fork and snag a bite.

It's delicious. Better than any pie you can remember having.

You take another bite, then another, until the slice is gone.

For a moment, you simply stand there, savoring the lingering sweetness.

Then, a strange warmth spreads through your stomach.

It isn't unpleasant. Quite the opposite. A comforting sensation washes over you, seeping into your limbs and easing the tension in your shoulders.

You feel... better.

You can't quite explain why, but the cabin seems a little less unsettling now. There's a new Innate Warmth spreading through you.
