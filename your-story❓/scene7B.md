# 🏭 Scene 7: The Complex

After falling you find yourself in an industrial complex.

The smell of smoke and fog encapsulate you to explore.

The sounds of hissing from pipes and flourecsent pipes fill you with dread.

An alarm sounds and voice com goes off.

"All workers must proceed to the exit in an orderly fashion."

A giant door with a countdown towers over you.

More sounds begin to emerge throughout the complex, you start to wonder what will happen if you don't choose to go through the door.

You start to become more fearful as the countdown declines.

You have about 10:00 minutes left to head to the exit.

Your mind urges you to explore further.

But it also tells you to head to the exit.

You decide on...
