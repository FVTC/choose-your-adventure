# 🛖 Scene 5: The Cabin

You grab the door handle and twist it. It's unlocked!

The door swings open, and you peer inside.

The cabin is small, its interior dimly lit by sunlight streaming through the cloudy windows. A wooden table sits in the center of the room, accompanied by two chairs. A stone fireplace rests against the far wall, its hearth cold and empty.

You see dust coating nearly every surface. Yet, strangely, there are no cobwebs. No signs of the pie you had smelled. Nothing to suggest that anything has ever disturbed this place.

The air inside is still. Unnaturally still.

For the first time since opening your eyes, you have somewhere to go.

But something about this cabin feels... wrong.
