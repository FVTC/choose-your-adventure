# 🔥 Scene 6: The Fireplace

You open your eyes once more.

You see nothing.

A sudden fear washes over you. Not the void. Not again!

But... something is different. You can feel this time.

The friction of air against your skin.

You're falling.

You adjust your body to face downward, now falling headfirst. Far ahead of you, you see three circles of light.

Holes in the void?

You feel as though you can direct your fall towards one of them.

You angle yourself towards...
