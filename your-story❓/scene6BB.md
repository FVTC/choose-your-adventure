# 🔥 Scene 6: The Fireplace

Now closer to the fire, you notice a space behind the flames.

Confident that the flames will not hurt you, you crouch down and crawl through the fire, over the glowing coals, and into the archway hidden behind the dancing flames.

You keep your eyes closed as you crawl, the brightness of the flames still an irritant.

As you pass through the archway, an event similar to what happened when you entered the cabin occurs.

Your hand reaches forward and lands on stone. You continue crawling, slowly beginning to open your eyes.

Just as you're almost through, your hands slip. Not on the ground, but somehow, through it.

You fall.
