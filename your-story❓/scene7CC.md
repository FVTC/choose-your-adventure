# 😎 Scene 7: Beachside: Umbrella

A lone beach umbrella stands planted in the sand.

Its faded fabric flutters gently in the breeze, casting a small patch of shade beneath it.

Strangely, despite the warm sunlight, the sand beneath the umbrella feels cold.

You can't quite shake the feeling that someone was sitting here moments ago.

...Perhaps there's more to this place than meets the eye.
