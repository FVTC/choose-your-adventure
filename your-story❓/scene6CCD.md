# 🔥 Scene 6: The Fireplace

The warmth from eating the pie is leaving you.

You slowly feel cooler, and cooler.

The unnatural warmth leaves you.

...

Then, your natural warmth begins fading as well.

As you realize this, you stand, moving back towards the pie, in hopes of regaining your warmth.

You rush, the more and faster you move, the faster the warmth leaves you. 

You get closer, reaching out to the pie, but before you do, the last of your bodily warmth leaves you...
