You arrive at Freddy's as the sun slides behind the buildings. The marquee hums; the windows are dark but for the glow spilling from the arcade machines. Behind the counter, a man in a cheap suit looks up from a clipboard and sizes you in a single, tired blink.

He gestures toward a folding chair. "Name?"

You give it. He nods, then asks the same questions every employer asks and none of the ones you want answered: availability, previous experience, references. His eyes linger on your hands like he's checking for something else.

When you mention the night shift, his mouth twists. "We need someone who can keep to themselves and keep the place secure. Nights are quiet... mostly. Pay's decent for the hours." He pauses, then offers a thin smile. "You comfortable being alone?"

You swallow. The fluorescent lights buzz. Outside, the parking lot seems to hold its breath.

He slides a pen and a single sheet across the counter. "Sign here if you want it. If you walk, that's fine too."
