# Crushed Em!
### ![Crossbow](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSFZ55rYk3KQRLq6DukJAA9jthzFDsCp4Jrvg&s)

You shoot down the chandelier, and it instantly falls down and crushes the monster, leaving a big splatter of green goo all along the floor.

With the monster dead, you look around the room, and you realize that there is a big lever at the end of the room. Once you pull it, a door starts to open.

You realize that you can go outside, but first you go back to the treasure room to get all the gold you want in life. But then you realize something, all the gold is just fools gold! You are upset at first, but then you take a good look at your crossbow.

The skin of it starts to peel off, and you realize it's a golden crossbow! And all the bolts also happen to be made of gold! You're rich!

With the gold you have, you head back home to go and love a wealthy life.

You got the cross bow ending! Congratulations!
But this is just 1 of 3 different endings you can acheive, have you acheived the other endings?
