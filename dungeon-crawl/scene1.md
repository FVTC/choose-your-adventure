# The Alcove
### ![stream](https://www.shutterstock.com/image-vector/mysterious-night-forest-landscape-swamp-600nw-2329365047.jpg)
You have grown exhausted. You have been traveling for several days. You have not seen a single person in what feels like ages. You stop to drink from the stream and rest your weary body.

After you have rested for some time, you see the weather is starting to turn and clouds are rolling in. You decide to look for shelter for the night.

You spot an alcove hidden in the distance and decide to make your way there. When you reach the alcove, you hear rustling behind you.

"You should not go in there" a deep voice states as you turn your body towards the noise, body poised for battle.

"Many have entered in search of its rewards, but none have left." You find an old man with a long walking stick leaning against a tree, pipe in hand.

"And what would you know about this place old man?" you retort, bravado in your tone and fists clenched.

"Fine, don't heed my words. Make your choice, go in blind, or sit and hear my tale." His voice was deep, but his words were enticing.
