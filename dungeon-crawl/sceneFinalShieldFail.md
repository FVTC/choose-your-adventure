# Sheild Don't Cut Through Skin
### ![Game Over](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTVBM1VdXnKpgvqgIr-g92-t7IBZpn7SPwJow&s)

As you strike the scales of the creature, you come to a decisive conclusion to the fight, sheilds can't cut through any type of skin.

The monster says "See? Why did you choose the sheild, you are one of the worst knights I have ever met. You don't even know the first rule to adventuring, pick a weapon that can actually damage someone!"

The monster doesn't even feel like fighting you anymore so he asks you to leave. He takes back the sheild and you leave with no treasure. As you walk home you wonder how things could've been if you used the sheild's ACTUAL purpose.

You live a somewhat redundant life without many twists and turns after that adventure, as you clearly can't be a good knight at all.

You became a carpenter, and spent your days helping the village install carpeting. All the local brats throw rocks at you as you do it.

Perhaps if you had done something different, your life could have been much better. Care to give it another go?
