#### Your Journey Begins
On the day of your 21st birthday, you begin your journey of discovery. You have planned a trek from your small fishing village of Keldahar to the capital city of Silvermoon City. Instead of taking the well worn merchant road, you decide to travel through the forest to explore along the way.

The first few days of your quest have been uneventful. You have made great distance, not knowing what lies ahead.
