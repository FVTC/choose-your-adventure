# The Old Man's Tale
### ![campfire](https://creator.nightcafe.studio/api/og-creation.jpg?id=hovpm5hA6UYbSx6lXA1b)

With a deep sigh, you relax your posture.
"Fine old man, tell me your story" you say as you gather wood from around the trees to make a fire. As you are looking for dry wood to use, you notice there are no tracks from where the man came. You shrug off your wandering thoughts, placing the gathered wood at the opening of the alcove. Keeping your body turned so that you can keep a watchful eye on this mysterious old man and the dark distance of the cave, you start stack the wood. The fire lights itself as if by magic, while you were busy looking for your flint.

Astonished, you look between the fire and the old man, now sitting across from you as if he moved in an instant.

_For many moons, weary travelers as yourself would find themselves at the opening of this very cave. Most could ignore its call, but those who couldn't,_ He took a drag from his pipe _succumbed to the terror it holds...and what that terror protects_

"Terror it holds? Calls to them? you are speaking madness!" You exclaim. All the while you hear a faint, hum that feels like it is pulling on your soul.

_Quiet and listen boy_ The old man's voice deepened. _I know you feel it. tugging ever so softly, like the smell of grandmother's pie in the window. But heed my words. Don't Follow the hum. If you must travel inside, do it at your own risk, but I repeat. Don't follow the hum._

As the last syllable left the old man's mouth, the rain began to pour. The rain fell with a magnifcient force, as if it was clingy to his warning. Watching the rain, you turn to ask the old man a question and find that he is no where to be seen.
