# He Could Eat That?
### ![Game Over](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTVBM1VdXnKpgvqgIr-g92-t7IBZpn7SPwJow&s)

You thrust the sword into his mouth, but in a stunning twist, he bites down on the sword and eats the blade!

You are now left with a handle and nothing else but the handle. The monster laughs at your attempt to beat him, and your cheeks start to blush.

You are so embarresed with what just happened! "How could this happen?" you think to yourself. Then you hit the ground hard. As it turns out, dying of embarressment is actually possible and it's what just happened to you!

The monster uses you as an extra leg on his table, along with 3 other knights he managed to defeat to prop up his table. He pours some victory wine into a chalice, and sits on his throne with a wide grin.

It turns out this monster is actually quite good and resourceful with the knights he defeats. But man, what a terrible way to go out. 

You should definitely try this adventure again, for this is just the worst ending ever! How disappointing!
