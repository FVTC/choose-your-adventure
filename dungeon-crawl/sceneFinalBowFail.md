# Yummy Arrow
### ![Game Over](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTVBM1VdXnKpgvqgIr-g92-t7IBZpn7SPwJow&s)

You shoot your shot at the eyes of the hideous monster, but something quite unexpected happens.

The monster has a much wider mouth then you remember, and he eats the arrow that you just shot him with!

"mmmmmmm, yummy." The monster says mocking your efforts.

It is at this point that you realize you are about to be eaten by him, he is too close to you for you to reload and shoot him again, so you accept your fate.

In his stomach, you light a match, and realize that his stomach is much bigger on the inside than it appears on the outside. You realize that you are stuck here forever. Your crossbow was dropped and is now in his digestive system, so you can't get it back.

With no way out, you simply decide to stay there. You are stuck there until the end of time along with the monster, and you both die at the end of it all. So... mission accomplished?

But that wasn't a satisfying ending! You should try again to get a better ending!
