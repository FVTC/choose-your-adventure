# The Shot Of A Lifetime
### ![Throne Room](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSAQpfDJKG-hP1sWqpo3Ipp_9oxA_xrbqW0Iw&s)
Crossbow in hand, you decide to go straight for the boss room.

You look around the room and see a chandelier from above, nice looking carpeting, a throne, and a mean looking monster who sits in the throne.

He has green skin with scales similar to a snake. Although weirdly enough, he has 6 legs and no arms. He appears to be more spider like in terms of his design. He is currently sitting with 2 legs down, and 4 legs up. He appears to be showing his teeth with a vicious look. His putrid yellow teeth are enough to make you sick. His mouth and eyes appear from the front of him. It makes you wonder how he ever is able to keep his stare at you. He looks at you with his 4 eyes, and you start to shudder in fear from his horrific pose he gives when he stands up. He stands almost 7 feet tall.

"Well, well, well" They say with a sharp grin. "So, you've made it this far on your adventure. It seems this will be the end of your journey however. There is no way to defeat me."I have real sharp reflexes, and these pointy legs will stab you more times than you can count. Prepare for the battle of a lifetime!" He gets down on all 6 legs and approaches you.

You are super worried right now. A big green spider looking creature is coming for you, and you have no idea what to do. You decide that there is only 2 things you can do.

You can either attempt to shoot straight at his eyes in hopes to blind and then attack him. Otherwise, there is a big chandelier at the top of the room that looks like can crush him.

What will you do?
