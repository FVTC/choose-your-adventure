# The Secret He Doesn't Know
### ![Shield](https://v.etsystatic.com/video/upload/q_auto/shields_ahnz1g.jpg)

You throw the shield in the distance ahead of you, and at first the monster stops because he thinks you just made the dumbest decision ever.

But that's when it hits him, literally, and he comes crashing into the wall. He gets absolutely smacked by the wall, and dies right there on the spot. You have defeated the monster!

You return to the treasure room super excited to claim your prize! But there's one problem, all the gold was actually just fools gold. you turn up disappointed until you give the shield you are holding a second look. 

You realize it belongs to Sir Frederick III. You just so happen to know Frederick. You see a big lever at the end of the throne room, you pull it and the exit emerges from it! You go to his cottage that lives in the meadows. You show him the shield and he brims with joy.

He lets you join the adventurers guild and you become one of the greatest knights who ever lived!
Fame and fortune come at you from each adventure you complete, and you become the top knight at the guild!

You got the shield ending! Congratulations!
