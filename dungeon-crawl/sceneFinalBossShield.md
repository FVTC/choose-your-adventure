# Unique Opportunity

You go inside the boss door after grabbing your sheild boomerang contraption, and you are shocked at the decor.

The floor has carpeting, there's a gorgeous chandelier hanging from the ceiling, and there's a big throne at the end of the room looking like it's made of gold. But what appears in front of the throne is what frightens you most.

It is a giant spider looking hideous monster! Nut you notice some strange features on it. For one, it has 6 legs instead of 8 like a spider, it has 4 eyes on the fron of it's face, it's mouth is directly below its eyes, and it's sking is more scaley like a snake, and the scales are also green. As he stands up in front of you, you shudder as he stands almost 7 ft tall!

"I've been expecting y-" He cuts off and looks at what weapon you have in your hand. "A shield? Really? A shield?" "What's wrong with it?" You ask. "Out of all the offensive you could've chose you chose a shield? This is going to be one of the easiest battles of my life!" He laughs maniacally as he stampedes towards you with thunderous movement.

The ground begins to shake around you, you feel uneasy, but you courage persists as you get ready to attack. You take note that this is not just any ordinary shield, and you make a quick decision on what to do.

You know that your 2 choices are either to strike the monster with the shield, or to throw it off into the distance. It's your move, what do you do?
