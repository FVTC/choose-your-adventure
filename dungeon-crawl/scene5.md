# The Dark Room
### ![darkroom](https://www.shutterstock.com/image-vector/night-medieval-stone-castle-cartoon-600nw-2284394047.jpg)

You shake off your nerves and keep moving. You travel for what seems like miles, all the while your torch doesn't appear to be losing any fuel. You start to notice changes in the wall. What was once jagged cave walls have turned to large smooth bricks. The floor large symeterical tiles. The humming has danced to life. It is all you can hear and your stomach is grows tighter and tighter and you push along the tiled floor. At the center of the room the humming stops instantly. At the same time, torches on the walls light one by one. You find yourself in an octagon shaped room. In the middle of the room you find a sword sticking out of a scuplted marble base.

You found the treasure! Excitedly you run to the sword. You climb up the marble base and place your hands on the hilt. You hear faint snarling and scratching, but can't find it source. Your nerves are now thrashing inside you. Something doesn't feel right. You take a deep breath and you

Pull...

and Pull...

and Pull...

The sword appears stuck. 

With all your might you heave on the hilt. A crackling sound emits from the sword and it releaseses its hold on the marble base. The torches around the room shine with intensity. You appear around the room, sword in hand. With the lighting increase, you can see where the sounds were coming from. The walls were lined with iron cage doors. Inside the cages you see several beasts of different types staring back at you. You also see the entrance you came from, and another way out.
 The doors are starting to slowly close from the ceiling.

What do you do?
