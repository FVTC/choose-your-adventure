# The Entrance
### ![entrance](https://thumbs.dreamstime.com/b/dark-cave-light-end-tunnel-rocks-ground-generative-ai-dark-cave-light-end-tunnel-280267349.jpg)

You travel far. Following twists and turns. You follow a faint light in the distance, no hum to be heard. As the light gets brighter, you see door at the top of a tall set of stairs. Relieved. You begin to climb. At the top of the stairs you find yourself at the foot of a great gold door. On the door etched in an ancient language:

___ONLY THE STRONG SURVIVE___
