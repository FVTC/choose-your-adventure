# Rested and Ready
### ![caveroom](https://www.shutterstock.com/image-vector/cave-painting-on-wall-cracks-600nw-2158825541.jpg)
Drip....Drip... You wake up to water dripping from the roof of the cave. Groggy, you rub your eyes. Gathering your bearings, you take in your surroundings. Next to you, the fire is still burning bright, the wood appears unburned. You shake your head, trying to acclimate to the foreign environment. 

You reach out to grab your bag. With a single hand you reach, into the bag and retrieve your waterskin. Taking a gulp, the cool rush of water satiates the void of moisture in your throat. The musty smell of the dark deep ahead of you fills your nose. You exchange your waterskin for the last of your dried meat left in your belongings. Chewing the tough meat, you survey your surroundings again.

Behind you sits the entrance of the cave. You contemplate leaving; forgetting what you experienced last night. But you can't, the humming feels stronger than the night before. Opposite the entrance, sits two jagged offshoots, going in seemingly separate directions. You grasp the end of a stick out of the fire. Wielding it as a torch. you travel further into the abyss to investigate. The wall between the two openings is covered in ancient carvings. As you are admiring the detail, tracing the carvings with your hands, you can feel the hum pulling you towards the left passage.

Troubled, you have to decide.
