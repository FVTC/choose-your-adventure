# Enter The Cave
### ![caveentrance](https://t4.ftcdn.net/jpg/02/37/65/53/360_F_237655386_Kk9P84HC9pbPyX2dW7Sz0h2QAH5UpUC0.jpg)

Branch in hand you enter the mouth of the cave. _Stupid Old Man, I don't need his advice,_ you think to yourself as you step onto the hard,uneven floor of the opening. ___Suit your self boy, good luck... your gonna need it___ you hear the Old Man's voice as if it was in your head. Heart racing, you spin around searching, stick in hand. Seeing no harm, you slowly lower the stick and chuckle to yourself.

You sit down to think about the encounter. As you are pondering what just happened, you see an old campfire a few yards further into the cave. Your body shivers from the cold air and you hoist yourself up to inspect the wood pile. As you kneel next to it, the fire blazes to life. You throw yourself back onto your hands to keep from getting burned. As you fall, your stick bounces out of the cave. Rain starts pouring.

You sit up and dust off your hands. You feel like you are going crazy. Trying to make sense of what is all going on, you hear a faint humming coming from inside the cave. You feel overwhelmed by this pulling feeling and the events of the day.
