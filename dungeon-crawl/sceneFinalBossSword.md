You enter the boss door, and are met with a most lavish of scenary. this room has complete carpeting, a big thronw, a big chandelier, and a great marble walling. You wonder how this si possible since most of the cave has just been rocks and dirt. But then, you see him.

A great big monster give you a shifty stare and gives you a quick overview. You take this opportunity to get a good look at him as well. He has many distinct features, such as a spider like structure, green scales that surround his body, 4 eyes, and 6 pointy legs. Definitely not the most friendly face you've seen today.

He starts to talk "Oh ho ho! So you are the hero that has come to take my treasure? Well I won't allow it. All the gold is mine mine mine!" He starts to give a confident look, a look that make syou think "Oh no, he's already won hasn't he." The monster then says "You'll be lucky to defeat me! I've lived hear for over 200 years, and no one has ever defeated me! Although I must say, old age has definitely worn me down. My scales aren't as fresh as they used to be." He charges at you full force with his 6 legs. You start panicking, you've never had to fight anyone this tough before. But then you get a good idea of what to do after what he just said. You decide that there are 2 possibilities of what you are going to do.

You can either chop off all of his legs so that he can't move, and then go for it. Or alternatively, you can try and get your sword through his mouth for a sick and somewhat brutal kill. So which will it be?
