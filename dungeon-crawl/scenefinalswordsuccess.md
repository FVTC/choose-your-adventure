You take a quick sweep amongst all his legs! Immediately all his legs become decapitated from his body, and he is just left with his body and head.

You ask for his last words and he just replies with "I will always be 6 steps a head of you! Ahahahahahahaha!" You are quite annoyed with the joke he just told, so you quickly end him before his laugh gets to you.

After you defeated the monster, you go back to the treasure room in excitement for the treasure you will find there! But you are quite disappointed, as all the gold that was in that room was just fools gold. 

You reluctantly turn back in anger and disappointment. But after a quick look at the sword you realize something. "No, it can't be." You think to yourself. After pulling it, you notice an exit door that appears out of the cave.

You quickly go to the king's castle. It has been years since anyone has been there, but you just have to do something there. You go to the crown room, insert the jewel in the crown, and you realize that this is what you have been waiting for.
