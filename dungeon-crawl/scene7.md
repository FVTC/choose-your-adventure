# Narrowly Escape
### ![snakesword](https://media.fab.com/image_previews/gallery_images/02e95484-4c36-4186-a307-882e31695880/c966d146-2082-4663-9e88-272c0b49f9e7.jpg)
You race to the new opening and jump through its gate right on time. You pant heavily, as you stand to your feet. Holding the sword in front of you to inspect it, you are puzzled. The sword that was once in your hand is starting to change form. You feel it starting to wiggle around. You hold your torch close to it and watch as it turns into a snake.

___YIKES___ You yelp as you drop the snake. It slithers away. You have been fooled! Frustrated and lost you continue you on your journey down the dark halls. You see a faint light in the distance and you pick up your pace.
