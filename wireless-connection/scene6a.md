# 🏨 Scene 6A: The Hotel Penthouse

After a while of rushing through the neon streets of the main districts, you and Lyric finally get back on track to follow the Ghost Rats and reach the luxurious hotel. You shuffle through the revolving doors and charge to the stairwell, where you hear faint footsteps fade out.

You quickly take the stairs up to the top floor, and find a group of wealthy-looking people hanging out in the penthouse, gossiping and drinking expensive champagne.

All of a sudden, you are both ambushed by Redmark PMC soldiers, wishing to eliminate you as an "obsolete robot," along with Lyric as an "unauthorized runner." They corner you and Lyric, and shoot you two out the glass window and fall all the way to the bottom, right on top of a degrading mattress floating in a drainage canal. You go blank...
