# 💣 Scene 3A: Save the Person!

Your protective instincts kick in and you decide to throw a grenade at the mech to save the person under attack. You quickly pull out a stun grenade from your utility belt and toss it at the mech. The grenade explodes with a bright flash and a loud bang, and it disorients the mech for a moment, giving you a chance to grab the person and take them into your hovering police car.

The person's name is Lyric. You can't quite tell what their gender is, but they have black hair with yellow streaks, and is wearing a black t-shirt with artistic graffiti on it, along with baggy jeans and metallic boots.

You both drive forward, but that intense battle made you lose track of the Ghost Rats! Both of you are in the middle of one of the main districts, and see a slick, neon-choked nightclub in the distance. Maybe someone there knows directions...
