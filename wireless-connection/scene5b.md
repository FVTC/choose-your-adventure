# 🍻 Scene 5B: Conversation with the Bartender

You and Lyric approach the bar, and the cyborg bartender greets you with a gravelly voice. He has a weathered face, bronze cybernetic upper head resembling a skull, and a black gruff beard. He introduces himself as David Murray, and says you look familiar... and that you remind him of a prototype he's blueprinted while working for Project Beacon, an initiative to create a fully-automated utopian city. However, it had been bought out by ApeX, and he was then drafted to fight in the War in Canada.

You mention that you're after the Ghost Rats, and David reveals they were actually formed as a rebellion group after the CEO of ApeX, Joe Slingbang, bought out Project Beacon and took over the city. He then points in the direction where the Ghost Rats were last spotted: the luxurious hotel further in the main districts, presumably headed for the top floor where the wealthy hang out.

You thank David for the information and hit the road.
