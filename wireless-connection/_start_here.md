# 🛜 Wireless Connection

The year is 2090 in Platinum City. This colossal, neon labyrinth is a corporatocratic city-state ruled by a megacorporation and conglomerate known as ApeX, where the uber rich enjoy the synthetic, euphoric lifestyle of the main districts, and the poor face cutthroat struggle and survival in "The Outskirts," where violent crime and gangs rule with a literal iron fist.

Even for the lucky residents in the main districts, life is locked behind a subscription provided by Athena, the supercomputer that runs the whole city, including housing, services, and even androids. Towering, zig-zagging neon skyscrapers show the horizon that it's the city of the future, but poverty, crime, and corruption lie beneath the slick, shiny surface.

What the city doesn't know, however, is that there are secrets buried deep about the city's true origins...
