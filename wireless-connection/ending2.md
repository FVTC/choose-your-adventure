# ☠️ Ending 2 - Obsolete and Fallen

You run upstairs to the hotel's glass penthouse. You see no one suspicious at first. However, Redmark PMC android soldiers corner you as an "obsolete robot" and terminate you on the spot. You fall out the window all the way to the bottom in an alleyway. Athena shuts down after your fall, and so do you.

There is no one to save you, and no one to mourn you. You are just another forgotten casualty of the gang wars in the Outskirts, and the city moves on without you.

## 💀 The End
