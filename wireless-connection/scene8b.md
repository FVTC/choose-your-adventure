# 📦 Scene 8b: Hidden on Board

After quietly sneaking onto the cargo ship and waiting for what feels like forever, you feel the ship start moving. Once loaded into Slingbang's rocket from the back, you navigate through the claustrophobic rocket chamber. Crawling out of the chamber, you notice Joe Slingbang, flexing his cybernetics into his smartphone and onto a broadcast. 

His cybernetics look advanced and fighting him may not result in a victory.. there may be another way of stopping him, however.
