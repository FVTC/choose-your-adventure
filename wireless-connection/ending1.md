# 🚚 Ending 1 - Like the Trash You Are Cleaning Up

Whatever you were going to do, Athena shuts down during your shift at the holographic beach, and so do you. You are dispensed like the trash you were cleaning up, and the city forgets about you as quickly as it forgets about the garbage. You are left to rot in the landfill, and your only company is the rats that scurry around you.
