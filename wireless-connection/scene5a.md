# 🤖 The Android Woman

You and Lyric approach the brightly-colored android. She has long, flowing purple and green hair, and her body is scattered with neon pink and red lights.

You ask her where the Ghost Rats may have gone, but she stares at you for a couple of seconds and shouts into her microphone:

> "ENEMY SPOTTED! ALERTING K1SS SAN! ALERTING K1SS SAN!"

That is when you realize she's a spy for GlittR BombZ, and soon enough, the leader of the gang, K1SS San, fashionably enters the club with her entourage of holographic duplicates and lethal laser katana, which even has a microphone at the end of it!

What do you do?
