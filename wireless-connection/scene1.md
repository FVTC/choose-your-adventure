# 🖥️ Scene 1: Athena's Chamber

You are a robot policewoman named Alyssa guarding Athena the supercomputer, inside its chamber at the headquarters of ApeX.

You get the news from your chief on your personal comm:

> 🔉 "The Platinum City Police Department has had its funds cut. We are reassigning you to clean up litter on a holographic beach nearby. We don't want to fight a losing battle anymore."

However, you spot gang activity in the Outskirts on your scanner! It's the Ghost Rats! They are a street gang of human hackers who have a mission to shut down Athena, which you depend on for your livelihood! You want to stop them, but you also want to keep your job.
