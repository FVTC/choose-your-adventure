# 💿 Ending 3 - A New Life

Taking just several moments to harvest a small handful of the non-damaged andriod CPU's, after which you make your way to a repairman's shack several blocks away. You don't know how long it took as you shut down for the CPU replacement, but after powering back on you find that the CPU has been successfully swapped with the new one from an andriod.

Leaving the shop, you now begin your life as a rogue robot.
