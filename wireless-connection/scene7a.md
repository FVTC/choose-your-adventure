# 🚀 Scene 7a: Awakened

You wake up in what appears to be the entrance of an active space station, and Lyric has saved you with something they have found! It was a small prototype of Athena, made by the original head of Project Beacon, who wanted people to power their own robots with it.

Lyric tells you more about Joe Slingbang, the man behind everything, and how he is planning his twentieth mission to Mars for a "PR stunt."

However, you also notice you're surrounded by the Ghost Rats, but Lyric insists they know the truth about Project Beacon now. A part of you doesn't trust them, and you see a hovering cargo in the distance with the "ApeX Space" logo on it, which may be your best bet to get to Slingbang's rocket solo.
