
# 🗺️ Scene 12B: Wanderers

You agree to the princess’s suggestion but tell her that you do not wish to return to the castle due to your bounty and distrust of the king. You guide your horse in the direction of the nearest town. The princess is conflicted -- she looks forward to the adventure that awaits her but will miss her home. The two of you find a lodge to safely spend the night in. 

Years pass with you on the run, and while you find yourself having fun going on adventures with the girl, moving from town to town, there is a looming sense of danger. The princess regrets worrying her father, as she has become a missing person, and at any point you may be accused of kidnapping and executed by the royal guards. 

You and the princess have successful adventures together and gather an impressive hoard of wealth, but you may never know peace from the anxious thoughts of capture in your future.<br><br>
