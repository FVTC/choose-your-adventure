
# 🔊 Scene 6: Following the Tapping Sound

You follow the tapping sound until you find yourself in the Engineering Section. You carefully climb out of the vent and shine your flashlight around the room.

Everything is covered in twisted wires and bits of soft white and yellow.

Then something else catches your eye. The emergency lights illuminate the room just enough for you to make it out. On the far side of the room, someone has taken a laser tool and burned something onto one of the walls:

> ✏️ “022722”

You try to contact your teammate on the radio, but you are interrupted by the sound of frantic footsteps.

> 💬 “I found something in the lower…wait. Did you hear that?”

Before you can get a reply from your teammate, the reactor core starts firing. It quickly overloads and explodes, the blast knocking you unconscious.
