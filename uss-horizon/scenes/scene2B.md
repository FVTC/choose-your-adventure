
# 🕳️ Scene 2B: The Vent

You drop into a narrow maintenance vent. The air is colder here and smells faintly sweet, almost like wet feathers.

Scratched into the metal wall is a crude drawing of something with a round body and a long neck. Next to it someone wrote:

> “It’s smaller than we thought. Still dangerous.”

A soft *tap-tap-tap* echoes from deeper in the vent.
