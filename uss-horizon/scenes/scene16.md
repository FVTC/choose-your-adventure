
# 🦆 Scene 16: Going Home

You scoop Kevin up. He is warm, light, and surprisingly calm for a duck who has spent twenty years in a freezer.

The AI is silent for a long moment. Then every door on the way to the docking bay slides open, one after another.

> 💬 “You didn’t treat him like a tool. That is more than the last crew ever managed. Take him home. The planet below is where he was taken from. The signal was never a warning. It was Kevin, calling for someone to come and get him.”

You carry Kevin through the corridor, past the scratched walls and the shattered lab. Somewhere in the vents, something small answers his quack with a soft, hopeful peep. A second, smaller duck waddles out and follows close behind.

Aboard your shuttle, you set course for the unexplored planet. Through the viewport, the Horizon’s lights go dark, one by one, as if the ship is finally at rest.

Kevin quacks once, gently, and settles into your lap. For the first time in twenty years, the signal falls silent.
