
# 🧊 Scene 14: Leaving It Sealed

You step back from the containment unit. The thing inside stops moving the moment your light leaves it.

The AI speaks again, quieter than before.

> “Wise. The signal cannot leave the ship while the unit remains closed. But it can still use the rest of us.”

Somewhere deeper in the station a door slams shut. The lights in the lab flicker and die one by one until only your flashlight is left.

You’re running out of safe places to stand.
