
# 👁️ Scene 6A: Waking Up

You wake up to your teammate calling your name over the radio. At first, you think it’s your imagination, but then you realize they are here and looking for you.

> 💬 “I’m here… don’t move too fast. It’s tracking sound.”

Your teammate stops in their tracks when your attacker disappears into a hole in the floor that was covered by a big metal piece.

You try to breathe in, but it hurts. You scrunch your eyes closed as you gather up your strength to speak again.

> 💬 “If I don’t make it to you, the override codes are in the captain’s quarters. Don’t let the AI finish whatever it’s doing.”

You open your eyes to look at your teammate for what may be the last time, but they are gone.

What will you do?
