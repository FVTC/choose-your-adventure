
# 🖥️ Scene 2: The Command Bridge

You make your way up a narrow staircase toward the command bridge. The lights flicker overhead, and the sound of metal creaking echoes throughout the ship. When you enter the bridge, you find abandoned control stations, shattered screens, and a captain's chair slowly rotating in the darkness.

You access the central console and begin reviewing the ship's logs. Most of the files are corrupted, but one recording catches your attention.

> 💬 "Day 147. The signal is getting stronger. The crew is experiencing strange behavior, and the ship's AI has started locking doors without authorization. If anyone finds this recording, do not trust the computer..."

Suddenly, the console flashes red.

> ⚠️ "WARNING: Unauthorized access detected. Security lockdown initiated."

The bridge doors slam shut behind you. On a nearby monitor, a shadowy figure appears in the corridor outside. You try to contact your teammate, but your radio only produces static.
