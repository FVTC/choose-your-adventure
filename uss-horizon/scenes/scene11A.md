
# 📡 Scene 11A: Faint Signal

You get a weak signal. Your teammate’s voice is barely there.

> “I found something in the lower… wait. Did you hear that?”

In the background you catch a short, sharp sound. It could be metal. It could be something else entirely.

Then the channel fills with static and drops.

The containment unit behind you gives a soft *thunk*, like whatever is inside just shifted its weight.
