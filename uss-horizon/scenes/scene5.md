
# 📡 Scene 5: Making Contact with Your Crewmate

You switch through every channel the ship still has. Most are dead. One finally opens with a burst of static.

Your teammate’s voice comes through, strained and quiet.

> “I’m in engineering. Power’s partially back, but the AI keeps sealing me off. I saw something move past the observation window. It wasn’t human.”

The line crackles. You hear rapid footsteps on their end, then silence for a few seconds.

> “Don’t trust the computer. And don’t go to the lab alone. Meet me at the—”

The signal drops.

You’re alone again on the locked bridge.
