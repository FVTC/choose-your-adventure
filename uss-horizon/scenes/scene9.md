
# 📡 Scene 9: Calling Out

You call your teammate’s name into the dark engineering bay.

No answer at first. Then a weak reply comes from somewhere near the reactor.

> “I’m here… don’t move too fast. It’s tracking sound.”

You hear them shift, then a sharp intake of breath. Something metal scrapes across the floor between you.

The radio crackles again, quieter this time.

> “If I don’t make it to you, the override codes are in the captain’s quarters. Don’t let the AI finish whatever it’s doing.”

The line goes dead.
