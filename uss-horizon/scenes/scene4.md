
# 🔓 Scene 4: Forcing the Doors

You override the lock. The doors grind open halfway and stick.

The corridor beyond is quiet for a few seconds. Then the emergency lights shift and you catch the edge of something tall and thin sliding into a side passage. It doesn’t move like a person.

Every working screen suddenly shows the same line:

> “You were warned.”

The AI isn’t pretending anymore.
