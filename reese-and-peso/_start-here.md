# The Adventures of Reese & Peso

## The Open Window

It’s twilight in the neighborhood — the hour when porch lights flicker on and shadows stretch long across the lawns. From your cozy perch on the windowsill, you, Reese, watch the world outside with your huge, gleaming eyes.

Peso, your young apprentice, is curled on the couch behind you, batting lazily at a feather toy. The air smells faintly of rain, and something beyond the street catches your attention — a flicker of light in the forest beyond the cul-de-sac. It pulses once. Twice. Then vanishes.

Peso perks up. “Did you see that, Reese? It was like the stars came down!”

Your tail twitches. You’ve heard stories about that forest — the whispering woods. Most cats don’t go near it. But the light calls to your adventurous heart.

You leap down from the sill.
