# Scene 1: Into The Forest

The window slides open with a soft creak — you’ve mastered that trick long ago. With a graceful leap, you land silently on the cool grass outside. The air hums faintly, alive with crickets and the smell of damp earth.

Behind you, Peso lets out a small mew. He dutifully follows close.

The faint light shimmers again beyond the street, deeper now — hovering just inside the tree line of the forest. It’s not like any firefly you’ve seen. It glows a soft, steady gold, like sunlight caught in a jar.

You pad quietly down the street, your paws making no sound, sticking to the shadows, dipping beneath shrubbery, slinking through gardens lining neighboring houses.

As you near the trees, the world seems to hold its breath. The glow moves once more — beckoning you deeper.

You stand at the forest’s edge. The familiar smells of the neighborhood fade behind you, replaced by the scent of pine, moss, and mystery.
