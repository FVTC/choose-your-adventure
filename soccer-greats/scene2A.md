# 🎯 Scene 2A: Messi's Close-Control Challenge

Messi sets up a course of cones, then asks you to dribble through it while two defenders close in. Your first attempt ends with the ball rolling away. On the next try, you slow down, look up, and slip through a narrow gap.

You can use your new touch to create a chance for a teammate or test it in a fast street-football game.
