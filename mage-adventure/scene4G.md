# Scene 4G — Ending: Knife at the Fountain (Neutral)

The scribe arrives shaking. “Your Archmage leads them,” he whispers. An arrow answers him. Guards pour in; the city swallows your name.

You leave your robes in a gutter and vanish into the roads, a mage no more.
