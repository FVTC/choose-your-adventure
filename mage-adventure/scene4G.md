# Scene 4G — The Midnight Meeting

You wait by the fountain, the moon casting silver ripples over the water. The scribe arrives, cloaked and trembling. “They’re within the council,” he whispers. “Your Archmage… he’s not fighting the Veilborn — he’s leading them.”

Before you can react, a dagger strikes from the shadows. The scribe collapses. Guards rush in, accusing you of murder.
