# Scene 5G — The Chase Through Elysreach

You burst into the streets, chasing the fleeing elf. She darts into a market, vanishing in the crowd. When you reach the city gates, you find her standing there, waiting.

“Still following orders?” she asks. “Or do you ever think for yourself?” She tosses you a fragment identical to your shard — but black as night.
