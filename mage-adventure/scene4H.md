# Scene 4H — Ending: The Study of Ash (Doom)

You break into the Archmage’s chambers. Letters blaze with the Veilborn sigil — then ignite on their own. Behind you: a calm voice, a binding spell, a black sleep.

You wake as his instrument. Your hands do his work, your mouth speaks his lies.
