# Scene 2A — Oath on Broken Stone

You kneel on cracked marble. The shard hums against your skin — not bright, not dark, just hungry. “I’ll bring it back,” you say, and the words taste like iron.

The Archmage points east. “The Whispering Woods. Tracks vanish there.” He does not meet your eyes.
