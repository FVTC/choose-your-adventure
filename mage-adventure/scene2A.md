
# Scene 2A — The Oath of Light

You kneel before the Archmage, clutching the shard tightly. “I will recover the Crystal, no matter the cost.” The words burn with purpose. The Archmage nods solemnly, his robes flickering with fading enchantment.

He gives you a rune-inscribed satchel and a map pointing toward **the Whispering Woods**, a place said to warp both time and sanity. “The thief was last seen heading east,” he warns. “Trust no one who speaks too kindly — for the darkness wears many faces.”

As you step through the academy gates, the once vibrant sky seems duller, and the wind whispers your name.
