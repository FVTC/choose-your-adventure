# Scene 5B — The Forest’s Bargain

You flee, gasping, until a tree with a face of bark and flame blocks your way. It speaks, voice deep as thunder. “The shard you carry was born of me. Will you return what was stolen, or repeat your master’s greed?”

Its eyes burn like dying suns. You realize the forest itself remembers the crystal.
