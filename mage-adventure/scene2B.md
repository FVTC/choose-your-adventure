# Scene 2B — The Archmage’s Secret

You glare at the Archmage. “You knew this was coming, didn’t you? Who would dare steal something so sacred?”

His silence speaks louder than any denial. Finally, he confesses: “The crystal’s power is unstable. I sent it away — to protect it — but someone intercepted my spell. It may have fallen into the hands of the **Veilborn**, a cult seeking to reshape the world.”

He gives you a small crystal shard — warm to the touch, pulsing faintly. “Find them, before they learn to control it. If they do, Eldor’s light will be extinguished.”
