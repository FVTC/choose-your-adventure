# Scene 4A — The Stranger Unmasked

You step forward, demanding answers. The hooded figure turns — revealing an elf with glowing eyes and a scar running across her cheek. “So, the apprentice comes chasing legends,” she sneers. “The Crystal is more than your masters ever told you.”

The lights around you begin to fade into shadows that twist into shapes — beasts of mist and malice. “Run, or be consumed by what your order created.”
