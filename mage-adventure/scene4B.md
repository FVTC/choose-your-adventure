# Scene 4B — The Whispering Path

You heed the warning and flee deeper into the woods. The trees seem to shift and rearrange themselves, forming a path that leads to a crystalline pond. When you kneel to drink, your reflection stares back — but it smiles before you do.

Suddenly, a vision flashes before your eyes — a temple beneath the mountains, and a hand clutching the stolen Crystal. You awaken gasping, the forest silent once more.
