# Scene 3C — The Path of Shadows

You travel for days until the mountains rise like jagged blades against the horizon. The wind howls through the cliffs, carrying whispers of long-dead spirits. The Veilborn cult is said to dwell within these peaks, worshipping the Crystal’s dark reflection.

At night, you spot firelight flickering from a cave entrance. Figures in bone masks chant around a glowing crystal — not Eldor’s, but a corrupted imitation. One of them turns toward you, as if sensing your presence.
