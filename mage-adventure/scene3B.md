# Scene 3B — The Guard’s Suspicion

You arrive at the guard post in the border town of Elysreach. Captain Thorne greets you with suspicion. “An apprentice mage wandering alone during a crisis? You’ll forgive me if I don’t believe this is coincidence.”

He gestures toward a detained traveler in the corner — a cloaked elf with cold, intelligent eyes. “Caught her near the eastern road. Claims she’s innocent. Maybe she knows something about your crystal.”

Do you trust her… or the captain?
