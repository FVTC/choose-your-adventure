# Scene 5A — The Battle in the Clearing

You stand firm as the shadows lunge. Your shard blazes with white fire, burning the corruption around you. The elf’s eyes widen — surprise, maybe respect. “So you can still wield its light,” she says.

When the last shadow fades, she steps closer. “The Veilborn are not wrong — the crystal was never meant to be bound. Magic dies when owned.” She offers her hand. “Come with me, and you’ll see.”
