# Scene 5C — Vision of the Peak

Following the vision, you climb for days through the freezing peaks. One night, a ghostly wolf approaches your camp, eyes glowing blue. It drops a piece of parchment before vanishing — a rune map showing a hidden temple.

The path ahead splits: one way is quicker but cursed, the other long but safe.
