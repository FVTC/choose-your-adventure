
## 🌅 Scene 1: A thief calls to Adventure
# Scene 1 — The Shattered Dawn

The morning light filters through the arched windows of Eldorhaven Academy. You wake to the echo of panicked footsteps and the sharp clang of the tower bells — a sound reserved only for catastrophe. When you rush to the Grand Hall, you find the Archmage himself trembling before the shattered pedestal where the **Crystal of Eldor** once rested. The source of all magic in the realm is gone.

He sees you, eyes filled with grief. “Apprentice,” he says hoarsely. “Someone has stolen the crystal. Without it, our spells will fade, and the world will crumble into chaos.”

He presses a faintly glowing shard into your hand — the last fragment of the crystal. “Find the thief. Restore the balance… or embrace the darkness.”

Do you take up the quest willingly, or demand to know more before you risk your life?
