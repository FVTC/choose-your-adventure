# Scene 1 — The Shattered Dawn

Bells wail over Eldorhaven Academy. The pedestal that once held the **Crystal of Eldor** is cracked like bone, its light gone. Spells fizzle in the air — the world is already dimming.

The Archmage shoves a warm fragment into your hand. “Find the thief. Restore balance… or let the world burn.” His eyes are too calm.
