# :fork_and_knife: Scene 2C — The Kitchen

The kitchen is exactly how you remember it as a child and where the best memories of your Grandmother were made. The smells of her baking all come back to you as you enter- Homemade bread, cinnamon, cherry pie. As you are busy reminiscing, you almost don't see Uncle Ray standing at the counter. He appears to be cleaning a mug, but oddly, it doesn’t look dirty.

He jumps slightly when he sees you.

Fumbling, he says, “Oh, I didn’t hear you come in.” 

You notice:

- Mud on his boots.
- A small cut on his hand.
- A toolbox open on the table.

Ray claims he hasn’t left the house today. You are not so sure.

As you deciding if you should proceed with Ray, you hear a noise coming from the back door. Is someone else here?
