# :deciduous_tree:Scene 3C — The Family Tree Clue

You pull the binder from the shelf and flip through the pages. It’s a detailed family tree your grandmother kept updated for decades.

But something is wrong.

A name has been scratched out. Not erased — violently crossed out with thick black ink. You can still make out the first letter: **R**.

Uncle Ray?

You turn the page and find a handwritten note tucked inside:

> “The locket must stay hidden until the truth comes out.”

Your pulse quickens. Was someone in the family was involved?

You keep reading:

> "The secret must remain behind the door."
