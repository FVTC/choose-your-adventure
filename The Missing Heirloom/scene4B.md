# :door: Scene 4B — The Secret Door

You are standing in the hall, observing, when you notice a narrow passageway that you found through the wall panel. The passageway smells of old concrete and dust and has one single hanging light. The light is swaying slightly as if someone passed through moments ago.

You decide to switch gears and you step inside.

The hallway is tight, barely wide enough for one person. The walls are lined with shelves containing old books, boxes, and objects wrapped in cloth. Your grandmother clearly used this space for something important — but why hide it?

Halfway down the passage, you find a small table with a lantern and a stack of envelopes. The top envelope is addressed to **you**.

Your hands shake as you open it.

> “If you are reading this, it means the locket is no longer safe.  
> Trust your instincts.  
> Not everyone deserves the truth.”

You thought, “She knew the locket was at risk.”

At the end of the passage is a wooden door with a brass handle. Fresh footprints lead directly to it.
