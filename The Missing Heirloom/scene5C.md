# :running::running:Scene 5C — The Chase 

You burst out the back door. It is dark and cold. The intruder is running across the yard, with their dark coat flapping in the wind behind them.  You are out of shape, the long, wet grass is slowing you down, and you can't catch your breathe due to the cold, but you persist as adrenaline pushes you forward.

You yell, “Stop!” 

The intruder doesn’t look back and keeps running.

They jump over the garden fence with little difficulty, which leaves you in shock. You follow, not as agile, as you finally land on the other side. The figure darts toward the old shed — the same shed your grandmother always kept locked.

A glint catches your eye. Something metallic falls from the intruder’s pocket and lands in the mud. You don’t stop to pick it up.

You’re close now — close enough to hear their ragged breathing.

The intruder stumbles. This is your chance.
