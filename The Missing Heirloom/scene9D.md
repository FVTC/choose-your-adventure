# :eyeglasses: Scene 9D — Search the Inside 

 

You have your friends and family at the house- ready to search the inside. 

 

Teams divide, looking through every nook and cranny. 

 

After a few hours of searching, you hear a scream. 

 

Running downstairs, you see Cousin Betty in the foyer next to Uncle Ray. 

 

She begins yelling: 

 

> "I walked in and saw him! I think he has the locket! I think he is putting the locket back! I saw him take it out of his pocket!" 

 

You say: 

> "Uncle Ray, is this true?" 

 

Uncle Ray proceeds to say: 

>"This is a big misunderstanding. I promise you that's not what happened!"
