# :flashlight:Scene 6B — Search for Object

You saw the intruder laying on the ground, scrambling to get back up and run. You realized you do not have the strength to tackle or fight them, so you decide to focus your efforts on the shiny metal object that has fallen from them during the chase. 

You go back to the house for your flashlight and proceed down the path the intruder took, searching the wet ground and mud piles. 

After hours of searching and even going back out the next morning to search in morning, you are unable to retrieve the object.
