# :older_man: Scene 10A — Interview with Harold 

 

You sit with Harold in his kitchen. He appears friendly and helpful as he offers you a glass of water. 

 

He makes you feel more at ease, so you decide to tell him exactly what you are looking for and why you came to his property. 

 

He tells you how fond he was of my Grandmother and is willing to help in any way he can, and he says he thinks he can. 

 

Harold goes on to explain that he went out to get his mail and found something in his box. No envelope, no letter, no details.  

 

Confused by this, he says he brought it inside. 

 

You want to see this, for sure, and Harold offers to get it.
