# :house: Scene 1 — Arrival at the Old House

The rain has just stopped when you pull up to your late grandmother’s old house. The place looks exactly as you remember it—quiet, worn, and full of secrets. You step inside, expecting dust and memories.

But instead, you find something unsettling.

The display case in the front hall, where your grandmother always kept her silver heirloom locket, is empty. The velvet stand is overturned. A faint scratch marks the glass.

The heirloom is gone.

Someone has been here recently.

You decide to start searching the house.
