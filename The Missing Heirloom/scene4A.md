# :older_man: Scene 4A — Suspicion of Uncle Ray

You head downstairs and see Uncle Ray. He appears to be speaking to himself, but becomes quiet as you enter. He looks agitated. 

Ray looks at you, smiles and asks "“Did you find anything?” Something appears odd. Why would he be asking you that? 

You notice:

- Dust on Ray’s sleeves, as if he’s been rummaging through old boxes.
- A faint scratch on his wrist.
- His boots—muddy, despite the fact that he claimed he hadn’t left the house all day.

Something isn’t right.

Your first thought is, “He’s hiding something.”

Ray just intently stares at you.
