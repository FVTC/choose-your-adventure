#  :warning:Scene 5B — Hidden Room

The hidden door creaks open. You see a small room, lit by only a single lantern. Dust is floating all over in the air. You cautiously step inside. 

The room is nothing like the rest of the house and you have never seen it before.

The walls are full of shelves that hold old journals, photographs, and small locked boxes. A large trunk sits in the center of the room, however this one has metal hinges that are polished and unbroken. This was a special place for someone. It was definately loved and cared for. 

On top of the trunk lies a folded piece of paper with your name written in your grandmother’s handwriting.

Your throat tightens as you unfold it.

> “If you are here, it means the locket is no longer safe.  
> Trust yourself to know whether you can handle the truth.  
> It may be too much. ”

You thought to myself, “She knew I would find this.”

You kneel beside the trunk and lift the lid. Inside, wrapped in velvet, is a small metal box with a beautiful clasp. The same box your grandmother always kept near the locket.

But the clasp is loose — someone opened it recently.

This is it. The moment everything could come together or the moment everything could fall apart.
