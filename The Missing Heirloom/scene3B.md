# :unlock:Scene 3B — The Broken Chest

You go to the broken chest. There are splinters on the floor in front of it, the lock is hanging from it with a bent metal clasp and it is clear to you that someone used force to get this chest open. 

Inside, you find:

- Torn fabric that looks like it once wrapped something valuable.
- A scrap of paper with soome scribbled handwriting.
- A footprint in the dust, too large to have been yours.

You unfold the scrap of paper:

> “They’re getting close. I have to move it tonight.”

You're confused. Move what? The heirloom?

As you are studying the scene, you hear something downstairs. Is someone else inside the house?
