# The Missing Heirloom 

##  Are you ready for an adventure? :mag_right:

### 

> You inherited an old family home from your late grandmother, but on your first night inside, you discover that her most valuable heirloom has vanished — a silver locket. It was said that this locket may also contain a hidden message. You must explore areas of the house, question any potential suspects, uncover secrets, and choose who and what to trust.
>
> Your choices determine whether you recover the heirloom, expose a secret betrayal, or lose everything.
