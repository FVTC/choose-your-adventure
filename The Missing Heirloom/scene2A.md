# :key: Scene 2A — The Attic

The attic smells dusty and dry with hints of old wood. There are boxes stacked all over, cobwebs are in every corner, and the dim, single bulb above your head is flickering. This attic is a scary place and you now know why your grandmother warned you not to play up here as a kid.

You begin walking forward, clearing the cobwebs as you go. 

Like out of the movies, you hear a "thump" in the distance.

You freeze. What was that? A box falling? A mouse? A person? 

You look around the room and see two things: 

- A **dusty trunk** with your grandmother’s initials carved into the lid.
- A **broken wooden chest**, looking like it broke due to being forced open.

Both appear to be significant as they draw your attention.
