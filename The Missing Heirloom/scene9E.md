#  :eyeglasses: Scene 9E — Search the Outside 

 

You have your friends and family at the house- ready to search the outside. 

 

Teams divide, turning every stick and stone to search for the locket. You know that things fall out of pockets, things get hidden, and things could be anywhere out here.  

 

After a few hours of searching, you hear a scream. 

 

Running throught the trees, you see Cousin Betty standing next to Uncle Ray. 

 

She begins yelling: 

 

> "I walked past and saw him! I think he has the locket! I think he was putting the locket in his pocket! I think I saw it! It was shiny!" 

 

You say: 

> "Uncle Ray, is this true?" 

 

Uncle Ray proceeds to say: 

>"This is a big misunderstanding. I promise you that's not what happened!"
