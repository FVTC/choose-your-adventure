#   :bomb: Scene10E — No Trust with Uncle Ray 

 

You have always been leery of Uncle Ray. 

 

You knew you could not trust him before and you certainly aren't now. Cousin Betty says she saw him and you believe her.  

 

You are fuming when you ask Uncle Ray: 

 

> "Where is my Grandmother's locket?" 

 

Uncle Ray begins explaining: 

 

> "I knew you were determined to find your Grandmother's locket. I decided to start my own investigation. I wanted to help you find it and let it be a surprise. You have never trusted me and I wanted to regain your trust and do something nice and meaningful for you. I found it and hid it somewhere safe until I could give it to you. I promise this is the truth!" 

 

He sounds sincere, but sincere isn't proof. You have your doubts in Uncle Ray based on his past.

 

Ray lifts his chin. 

> "Search me if you have to. But with Betty as a witness, nothing between us will ever be the same."
