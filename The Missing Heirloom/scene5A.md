# :mega:Scene 5A — Confrontation

You corner Ray in the living room. He stands stiffly, looking at you with his hands clenched at his sides.

“Ray,” you say, “I know you’ve been sneaking around the house. The attic. The study. The kitchen. You’ve touched everything connected to the heirloom.”

Ray’s jaw tightens. “I was trying to protect it.”

“By hiding it?” you snap. 

Ray looks down, conflicted. “Your grandmother told me the locket contained something dangerous. Something that could tear the family apart. She trusted me to keep it safe.”

You step closer. “Where is it now?”

Ray hesitates — and that hesitation tells you everything. He’s lying. Or hiding something. Or both.

His voice cracks. “I didn’t mean for things to get out of control.”

The tension in the room is suffocating. Your fists are now clenched. Ray’s eyes dart toward the hallway, as if considering escape.
