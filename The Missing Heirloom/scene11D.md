# :mag: Scene 11D - Search Uncle Ray

You tell Uncle Ray to empty his pockets.

His jaw tightens. For a moment, you think he'll try to flee. However, he doesn't argue.

He turns them inside-out one at a time. A set of keys, a crumpled receipt, and a pocket knife. No locket.

You should feel relieved. Uncle Ray should feel vindicated. However, he just looks tired.

"I just wanted to do right by you.." he says deeply and quietly. He puts everything back in his pockets, walks out, and doesn't look back.

Wherever he hid the locket, he took the secret with him.
