# :key:Scene 3D — The Hidden Key

You kneel beside the desk and tug on the drawer. Locked.

You begin to inspect the underside. Your fingers feel a small metal object taped to the wood. It's a key!

Someone hid this here!

You unlock the drawer and find:

- A torn piece of cloth that looks like it once wrapped the locket.
- A small metal tool shaped like a hook.
- A note:  
  > “If someone finds this, the intruder is closer than you think."

  > "Some things must remain beyond secret doors.”

A sudden thump echoes from the hallway.
