# :ledger:Scene 3A — The Old Journal

Inside the dusty trunk, you find an old, leather journal. You recognize the handwriting immediately as your Grandmother’s looping script.

You open it carefully.

Most entries are ordinary and as you would expect: recipes, garden notes, birthday reminders. You keep reading though and near the end, the content changes. She writes about “family tensions,” “hidden truths,” and “the locket’s message.”

One page stands out:

> “If anything happens to me, the locket must be protected.  
> There is someone who cannot be trusted.”

Your stomach drops.

You flip to the last entry. It mentions a secret door somewhere in the house.

But another detail catches your eye: a name scribbled angrily in the margin. Someone she suspected.
