# :muscle:Scene 6A — Tackle the Intruder

You see the intruder laying on the ground and you take your chance.

You leap on top of them and yell "Stay put". 

We start wrestling in the wet grass and you lose your grip as the intruder stands up and attempts to flee again. 

Before the intruder flees, you are able to rip a piece of their jacket off and you have it tightly gripped in your hand.

You head back inside feeling a bit deflated, however you may have a new clue. Once inside you realize the cloth piece you are holding has an emblem: :honeybee:

This looks like it belongs to part of the Honey Bee Farm next door, the one that is owned by a known murderer.
