 Freedom Achieved

Against all odds, you fight your way out and escape into the wider world, free at last.  
The air feels lighter, the horizon endless you’ve made it.

Player Reflection:
You stand at the edge of the labyrinth, sunlight warming your face for the first time in what feels like ages.  
Every turn, every risk, every moment of doubt led you here. You learned to trust your instincts, to think before you act, and to keep moving even when the walls seemed to close in.  

Freedom wasn’t just escaping the maze it was realizing you had the courage to finish it.
