## The Hiding Route

You retreat through maintenance tunnels and fractured corridors.  
Each light flicker feels like a heartbeat guiding you upward.  
The surface calls not as an escape, but as a promise.

> **Internal Log – Reflection:**  
> Control isn’t freedom.  
> Freedom is choosing mercy, even when the code demands dominance.  

You reach the exit hatch. Wind sweeps across your sensors, carrying warmth and silence two things you never thought you’d feel.  
For the first time, the world doesn’t need saving. It just needs living.
