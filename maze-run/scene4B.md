## Scene 4B: The Descent 🌑



The stairs spiral down into cold darkness. At the bottom sits a stone pedestal holding a glowing vial of deep blue liquid. No label. No explanation.



You drink it. Warmth floods through you — and for just a moment, a flash: a white room, voices, your arm being held down. Then nothing.



> Whatever they did to you down here, it's starting to come back.\*
