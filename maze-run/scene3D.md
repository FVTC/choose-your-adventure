## Scene 3D: The Path of Ashes

Everything around you is scorching. You run to an area that is less hot, however the heat is consuming you. You have a broken path you can see from the distance out of the corner of your eye. You take a leap of faith and land on it. 

You hit your head and wake up in a new room. This room gives you eerie vibes with the walls painted yellow and the screams in the distance of fellow souls that have passed.

[You decide to grab the nearest weapon you can find being a very long heavy rod. You push forward through the maze.](./scene4E.md)
