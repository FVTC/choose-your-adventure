## Scene 1: Maze of Decisions

You see a mirror in the middle of the maze, you decide to walk up to it and discover that you are a younger man who seems to have lost his memory entirely as you cannot even remember your name. However you have a tattoo on your arm that says "Subject 1563" You decide you will call yourself 1563. You pick up a sharp stick off of the ground to decide to defend yourself if needed.

You step forward on the path to discover 3 paths you can possibly take.

The far left path has a symbol resembling water 🌊, the middle path has a symbol resembling fire 🔥, and the right path has a symbol resembling nature 🍃.
