## Scene 2C: Nature Path

🌲🌳🌿 You see a large crowd of towering trees that seem to be as old as the earth. Bushes crowd the pathways, ferns are unfurling in front of you as you try to push towards the area caution of any poison ivy. Suddenly the ground shifts and you see two trees push towards you. You jump up in terror and suddenly both of the trees bellow two words one by one. Each tree seems to also have a opening in the middle of the tree, big enough to just crawl in.

> The left tree exclaims "Meadow!" echoing throughout the area.

> The right tree exclaims "Forest!" also echoing throughout the area.
