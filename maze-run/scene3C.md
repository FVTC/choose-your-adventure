## Scene 3C: The Dragon's Den 🐉🔥



You step through the opening marked with the symbol of the dragon. 



The heat is immediate and overwhelming the walls of the tunnel are scorched black and the air shimmers with waves of intense warmth. Your fire-resistant boots are the only thing keeping you moving forward. 



the tunnel opens up into a massive underground chamber. The ceiling stretches so high above you it disappears into darkness. the ground is cracked and glowing orange beneath your feet like cooling lava. 



And then you see it. 



Coiled in the center of the chamber is an enormous dragon, easily the size of a building. Its scales are deep obsidian black and veins are molten orange glowing. One massive eye slowly opens and fixes directly on you.



It does not attack. 



Instead it speak... its voice a deep rumble that you feel in your chest more than you hear with your ears. 



> "Subject 1563. You finally found your way back. I have been waiting for a very long time." 



Your blood runs cold. It knows your name. Or rather.. your number. 



The dragon slowly uncurls and you notice three things around the chamber. A glowing door carved into the far wall. A chest sitting at the dragon's feet, unlocked. And a narrow tunnel exit to your right that the dragon does not seem to be watching. 



Do you trust the dragon ? Or do you run?
