## Opening Crawl

## Trapped in a maze

🫩 You wake up from a desolate slumber. Unaware of anything around you. Your memory is fogged. You decide to scan around the area, you discover that the area is littered with tall, dense bushes that block your path entirely. However you can see a small opening in one of the bushes. You proceed to crawl through the small space, discovering a vast mass of bushes blocking more of your path. 

🚪 You stand back up and there shows to be three entrances you could possibly take.
