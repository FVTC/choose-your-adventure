\## Scene 3A: The Ocean Maze 🌊🏝️



The salty air hits your face as you step through the entrance marked "Ocean." The walls of the maze are made entirely of coral and ancient sea rock, dripping with moisture. Strange bioluminescent algae lines the walls, casting an eerie blue glow on everything around you. 



You grip your sharp stick tightly and move forward. The sound of the crashing waves echoes all around you, though you cannot see the source. 



> "Subject 1563..." you whisper to yourself glancing at the tattoo on your arm. Does this place have something to do with who I am?



Suddenly the path splits to your left, you spot a shipwreck half buried in the sandy maze floor. Its old wooden hull creaking and groaning as if alive. To your right, a stone staircase descends sharply downward into the darkness. The sound of dripping water grows louder the closer you step toward it. Carved into the wall above the stairs is a single word. "DEEPER." 



Straight ahead, a massive sea turtle sits motionless in the middle of the path, blocking your way entirely. its ancient eyes blink slowly at you as if it has been waiting. On its shell is a symbol... the same kind of symbol you have seen before, but cannot place where.
