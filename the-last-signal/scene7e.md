# Ending: Everyone Comes Home

Captain Reyes recovered Alex, Morgan, Dr. Vale, and Pip. Below them, Pelican's blue lights faded.

Without further repairs, the station could not survive.

> "I'm sorry," Alex told Dr. Vale as Reyes wrapped a blanket around her.

Dr. Vale looked toward the horizon.

> "I thought I would never see the sky again."

They watched the sunrise together.
