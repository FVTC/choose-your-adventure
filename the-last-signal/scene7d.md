# Waiting in the Dark

The observation room door stopped the water. The ceiling hatch would not open.

> "We're trapped," Alex told Captain Reyes.

> "Stay away from the window. Divers are coming."

Hours passed beneath the emergency lights. Finally, someone knocked from the other side.

The divers rescued everyone, but Pelican collapsed before another team could reach its research.

That night, Alex kept thinking about the staircase they had passed.
