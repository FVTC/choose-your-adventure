# The Roots Stop

With the generator running, cold water flowed steadily through the gardens. The roots loosened around the beams.

Everyone waited for another crack.

None came.

Captain Reyes asked whether they wanted to leave or wait for her repair team. Morgan looked around the brightened room.

> "For once, waiting might help."
