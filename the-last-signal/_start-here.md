
## THE LAST SIGNAL

# The Message

The radio had been silent all evening. Then a voice broke through the static.

> “Someone is still down here. Please hurry.”

Alex looked up. Across the cabin, Morgan stopped turning their chair. The signal came from Pelican, an underwater research station that had been abandoned for three years. Captain Reyes listened to the message twice before handing Alex the submarine keys.

> “Take Morgan, and keep your radio on.”
