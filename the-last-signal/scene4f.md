# The Gardens

Thousands of blue leaves moved beyond the glass. NORA explained that cooling would slow their growth. An emergency release could detach the entire garden platform.

> "The station would lose its oxygen supply," NORA warned.

Morgan rested a hand on the release switch's safety cover.

> "Then we would have to leave."
